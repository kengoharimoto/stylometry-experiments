# The closing-parvans claim for the article: formulation, objections, answers

2026-08-14. Kengo's intended claim: the closing parvans' epic-pole position
is a *finding* of the stylistic analyses, grounding the theory that they may
retain the earliest composed text of the Mahābhārata, while the other
parvans — 12 and 13 aside — went through heavy editing before the archetype
behind the Critical Edition was fixed. This note records the agreed
formulation, its evidential supports, and the anticipated objections with
their answers (worked out 2026-08-14; see
`2026-08-14_e1_apparatus_experiment.md` incl. correction + addendum, and
`2026-07-15_closing_parvans_length_diagnostics.md` incl. correction).

## Formulation to use

1. **Finding (block level):** the closing parvans *as a group* sit at the
   epic pole. The merged 15+16+17+18 block (~17k words) stays at the pole,
   so this is not a length artifact; the genre explanation is refuted by
   measurement; the axis itself is robust to MFW, distance measure, and
   reuse removal. Do NOT state individual ranks for 16/17/18 — sub-3k units
   are uncertainty regions (D1).
2. **Theory (graded, not binary):** redactional overlay varies continuously
   across the parvans and the drift axis tracks it. The closing parvans and
   the late battle books carry the least overlay (Śalya pct 3, Sauptika 7,
   Karṇa 6, closing parvans 0–5, apparatus fractions 8–22%); the frame and
   didactic-adjacent books sit mid (Ādi 11 @64% apparatus, Sabhā 15,
   Udyoga 17, Āśvamedhika 18 @66%); 12–13 are the limiting case (28 @24%,
   32 @84%). Supporting gradient: Spearman ρ(position, apparatus fraction)
   = 0.40 over all 18 parvans. Outlier understood: Virāṭa (141% apparatus,
   pct 10) — recension divergence, not overlay.
3. **Wording:** "heavily edited before the archetype behind the Critical
   Edition was fixed" — not "before being codified as the CE Mahābhārata"
   (the CE recovers the archetype; the editing predates the archetype).
4. Converging external support: Lüders (Ghata-Jātaka 454, independent
   Yādava-destruction cycle), Jacobi (Jaina Dvārakā legend), i.e. philology
   independently posits early narrative material exactly where the axis
   puts it. Brockington's "late epilogue" concerns their status as *books*
   and is compatible.

## Anticipated objections and answers

**O1. "The constituted text of 16–18 rests on a narrow manuscript base"**
(Bronkhorst 2011: no Śāradā mss for MBh 13, 16, 17, 18).
*Answer:* E1 shows the CE's exclusions are uniformly *later*-styled
(apparatus strata at pct 16–62 vs constituted 0–5, CIs in
`e1_apparatus_{W1,C3}_500.tsv`) — the constitution was not arbitrarily
archaizing. And the finding survives augmentation: MBh 15–17 *with* their
apparatus restored still sit at pct 1–6. The narrow base could shift
readings, not the stylistic register of the whole.

**O2. "Language age is not book age."**
*Answer:* conceded and absorbed by the claim's wording ("retain the
earliest composed text"). The stylometry cannot distinguish early
composition from early fixation plus faithful transmission; the claim is
about the age of the *text*. Its status as late-attached *books* is
untouched — this reconciles with, rather than contradicts, the received
epilogue verdict.

**O3. "Why believe the axis measures age here at all?"**
*Answer:* the validation stack: (a) the axis reproduces the received
gross chronology and is stable across MFW 250–12000 / 80–800, all
standardized distance measures, and parallels removal; (b) it correctly
stratifies layers of *known* relative date — every CE-excluded accretion
in every parvan tested lands later than its host text (E1), and the PPL
stratum inside Vāyu/Brahmāṇḍa lands with the constituted PPL; (c) the
genre confounds actually tested (vaṃśa lists; expository-filler density)
failed to explain the placements. An instrument that passes those checks
earns the age reading for the closing parvans.

**O4. "The closing parvans are tiny; the result is noise."**
*Answer:* individually yes — which is why the claim is made at block
level. The merged block is ~17k words, comfortably above the D1 stability
floor, and stays at the pole under both feature systems.

**O5. "The apparatus-fraction gradient is a weak correlation (0.40) with a
glaring outlier."**
*Answer:* the fraction is a proxy for *continuing* expansion propensity,
offered as convergent (not load-bearing) support; the outlier (Virāṭa) is
diagnostic of the proxy's known limit (recension divergence vs overlay),
and removing it strengthens the trend. The load-bearing evidence is
D4 + E1 + the refuted confounds.
