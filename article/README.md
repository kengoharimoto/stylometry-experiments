## Multi-venue strategy (decided 2026-08-17: explore all three)

Drafting variants in parallel is exploration, not submission — the same
manuscript cannot be under review at two journals. Endgames:

1. **Pick one** after seeing which the argument fits best.
2. **Companion pair**: DH methods paper (variant B) + Indological
   findings paper (variant A), cross-citing, with non-overlapping
   claims-of-novelty. The material supports this; variant C would then
   be dropped or become a short perspective piece.
