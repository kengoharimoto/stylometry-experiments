## 1. Abschnitt. Sarga und Pratisarga | Adhy. 2

manur uvāca:
utpattiṃ pralayaṃ caiva vaṃśān manvantarāṇi ca |
vaṃśyānucaritaṃ caiva bhuvanasya ca vistaraṃ || 22 ||
dānadharmavidhiṃ caiva śrāddhakaḷpaṃ ca śāsṭaṃ |
varṇāśramavibhāgaṃ ca tatheṣṭāpūrtasamjñitaṃ || 23 ||
devatānāṃ pratiṣṭhādi yac cānyad vidyate bhuvi, |
tat sarvaṃ vistareṇa tvaṃ dharmaṃ vyākhyaṭum arbasī! || 24 ||
matsya uvāca:
mahāpralayakālānta etad āsīt tamomayaṃ |
prasuptam iva cātarkyam aprajñātam alakṣaṇaṃ, || 25 ||
ayijñeyam avijñātaṃ jagat sthāsnu cariṣṇu ca. |
tataḥ svayambhūr avyaktaḥ prabhavaḥ puṇyakarmaṇāṃ || 26 ||
vyañjayam etad akhilaṃ prādurāsīt tamonudaḥ. |
yo 'tindriyaḥ paro vyaktād aṇur jyāyān sanātanaḥ |
nārāyaṇa iti khyātaḥ, sa ekaḥ svayam udbabhau; || 27 ||
yaḥ śarīrād abhidhyāya viśrṛṣur vividhaṃ jagat |
apa eva sasariṣādau, tāsu vīryam avāsṛjat. || 28 ||
tad evaṇḍaṃ samabhaveḍ dhemarūpyamayaṃ mahat |
samvatsarasahasreṇa sūryāyutasamaprabhaṃ. || 29 ||
praviśyāntar mahātejāḥ svayam evātmasambhavaḥ |
prabhāvād api tadvyāptyā viṣṇutvam agamat punaḥ. || 30 ||
tadantar bhagavān eṣa sūryaḥ samabhaveḍ purā, |
ādityaś cādibhūtatvād, brahmā brahma paṭhan abhūt. || 31 ||
divaṃ bhūmiṃ samakarot tad aṇḍaśakaladvayaṃ, |
sa cākarot diśaḥ sarvā madhye vyoma ca śāsṭaṃ. || 32 ||
jarāyur merumukhyāś ca, śailās tasyābhavaṃs tadā, |
yad ulbaṃ, tad abhūn meghas(!) taḍitsamghātaṃaṇḍalaṃ. || 33 ||
nadyo 'ṇḍanāḍyaḥ sambhūtāḥ pitaro manavas tathā, |
sapta ye 'mī samudrāś ca, te 'pi cāntarjalodbhavaḥ |
lavanekṣusurādyāś ca nānāratnasamanvitāḥ. || 34 ||
sa viśrṛṣur abhūḍ devaḥ prajāpatir ariṃdama, |
tattejasaś ca tatraiṣa mārtaṇḍaḥ samajāyata. || 35 ||
mṛte 'ṇḍe jāyate yasmān, mārtaṇḍas tena samsnṛtaḥ. |
rajogunaṃayaṃ yat. tad rūpaṃ tasya mahātmanaḥ. |
caturmukhaḥ sa bhagavān abhūl lokapitāmahaḥ, || 36 ||
yena sṛṣṭaṃ jagat sarvaṃ sadevāsuramānuṣaṃ, |
tam avehī rajorūpaṃ; mahat sattvam udāhṛtaṃ. || 36 ||

## 1. Abschnitt. Sarga und Pratisarga | Adhy. 3

manur uvāca:
caturmukhatvam agamat kasmāl lokapitāmahaḥ, |
kathaṃ tu lokāu asṛjad brahmā brahmavidāṃ varaḥ? || 1 ||
matsya uvāca:
tapaś cakāra prathamam amarānāṃ pitāmahaḥ, |
āvirbhūtās tato vedāḥ sāṅgopāṅgapadakramāḥ. || 2 ||
purānaṃ sarvaśāstrānāṃ prathamaṃ brahmaṇā sūṛtaṃ |
nityaṃ śabdamayaṃ puṇyaṃ śatakoṭipravistaraṃ. || 3 ||
anantaraṃ ca vaktrebhyo vedās tasya vinihṛtāḥ |
mīmāṃsānyāyavidyās ca pramāṇāṣṭakasamyutāḥ. || 4 ||
vedābhyāsaratasyāsyā prajākāmasya mānasāḥ |
manasāḥ pūrvasṛṣṭā vai jāta yat, tena mānasāḥ. || 5 ||
maricir abhavat pūrvaṃ, tato 'trir bhagavān ṛṣiḥ, |
aṅgirās cābhavat paścāt, pulastyas tadanantaraṃ, || 6 ||
tataḥ pulahanāmā vai, tataḥ kratur ajāyata, |
pracetās ca tataḥ putro, vasiṣṭhas cābhavat punaḥ, || 7 ||
putro bhṛgur abhūt tadvan, nārado 'py acirād abhūt: |
daśemān mānasān brahmā munīn putrān ajījanat. || 8 ||
śārīrān atha vakṣyāmi māṭṛhinān prajāpateḥ: |
aṅguṣṭhād dakṣiṇād dakṣaḥ prajāpatir ajāyata, || 9 ||
dharmaḥ stanāntād abhavad, dhṛdayād kusumāyudhaḥ, |
bhrūmadhyād abhavat krodho, lobhas cādharasambhavaḥ, |
buddher mohaḥ samabhavad, ahaṃkārad abhūn madaḥ, |
pramodaś cābhavat kaṇṭhān, mṛtyur locanato nṛpa, |
bharataḥ karamadhyāt tu brahma-sūnur abhūt tataḥ. || 11 ||
ete nava sutā rājan kanyā ca daśamī punaḥ |
aṅgajā iti vikhyātā daśamī brahmaṇaḥ sutā. || 12 ||
manur uvāca:
buddher mohaḥ samabhavad iti yat parikīrtitaṃ, |
ahaṃkāraḥ smṛtaḥ krodho, buddhir nāma kim ucyate? || 13 ||
matsya uvāca:
sattvaṃ rajas tamaś caiva guṇatrayam udāhṛtaṃ, |
sāmyāvasthitir eteṣāṃ prakṛtiḥ parikīrtitā. || 14 ||
kecit pradhānam ity āhur, avyaktam apare jaguḥ, |
etad eva prajāsṛṣṭiṃ karoti vikaroti ca. || 15 ||
guṇebhyaḥ kṣobhamāṇebhyas trayo devā vijajñire, |
ekā mūrtis, trayo bhāgā, brahma-viṣṇu-maheśvarāḥ. || 16 ||
savikārāt pradhānāt tu mahattattvaṃ prajāyate, |
mahān iti yataḥ khyātir lokānāṃ jāyate sadā. || 17 ||
ahaṃkāraś ca mahato jāyate mānavardhanaḥ, |
indriyaṇi tataḥ pañca vakṣye buddhivaśāni tu, |
prādurbhavanti cānyāni tathā karmavaśāni tu: || 18 ||
śrotraṃ tvak cakṣuṣi jihvā nāsikā ca yathākramaṃ |
pāyūpasthaṃ hastapādaṃ vāk cetindriyasamgrahaḥ. || 19 ||
śabdaḥ sparśaś ca rūpaṃ ca raso gandhaś ca pañcamaḥ |
utsargānandanādānagatyālāpāś ca tatkriyāḥ. || 20 ||
mana ekādaśaṃ teṣāṃ karmabuddhiguṇānvitaṃ. |
indriyāyavavāḥ sūksmās tasya mūrṭiṃ manīṣiṇaḥ || 21 |
śrayanti yasmāt, tanmātrāḥ śarīraṃ tena samsmṛtaṃ. |
śarīrayogāj jivo 'pi śarīri gadyate budhaiḥ. || 22 ||
manaḥ sṛṣṭiṃ vikurute codyamānaṃ sisṛksayā. |
ākāśaṃ śabdatanmātrād abhūc chabdaguṇātmakaṃ. || 23 ||
ākāśavikṛter vāyuḥ śabdasparśaguṇo 'bhavat. |
vāyoś ca sparśatanmātrāt tejas cāvīrabhūt tataḥ, || 24 ||
triguṇaṃ tadvikāreṇa tac chabdasparśarūpavat. |
tejovikārād abhavad vāri rājaṃś cāturguṇaṃ, || 25 ||
rasatanmātrasambhūtaṃ, prāyo rasaguṇātmakaṃ. |
bhūmis tu gandhatanmātrād abhūt pañcaguṇānvitā, || 26 ||
prāyo gandhaguṇā sā tu, buddhir eṣā garīyasī. |
ebhiḥ samṛpāditaṃ bhuñkte puruṣaḥ pañcaviṃśakaḥ, || 27 ||
īśvarecchāvaśaḥ so 'pi, jīvātmā kathyate budhaiḥ. |
evaṃ ṣaḍviṃśakaṃ proktaṃ śarīram iha mānavaiḥ, || 28 ||
samkhyaṃ samkhyātmakatvāc ca kapilādibhir ucyate. |
etat tattvātmakaṃ kṛtvā jagad vedhā ajījanat, || 29 ||
sāvitriṃ lokasṛṣṭyarthaṃ hṛdi kṛtvā samāsthitaḥ. |
tataḥ samjapatas tasya bhittvā deham akalmaṣaṃ || 30 ||
strīrūpam ardhāṃ akarod, ardhāṃ puruṣarūpavat. |
śatarūpā ca sā khyātā sāvitri ca nigadyate, || 31 ||
sarasvaty atha gāyatrī brahmāṇī ca paraṃtapa. |
tataḥ svadehasambhūtām ātmajām ity akalpayat. || 32 ||
dṛṣṭvā tāṃ vyathitas tāvat kāmaḥbāṇārdito vibhuḥ: |
aho rūpam, aho rūpam iti cāha prajāpatiḥ. || 33 ||
tato vasiṣṭhapramukhā bhaginīm iti cukruṣuḥ; |
brahmā na kiṃcid dadṛṣe tanmukhālokanād ṛte. || 34 ||
aho rūpam aho rūpam iti prāha punaḥ punaḥ, |
tataḥ praṇāmanamrāṃ tāṃ punar evābhyalokayat. || 35 ||
atha pradakṣiṇaṃ cakre sā pitur varavarṇinī |
putrebhyo lajjitasyāsyā tadrūpālokanecchayā. || 36 ||
āvīrbhūtaṃ tato vaktraṃ dakṣiṇaṃ pāṇḍuganḍavat, |
vismayasphuradoṣṭhaṃ ca pāscātyam udagāt tataḥ. || 37 ||
caturtham abhavat paścād vāmaṃ kāmaśarāturaṃ. |
tato 'nyad abhavat tasya kāmāturāyā tathā || 38 ||
utpatantyās tadākāśa ālokanakutūhalāt. |
sṛṣṭyarthaṃ yat kṛtaṃ tena tapaḥ paramadāruṇaṃ, || 39 ||
tat sarvaṃ nāśām agamat svasutopagamecchayā. |
tenordhvaṃ vaktram abhavat pañcamaṃ tasya dhīmataḥ; |
āvīrbhavajjaṭābhiṣ ca tadvaktraṃ cāvṛṇot prabhuḥ. || 40 ||
tatas tān abravīd brahmā pūtrān ātmasamudbhavān: |
prajāḥ sṛjadhvam abhitaḥ sadevāsuramānuṣiḥ! || 41 ||
evam uktās tataḥ sarve sasṛjur vividhāḥ prajāḥ. |
gatesu teṣu sṛṣṭyarthaṃ praṇāmāvanatām imāṃ || 42 ||
upayeme sa viśvātmā śatarūpām aninditāṃ |
sa babhūva tayā sārḍham atikāmāuro vibhuḥ, |
salajjāṃ cakame devaḥ kamalodaramandire || 43 ||
yāvad abdaśataṃ divyaṃ yathānyaḥ prākṛto janaḥ. |
tataḥ kālena mahatā tasyāḥ putro 'bhavan manuḥ || 44 ||
svāyaṃbhuva iti khyātaḥ, sa virād iti naḥ śrutaṃ, |
tadrūpaguṇasāmānyād adhipuruṣa ucyate, || 45 ||
vairājā yatra te jāta bahavaḥ saṃśitavratāḥ, |
svāyaṃbhuva mahābhāgāḥ sapta sapta tathā pare. || 46 ||
svārociṣādyāḥ sarve te brahmatulyasvarūpiṇaḥ, |
auttamipramukhās tadvad, yeṣāṃ tvaṃ saptamo 'dhunā. || 47 ||

## 1. Abschnitt. Sarga und Pratisarga | Adhy. 4

manur uvāca:
aho kaṣṭataraṃ caitad aṅgajāgamanaṃ vibho! |
kathaṃ na doṣam agamat karmaṇānena padmabhūḥ, || 1 ||
parasparaṃ ca saṃbandhaḥ sagotrānām abhūt kathaṃ |
vaivāhikas tat sutānāṃ, chindhi me saṃśayaṃ vibho! || 2 ||
matsya uvāca:
divyeyam ādisṛṣṭis tu rajogūṇasamudbhavā |
atīndriyendriyā tadvad atīndriyaśarīrikā || 3 ||
divyatejomayī bhūpa divyajñānasamudbhavā, |
na martyair abhitaḥ śakyā vaktuṃ vai māṃsacakṣubhiḥ || 4 ||
yathā bhujamgāḥ sarpānām ākāśaṃ viśvapakṣiṇāṃ |
vidanti mārgaṃ, divyānāṃ divyā eva na mānavāḥ. || 5 ||
kāryākārye na devānāṃ śubhāśubhaphalaprade |
yasmāt, tasmān na rājendra tadvicāro nṛṇāṃ śubhaḥ. || 6 ||
anyac ca, sarvedānām adhiṣṭhātā caturmukhaḥ, |
gāyatrī brahmanas tadvad aṅgabhūtā nigadyate. || 7 ||
amūrtaṃ mūrtimad vāpi mithunaṃ tat pracakṣate. |
viriūcir yatra bhāgavāṃs, tatra devī sarasvatī; |
bhārātī yatra yatraiva, tatra tatra prajāpatiḥ. || 8 ||
yathātapo na rahitās chāyayā dṛṣyate kvacit, |
gāyatrī brahmanaḥ pārśvaṃ tathaiva na vimuñcati. || 9 ||
vedarāśiḥ smṛto brahmā, sāvitrī tadadhiṣṭhitā, |
tasmān na kaścid doṣaḥ syāt sāvitrigamane vibhoḥ. || 10 ||
tathāpi lajjāvanataḥ prajāpatir abhūt purā, |
svasutopagamād brahmā śaśapa kusumāyudhaṃ. || 11 ||
yasmān mamābhībhavatā manaḥ saṃkṣobhitaṃ śaraiḥ, |
tasmāt tvaddeham acirād rudro bhasmīkariṣyati. || 12 ||
tataḥ prasādayāmāsa kāmadevaś caturmukham: |
na māṃ akāraṇe śaptuṃ tvam ihārhasi mānada! || 13 ||
aham evamvidhaḥ sṛṣṭas tvayaiva caturānana |
indriyakṣobhajanakaḥ sarveṣām eva dehināṃ. || 14 ||
stripuṃsor avicāreṇa mayā sarvatra sarvadā |
kṣobhyaṃ manaḥ prayatnena, tvayaivoktaṃ purā vibho. || 15 ||
tasmād anaparādho 'haṃ tvayā śaptas tathā vibho; |
kuru prasādaṃ bhagavan svaśarīrāptaye punaḥ! || 16 ||
brahmovāca:
vaivasvate 'ntare prāpte yādavānvayasambhavaḥ |
rāmo nāma yadā martyo matsattvabalam āśritaḥ || 17 ||
avatiryasuradhvaṃsi dvarakam adhivatsyati, |
tadbhratus tatsamasya tvaṃ tada putratvaṃ cśyasi. || 18 ||
evaṃ śariram asadya bhuktva bhogan aśeśataḥ |
tato bharatavaṃśante bhutva vatsanṛpatmaḥ || 19 ||
vidyadharadhipatyaṃ ca yavad abhutasamplavaṃ |
sukhani dharmataḥ prapya matsamipaṃ gamiśyasi. || 20 ||
evaṃ śapaprasadabhyam upetaḥ kusumayudhaḥ |
śokapramodabhiyuto jagama sa yathagataṃ. || 21 ||
manur uvaca:
ko 'sau yadur iti prokto yadvaṃśe kamasaṃbhavaḥ, |
kathaṃ ca dagdho rudreṇa kimarthaṃ kusumayudhaḥ, || 22 ||
bharatasyanvaye kasya ka ca sṛstiḥ purabhavat? |
etat sarvaṃ samacaksva mulataḥ, samśayo hi me! || 23 ||
matsya uvaca:
ya sa dehardbasamṛbhuta gayatri brahmavadini, |
janani ya manor devi śatarupa śatendriya, || 24 ||
ratir manas tapo buddhir mahan dik samṛbhramas tatha, |
tataḥ sa śatarupayaṃ saptapatyany ajijanat. || 25 ||
ye maricyadayaḥ putra mānasas tasya dhimataḥ, |
teśam ayam abhul lokaḥ sarvajñanatmakaḥ pura. || 26 ||
tato 'sṛjad vamadevaṃ triśulavaradhariṇaṃ |
sanatkumaraṃ ca vibhuṃ purveśam api purvajaṃ. || 27 ||
vamadevas tu bhagavan asṛjan mukhato dvijan, |
rājanyan asṛjad bahvor, vicchudran ūrupadayoḥ. || 28 ||
vidyuto 'śanimeghaṃś ca rohitendradhanuṃśi ca |
chandaṃśi ca sasarjadau parjanyaṃ ca tataḥ paraṃ. || 29 ||
tataḥ sadhyagaṇan īśas trinetran asṛjat punaḥ |
koṭiś ca caturaśitiṃ jaramaraṇavarjitaḥ. || 30 ||
vamo 'sṛjad aḥmartyaṃś tan brahmaṇa vinivaritaḥ: |
naivamvidha bhavet sṛstir jaramaraṇavarjita! || 31 ||
śubhasubhatmika ya tu, saiva sṛstiḥ praśasyate. |
evaṃ stithaḥ sa tenadau sṛśteḥ sthanur ato 'bhavat || 32 ||

## 3. Abschnitt. Manvantara | A.  (Kū. 51.1—36; Vi. III. 1.5—47.)

atitānāgatānīha yāni manvantarāṇi vai |
tāny ahaṃ bhavate samyak kathayāmi yathākrāmam: || 1 ||
svāyambhuvo manuḥ pūrvo, manuḥ svārociṣas tataḥ |
auttamis tamasas caiva raivatas cāksuṣas tathā, || 2 ||
ṣaḍ ete manavo 'titāḥ, sāmprataṃ tu raveḥ sutāḥ |
vaivasvato 'yaṃ, yasyai'tat saptamaṃ vartate 'ntaraṃ. || 3 ||
svāyambhuvaṃ tu kathitaṃ kalpādāv antaraṃ mayā |
| devās tatharṣayaś caiva yathāvat kathitā mayā. |
ata ūrdhvaṃ pravakṣyāmi manuḥ svārociṣasya tu || 4 ||
| manvantarādhipān samyag devarṣiṃs tatsutāṃs tathā: |
pārāvatās ca tuṣitā devāḥ svārociṣe 'ntare. || 5 ||
vipaṣcin nāma devendro babhūvāsuramardanāḥ. |
ūrjas tambhas tathā prāṇo dattolir ṛṣabhas tathā || 6 ||
niṣvaras c-orvarivāṃś ca tatra saptarṣayo 'bhavan, |
caitra-kiṃpuruṣādyāś ca sutāḥ svārociṣasya tu. || 7 ||
dvitiyam etat kathitam antaraṃ, śṛṇu c-ottamaṃ! |
tṛṭiye tv antare caiva auttamir nāma vai manuḥ, || 8 ||
suśāntiṣ tatra devendro babhūvāmitrakarṣaṇaḥ, |
sudhāmanas tathā satyāḥ śivās cāsan pratardanāḥ || 9 ||
vaśavartinās ca pañcaite gaṇā dvādaśakāḥ smṛtāḥ. |
rajo gātro 'ṛdhabāhuś ca | vaśiṣṭhatanayās tatra
savanaś c-ānaghas tathā || 10 || | sapta saptarṣayo 'bhavan || 10 ||
sutapāḥ śakra ity ete | ajāḥ paraśu-divyādyās
sapta saptarṣayo 'bhavan. | | tasy-auttamimanoḥ sutāḥ. |
tāmasasyāntare devāḥ surūpā harayas tathā || 11 ||
satyās ca sudhiyas caiva saptaviṃśatikā gaṇāḥ |
śivir indras tathā cāsic chatayajñopalakṣaṇaḥ, |
babhūva śaikare bhakto | saptarṣayaś ca ye teṣāṃ
mahādevārcane rataḥ. || 12 || | tatra nāmāni me śṛṇu: || 12 ||
jyotirdhāmā pṛthuḥ kāvyaś caitro 'gnir vanakas tathā |
pivarās carṣayo hy ete sapta tatrāpi cāntare. || 13 ||
| tataḥ khyātiḥ śatahayo jānujaṅghādayas tathā |
| putrās tu tāmasasyāsan rājānaḥ sumahābalāḥ. || 13 ||
pañcame cāpi maitreya raivato nāma nāmataḥ |
manur vibhuś ca tatrendro, devāṃś caivāntare śṛṇu: || 14 ||
amitābh-ābhūtarajo-vaikuṇṭhāḥ sa-sumedhasaḥ |
ete devagaṇās tatra caturdaśa caturdaśa. || 15 ||
hiraṇyaromā vedaśṛir ūrdhvabāhus tathaiva ca |
vedabāhuḥ sudhāmā ca parjanyaś ca mahāmuniḥ, |
ete saptarṣayo vipra tatrāsan raivate 'ntare. || 16 ||
| balabandhus tu saṃbhavyaḥ satyakādyās ca tatsutāḥ |
| narendrāḥ sumahāviryā babhūvur munisattama. || 16 ||
svārociṣas c-auttamī ca tāmaso raivatas tathā |
priyavratānvayā hy ete catvāro manavas tathā. || 17 ||
| viṣṇum ārādhya tapasā sa rājarṣiḥ priyavratāḥ |
manvantarādhipān etān labdhavān ātmavamaṣajān. || 17 ||
ṣaṣṭhe manvantare cāsic cākṣuṣākhyas tathā manuḥ |
manojavas tathaivendro, devān api nibodha me: || 18 ||
ādyāḥ prasūtā bhavyāś ca pṛṭhugāś ca divaukasaḥ |
mahānubhāvā lekhāś ca, pañcaite 'py aṣṭakā gaṇāḥ. || 19 ||
sumedhā virajāś caiva haviṣmān uttamo madhuḥ |
atināmā sahiṣṇuś ca saptāsan ṛṣayaḥ śubhāḥ. || 20 ||
| ūruḥ pūruḥ śatadyumnapramukhāḥ sumahābalāḥ |
cākṣuṣasya manoḥ putrāḥ pṛṭhivīpatayo 'bhavan. || 20 ||
vivasvataḥ suto vipra śrāddhadevo mahādyutiḥ |
manuḥ sa vartate dhimān sāmprataṃ saptame 'ntare, || 21 ||

## 3. Abschnitt. Manvantara | B.  (Vi. III. 2.1—60).
