## 2. Abschnitt. Vaṃśa | 1. Kapitel  (Mt. 4.33—55).

svāyambhuvo manur dhīmāms tapas taptvā suduścaraṃ |
patnīm evāpa rūpādhyām anantāṃ nāma nāmataḥ. || 33 ||
priyavrat-ottānapādau manus tasyām ajījanat. |
dharmasya kanyā caturā sūnṛtā nāma bhāminī || 34 ||
uttānapādāt tanayān prāpa mantharagāminī. |
apasyatim apasyantaṃ kirtimantaṃ dhruvaṃ tathā || 35 ||
uttānapādo 'janayat sūnṛtāyāṃ prajāpatiḥ. |
dhruvo varṣasahasrāṇi trīṇi kṛtvā tapaḥ purā || 36 ||
divyam āpa tataḥ sthānam acalaṃ brahmano varāt, |
tam eva purataḥ kṛtvā dhruvaṃ saptarṣayaḥ sthitāḥ. || 37 ||
dhanyā nāma manoḥ kanyā dhruvāc chiṣṭam ajījanat, |
agnikanyā tu succhāyā śiṣṭat sā suṣuve sutān: || 38 ||
kṛpaṃ ripumjayaṃ vṛttaṃ vṛkaṃ ca vṛkatejasaṃ. |
cakṣuṣaṃ brahmadauhitṛyāṃ vīriṇyāṃ sa ripumjayaḥ, || 39 ||
viraṇasyātmajāyāṃ tu cakṣur manum ajījanat. |
manur vai rājakanyāyāṃ naḍvalāyāṃ sa cakṣuṣaḥ || 40 ||
janayāmāsa tanayān daśa śūrān akalmaṣān: |
ūruḥ pūruḥ śatadyumnas tapasvī satyavāk kaviḥ || 41 ||
agniṣṭud atirātrās ca sudyumnaś cāparājitaḥ |
abhimanyus tu daśamo naḍvalāyām ajāyata. || 42 ||
ūror ajanayat putrān ṣadāgneyi tu suprabhān: |
aṅgaṃ sumanasaṃ khyātiṃ kratum aṅgirasaṃ gayaṃ. || 43 ||
pitṛkanyā sunīthā tu venam aṅgād ajījanat. |
venam anyāyinaṃ viprā mamanthus, tatkarād abhūt |
pṛthur nāma mahātejāḥ, sa putrau dvāv ajījanat. || 44 ||
antardhānas tu māricaṃ śikhaṇḍinyām ajījanat. |
havirdhānāt ṣadāgneyi dhiṣaṇājanayat sutān: |
prācinabarhiṣaṃ sāṅgaṃ yamaṃ śukraṃ balaṃ śubhaṃ. || 45 ||
prācinabarhir bhagavān mahān āsīt prajāpatiḥ, |
havirdhānāt prajās tena bahavaḥ saṃpravartitāḥ. || 46 ||
savarnāyāṃ tu sāmudṛyāṃ daśādhatta sutān prabhuḥ, |
sarve pracetaso nāma dhanurvedasya pāragāḥ. || 47 ||
tattaporakṣitā vṛksā babhur loke samantataḥ, |
devādeśāc ca tān agnir adahad ravinandana. || 48 ||
somakanyābhavat patnī mārīṣā nāma viśrutā, |
tebhyas tu dakṣam ekaṃ sā putram agṛyam ajījanat. || 49 ||
dakṣād anantaraṃ vṛkṣān auṣadhāni ca sarvaśaḥ |
ajījanat somakanyā nadīṃ candravatīṃ tathā. || 50 ||
somamśasya ca tasyāpi dakṣasyāśītikotayaḥ, |
vakṣye tāsaṃ tu vistāraṃ, loke yaḥ supratisthitaḥ: || 51 ||
dvipadaś cābhavan kecit, kecid bahupadā narāḥ, |
valimukhāḥ śaṅkukarṇāḥ karṇaprāvaranās tathā, || 52 ||
aśvaṛkṣamukhāḥ kecit, kecid siṃhānanās tathā, |
śvasūkaramukhāḥ kecit, kecid uṣṭramukhās tathā. || 53 ||
janayāmāsa dharmātmā mleccān sarvān anekaśaḥ, |
sa sṛṣtvā manasā dakṣaḥ striyaḥ paścād ajījanat. || 54 ||
dadau sa daśa dharmāya, kaśyapāya trayodaśa,
saptaviṃśatiṃ somāya dadau nakṣatrasamjñitāḥ; |
devāsuramanuṣyādi tābhyāḥ sarvam abhūj jagat. || 55 ||

## 2. Abschnitt. Vaṃśa | 2. Kapitel | A.  (L. 63.1—21; Mt. 5.1—32; P.1. I. 6.1—33a; P.2.V. 6.1—33. cfr. Kū. 16.1—16.)

devānāṃ dānavānāṃ ca gandharvoragarakṣasāṃ |
utpattiṃ vistareṇaiva sūta brūhi yathātathaṃ! || 1 ||
sūta (pulastya) uvāca:
saṅkalpād darśanāt sparśāt pūrvesāṃ sṛṣṭir ucyate, |
dakṣāt prācetasād ūrdhvaṃ sṛṣṭir maithunasamḥhavā. || 2 ||
prajāḥ sṛṣṭeti vyādiṣṭaḥ pūrvaṃ dakṣaḥ svayamḥhuvā |
yathā sasarja caivādau, tathaiva śṛṇuta dvijāḥ! || 3 ||
yadā tu sṛṣṭas tasya devarṣigaṇapannagān |
na vṛddhim agamal lokas, tadā maithunayogatas || 4 ||
dakṣaḥ putrasahasrāni tad-āsiknyām ajījanat. |
tāms tu dṛṣṭvā mahābhāgān sisṛksur vividhāḥ prajāḥ || 5 ||
nāradaḥ prāha haryaśvān dakṣa-putrān samāgatān: |
bhuvaḥ pramaṇaṃ sarvatra jñātvordhvam adha eva ca || 6 ||
tataḥ sṛṣṭiṃ viṣeṣeṇa kurudhvaṃ ṛṣiṣattamāḥ! |
te tu tadvacanaṃ śrutvā prayātāḥ sarvatodiśaṃ, || 7 ||
adyāpi na nivartante samudrād iva sindhavaḥ. |
haryaśveṣu pranaṣṭeṣu punar dakṣaḥ prajāpatiḥ || 8 ||
vairinyāṃ eva putrānāṃ sahasram asṛjat prabhuḥ; |
śabalā nāma te vipraḥ sametāḥ sṛṣṭihetavaḥ. || 9 ||
nārado 'nugatān prāha punas tān pūrvavan muniḥ: |
bhuvaḥ pramāṇaṃ sarvatra jñātvā bhrātr̄n atho punaḥ || 10 ||
āgatyā cātha sṛṣṭiṃ vai kariṣyatha viśeṣataḥ! |
te 'pi tenaiva mārgeṇa jagmur bhrātr̄pathā tadā. || 11 ||
tataḥ prabhṛti na bhrātuḥ kaniyān mārgam icchati, |
anviṣyan duḥkham āpnoti, tena tat parivarjayet. || 12 ||
tatas teṣu api naṣṭeṣu ṣaṣṭiṃ kanyāḥ prajāpatiḥ |
vairinyāṃ, janayāmāsa dakṣaḥ prācetāsa tadā. || 13 ||
prādāt sa daśa dharmāya, kaṣyapāya trayodaśa, |
saptaviṃśati somāya, catasro 'riṣṭanemaye || 14 ||
dve caiva bhṛguputrāya, dve kṛśāśvāya dhimate |
dve caiv-aṅgirase tadvat; tāsāṃ nāmāni vistarāt || 15 ||
śṛṇudhvaṃ devamātṛṇāṃ prajāvistāram āditaḥ: |
arundhati vasur yāmi lambā bhānur marutvati || 16 ||
saṃkalpā ca muhūrtā ca sādhyā viśvā ca bhāmini |
dharmapatnyaḥ samākhyātas, tāsāṃ putrān nibodhata! || 17 ||
viśvedevās tu viśvāyāḥ, sādhyā sādhyān ajījanat, |
marutvatyāṃ marutvanto, vasos tu vasavas tathā, || 18 ||
bhānos tu bhānavo jātā, muhūrtāyāṃ muhūrtakāḥ, |
lambāyāṃ ghoṣanāmano, nāgavīthī tu yāmijā || 19 ||
pṛthivitalasaṃbhūtam arundhatyām ajāyata, |
saṃkalpāyās tu saṃkalpo; vasu-sṛṣṭiṃ nibodhata! || 20 ||
jyotiṣmantas tu ye devā vyāpakāḥ sarvatodiṣaṃ, |
vasavas te samākhyaṭās, teṣāṃ sargaṃ nibodhata: || 21 ||
āpo dhruvas ca somas ca dharaś caiv-ānilo 'nalaḥ |
pratyūṣaś ca prabhāsaś ca vasavo 'ṣtau prakīrtitāḥ. || 22 ||
āpasya putrās catvāraḥ: śānto vaitaṇḍa eva ca |
śambo 'tha munibabhrus ca yajñarakṣādhikāriṇaḥ. || 23 ||
dhruvasya kālaḥ putras tu, varcāḥ somād ajāyata, |
draviṇo havyavāhaś ca dharaputrāv imau smṛtau. || 24 ||
kalyāṇinyāṃ tataḥ prāṇo ramaṇaḥ śīsiro 'pi ca, |
manohard dharāt putrān avāpātha hareḥ sutā. || 25 ||
śivā manojavaṃ putram avijñātagatiṃ tathā |
avāpa c-ānalāt putrāv agniprāyagunau punaḥ. || 26 ||
agniputraḥ kumāras tu śarastambe vyajāyata, |
tasya śākho viśākhaś ca naigameyaś ca pṛṣṭhajāḥ. || 27 ||
apatyaṃ kṛttikānāṃ tu kārttikeyas tataḥ smṛtaḥ, |
pratyūṣasya ṛṣiḥ putro munir nāmātha devalaḥ. || 28 ||
viśvakarmā prabhāsasya putraḥ śilpi prajāpatiḥ |
prāsādabhavanodyānapratimābhūṣaṇādiṣu || 29 ||
taḍāgārāmakūpeṣu smṛtaḥ so 'maravardhakiḥ. |
ajaikapāḍ ahirbudhnyo virūpākṣo 'tha raivataḥ || 30 ||
haraś ca bahurūpaś ca tṛyambakaś ca sureśvaraḥ |
sāvitraś ca jayantaś ca pināki cāparājitaḥ, || 31 ||
ete rudrāḥ samākhyaṭā ekādaśa gaṇeśvarāḥ. |
eteṣāṃ mānasānāṃ tu triśūlavaradhārīṇāṃ || 32 ||
koṭyaś caturaśitis, tatputrās cāḥṣayā matāḥ |
dikṣu sarvāsu ye rakṣāṃ prakurvanti gaṇeśvarāḥ. |
ete vai putrapautrās ca surabhigarbhasambhavāḥ. || 33 ||

## 2. Abschnitt. Vaṃśa | 2. Kapitel | B.  (L. 63.22—41; Mt. 6.1—47; P.1 I. 6.33b—79; P.2 V. 6.34—80;cfr. Kū. 16.17—20, 18.11, 12.)

kaśyapasya pravakṣyāmi patnībhyāḥ putrapautrakān: |
aditir ditir danuś caiva ariṣṭā surasā tathā || 1 ||
surabhir vinatā caiva tāmrā krodhavaśā irā |
kadrūḥ khasā munis tadvat, tāsaṃ putrān nibodhata! || 2 ||
tuṣitā nāma ye devāś cāksuṣasyāntare manoḥ, |
vaivasvate 'ntare caite hy ādityā dvādaśa smṛtāḥ: || 3 ||
indro dhātā bhagas tvaṣṭā mitro 'tha varuṇo 'ṛyamā |
vivasvān savitā pūṣā amṣumān viṣṇur eva ca || 4 ||
ete sahasrakiraṇā ādityā dvādaśa smṛtāḥ, |
māricāt kaśyapāj jajñe putrān āditir uttamān. || 5 ||
kṛṣāśvasya ṛṣeḥ putrā devapraharaṇāḥ smṛtāḥ. |
ete devagaṇā viprāḥ pratimanvantareṣu ca || 6 ||
utpadyante praliyante kalpe kalpe tathaiva ca. |
ditiḥ putradvayaṃ lebhe kaśyapād iti naḥ śrutaṃ. || 7 ||
hiraṇyakaśipuṃ caiva hiraṇyākṣaṃ tathaiva ca. |
hiraṇyakaśipos tadvaj jātaṃ putracatuṣṭayam: || 8 ||
prahladāś c-ānuhlādaś ca samhlādo hlāda eva ca. |
prahladāputra āyuṣmān śibir bāṣkala eva ca || 9 ||
virocanaś caturthaś ca, sa baliṃ putram āptavān. |
baleḥ putraśataṃ tv āsid bāṇajyeṣṭhaṃ tato dvijāḥ: || 10 ||
dhṛtarāṣṭras tathā sūryaś candraś caudrāṃsutāpanaḥ |
nikumbhanābho gurvakṣaḥ kuṣir bhimo bibhiṣaṇaḥ, || 11 ||
evamādyās tu bahavo bāṇajyeṣṭhā guṇādhiḥā, |
bāṇaḥ sahasrabāhuś ca sarvāstragaṇasamyutaḥ, || 12 ||
tapasā toṣito yasya pure vasati śūlabhṛt, |
mahākālatvam agamat sāmyaṃ yaś ca pinākinaḥ. || 13 ||
hiranyākṣasya putro 'bhūd ulūkaḥ śakunis tathā |
bhūitasamṭāpanaś caiva mahānābhas tathaiva ca. || 14 ||
etebhyaḥ putrapautrāṇāṃ koṭayaḥ saptasaptatiḥ |
mahābalā mahākāyā nānārūpā mahaujasaḥ. || 15 ||
danuḥ putraśataṃ lebhe kaśyapād baladarpitaṃ; |
vipracittiḥ pradhāno 'bhūt teṣāṃ madhye mahābalaḥ, || 16 ||
dvimūrdhā śakuniś caiva tathā saṅkuśirodharaḥ |
ayomukhaḥ śambaras ca kapilo vāmanas tathā || 17 ||
māricir meghavāṃś caiva iragarbhaśirās tathā |
vidrāvaṇaś ca ketuś ca ketuviryaḥ śatahṛadaḥ || 18 ||
indrajit saptajic caiva vajranābhas tathaiva ca |
ekacakro mahābāhur vajrāksas tārakas tathā || 19 ||
asilomā pulomā ca bindur bāṇo mahāsuraḥ |
svarbhānur vṛṣaparvā ca, evamādyā danoḥ sutāḥ. || 20 ||
svarbhānos tu prabhā kanyā śaei caiva pulomajā, |
upadānavī mayasyāsit tathā mandodarī kuhūḥ, || 21 ||
śarmiṣṭhā sudarī caiva candrā ca vṛṣaparvanāḥ. |
pulomā kālakā caiva vaiśvānarasute hi te || 22 ||
bahvapatye mahāsatve māricasya parigrahe. |
tayoḥ ṣaṣṭiḥ sahasrāṇi dānavānām abhūt purā. || 23 ||
paulomān kālakeyāṃś ca mārico 'janayat purā, |
avadhyā ye 'marāṇāṃ vai hiranyapuravāsinaḥ; || 24 ||
caturmukhāl labdhavarās te hatā vijayena tu. |
vipracittiḥ saimhikeyān simhikāyām ajijanat, || 25 ||
hiranyakaśipor ye vai bhāgiṇeyās trayodaśa: |
vyāṃsaḥ śalyaḥ ca rājendra nalo vātāpir eva ca || 26 ||
ilvalo namucis caiva śvasṛpaś c-āñjanas tathā |
narakaḥ kālanābhas ca saramānas tathaiva ca || 27 ||
kālavīryaś ca vikhyāto danuvamśavivardhanāḥ. |
saṃblādasya tu daityasya nivātakavacāḥ smṛtāḥ, || 28 ||
avadhyāḥ sarvadevānāṃ gandharvoragarakṣasāṃ, |
ye hatā balam āśritya tv arjunena raṇājire. || 29 ||
ṣaṭ kanyā janayāmāsā tāmṛā māricabijataḥ: |
śukī śyeni ca bhāsi ca sugrīvī gṛdhrikā śuciḥ. || 30 ||
śuki śukān ulūkāṃś ca janayāmāsa dharmataḥ, |
śyenī śyenāṃś tathā bhāśī kurarān apy ajījanat, || 31 ||
gṛdhri gṛdhrān kapotāṃś ca pāravatavihaṃgamān, |
haṃsasārasakāraṇḍaplavāñ chucir ajījanat, || 32 ||
ajāśvameṣoṣṭrakharaṇ sugrīvi cāpy ajījanat. |
eṣa tāmrānvayaḥ prokto, vinatāyā nibodhata! || 33 ||
garuḍaḥ patatāṃ śreṣṭho aruṇaś ca patatrināṃ, |
saudāmanī tathā kanyā yeyaṃ nabhasi viśrutā. || 34 ||
sāṃpātīs ca jaṭāyus ca aruṇasya sutāṃ ubhau, |
sāṃpātīputro babhrus ca śiḥragas cāpi viśrutaḥ. || 35 ||
jaṭāyusaḥ karnikāraḥ śatagāmī ca viśrutau, |
sāraso rajjuvalās ca bheruṇḍas cāpi tatsutāḥ. || 36 ||
teṣām anantam abhavat pakṣiṇāṃ putrapautrakaṃ. |
surasāyāḥ sahasraṃ tu sarpāṇām abhavat purā. || 37 ||
sahasraśīrasāṃ kadruḥ sahasraṃ prāpa suvrata, |
pradhānās teṣu vikhyātāḥ saḍviṃśatir arīṃdamāḥ: || 38 ||
śeṣa-vāsuki-karkoṭa-śaṅkh-airāvata-kambalāḥ |
dhanamjaya-mahānila-padm-āśvatara-takṣakāḥ || 39 ||
elāpatra-mahāpadma-dhṛtarāṣṭra-balāhakāḥ |
śaṅkhapāla-mahāśaṅkha-puṣpadamṣṭra-śubhānanāḥ || 40 ||
sañkhuromā ca nahuṣo vāmanaḥ pāṇinas tathā |
kapilo durmukhaś cāpi patañjalir iti smṛtāḥ. || 41 ||
eṣām anantam abhavat sarveṣāṃ putrapautrakaṃ, |
prāyaṣo yat purā dagdhaṃ janamejayamandire. || 42 ||
raksogaṇaṃ krodhavaśā sunāmānam ajījanat, |
daṃṣṭriṇāṃ niyutaṃ teṣāṃ bhīmasenād agāt kṣayaṃ. || 43 ||
rudrāṇāṃ ca gaṇaṃ tadvad gomahiṣyau varāṅgaṇā |
surabhir janayāmāsa kaśyapāt saṃyatavratā. || 44 ||
munir munināṃ ca gaṇaṃ gaṇaṃ apsarasāṃ tathā, |
tathā kinnaragandharvān ariṣṭājanayad bahūn. || 45 ||
tṛṇavṛkṣalatāgulmam irā sarvam ajījanat, |
khasā tu yakṣarakṣāṃsi janayāmāsa koṭiśaḥ. || 46 ||
ete kaśyapadāyādāḥ śataṣo 'tha sahasraśaḥ |
eṣa manvantare rājan sargāḥ svārociṣe smṛtaḥ. || 47 ||
tata ekonapañcāsan marutāḥ kaśyapād ditiḥ |
janayāmāsa dharmajñān sarvān amaravallabhān. || 48 ||
