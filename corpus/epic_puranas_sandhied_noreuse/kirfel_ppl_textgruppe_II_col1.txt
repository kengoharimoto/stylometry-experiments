nopaskareṣu musalolūkhalādiṣu
jalaṃ ca nāvagāheta śūnyāgāraṃ ca varjayet
valmīkeṣu na tiṣṭheta na codvignamanā bhavet
vilikhen na nakhair bhūmiṃ nāṅgāreṇa na bhasmanā
na śayāluḥ sadā tiṣṭhed vyāyāmaṃ ca vivarjayet
na tuṣāṅgārabhasmāsthikapāleṣu samāviśet
varjayet kalahāṃ lokair gātrabhaṅgaṃ tathaiva ca
na muktakeśī tiṣṭheta nāśuciḥ syāt kadācana
na śayītottaraśirā na cāparaśirāḥ kvacit
na vastrahīnā nodvignā na cādracaranā satī
nāmaṅgalyāṃ vaded vācaṃ na ca hāsyādīkā bhavet
kuryāc ca gurubhir nityaṃ pūjāṃ māṅgalyātatparā
sarvauṣadhibhiḥ sṛṣṭena vāriṇā snānam ācaret
kṛtarakṣā subhūṣā ca vāstupūjanatatparā
kṛtarakṣā tu śuśrūṣā vācā pūjanatatparā
tiṣṭhet prasannavadanā bhartpriyahite ratā
dānaśīlā tṛtīyāyāṃ pārvaṇyaṃ naktam ācaret
itivṛttā bhaven nārī viśeṣeṇa tu garhiṇī
yas tu tasyā bhavet putraḥ śilāyurvṛddhisamyutaḥ
na garhayec ca bhartāraṃ sarvāvasthāsv api kvacit kṛṣāhaṃ durbalā caiva vārdhakyaṃ mama cāgataṃ
stanau me calitau sthānān mukhaṃ ca valibhaṅguraṃ
anyathā garbhapatanaṃ avāpnoti na saṃśayaḥ
tasmāt tvam anayā vṛttyā garbhe 'smīn yatnam ācara
svasty astu te gamiṣyāmi tathety uktas tayā punaḥ
paśyatāṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ tatraivāntaradhīyata
tataḥ sā kaśyapoktena vidhinā samatiṣṭhata
atha bhītas tathendro 'pi diteḥ pārśvam upāgamat
vihāya devasadanaṃ tāṃ śuśrūṣur avasthitaḥ
diteś chidrāntaraprepsur abhavat pākaśāsanaḥ
vīṇīto 'bhavad avyagraḥ praśāntavadano bahiḥ
ajānan kila tatkāryam ātmanaḥ śubham ācaran
tato varṣaśatānte sā nyūne tu divasais tribhiḥ
mene kṛtārtham ātmānaṃ prītyā vismitamānasā
akṛtvā pādayoḥ śaucaṃ śayānā muktamūrdhajā
nidrābharasamākrāntā divāparaśirāḥ kvacit
tatas tad antaraṃ labdhvā praviṣyāntaḥ śacīpatiḥ
vajreṇa saptadhā cakre taṃ garbhaṃ tridaśādhipaḥ
tataḥ saptaiva te jātaḥ kumārāḥ sūryavarcaṣaḥ
rudantaḥ sapta te bālā niṣiddhā dānavāriṇā
bhūyo 'pi rudataś caitān ekaikaṃ saptadbā hariḥ
ciccheda vṛtrahantā vai punas tūdarasaṃsthitaṃ
evam ekonapañcāśad bhūtvā te rurudur bhṛsaṃ
indro nivārayāmāsa mā rudadhvaṃ punaḥ punaḥ
tataḥ sa cintayāmāsa kim etad iti vṛtrahā
dharmasya kasya māhātmyāt punaḥ saṃjīvitās tv amī
viditvā dhyānayogena madanadvādaśīvrataṃ
nūnam etat pariṇataṃ adhunā kṛṣṇapūjanāt
vajreṇāpi hatāḥ santo na vināśam upāyayuḥ
eko 'py anekatām āpa yasmād udarago 'py alaṃ
avadhyā nūnam ete vai tasmād devā bhavantv iti
yasmān mā ruda ity uktā rudanto garbhasaṃsthitāḥ
maruto nāma te nāmṇā bhavantu makhabhāgināḥ
tataḥ prasādyā deveśaḥ kṣamasvṛti ditiṃ punaḥ
arthaśāstraṃ samāsthāya mayaitad duṣkṛtaṃ kṛtaṃ
kṛtvā marudgaṇaṃ devaiḥ samānam amarādhīpaḥ
ditiṃ vimānam āropya sasutām anayad divaṃ
yajñaabhāgabhujo jātā marutas te tato dvijāḥ
na jagmur aikyam asurair atas te suravallabhāḥ
ṛṣaya ūcuḥ bhiṣma uvāca ādisargaś ca yaḥ sūta kathito vistareṇa tu
pratisargaś ca yo yeṣām adhipāṃs tān vadasva naḥ
sūta pulastya uvāca yadābhiṣiktaḥ saka'e 'pi rājye pṛṭhur dharitṛyām adhipo bahūva
tathau adhīnām adhipaṃ cakāra dvija vratānāṃ tapasāṃ ca somaṃ
nakṣatratārādvijavṛkṣagulmalatāvitānasya ca rukmagarbhāṃ
apām adhiśaṃ varuṇaṃ dhanānāṃ rājñāṃ prabhuṃ vaiśravaṇaṃ ca tadvat
viṣṇuṃ raviṇām adhipaṃ vasūnām agniṃ ca lokādhīpatiṃ cakāra
prajāpatinām adhipaṃ ca dakṣaṃ cakāra śakraṃ marutām adhiśaṃ
daityādhīpānām atha dānavānāṃ prahḷādam īśaṃ ca yamaṃ pitṝṇāṃ
piśācarakṣaḥpaśubhūtayakṣavetālarājaṃ tv atha śūlapāṇiṃ
prāleyaśailaṃ ca patiṃ girīṇām īśaṃ samudraṃ saritām adhiśaṃ
gandharvavidyādharakinnarāṇām īśaṃ punaś citrarathaṃ cakāra
nāgādhīpaṃ vāsukim ugravīryaṃ sarpādhīpaṃ takṣakam ādideśa
digvāraṇānām adhipaṃ cakāra gajeudram airāvaṇanāmadheyaṃ
suparṇam īśaṃ patatām athāśvarājānam uccaiḥśravasaṃ cakāra
siṃhaṃ mṛgāṇāṃ vṛṣabhaṃ gavāṃ ca plakṣaṃ punaḥ sarvavanaspā tīnāṃ
pitāmahaḥ pūrvam athābhyaṣiñcad etān punaḥ sarvadiśādhīnāthān
pūrveṇa dikpālam athābhyaṣiñcan nāmnā sudharmāṇam arātiketuṃ
tato 'dhipaṃ dakṣiṇataś cakāra sarveśvaraṃ śaṅkhapadābhidhānaṃ
sa ketumantaṃ digadhīśam iśaś cakāra paścād bhuvanāṇḍagarbhaḥ
hiraṇyaromāṇam udagdigīśaṃ prajāpātir meghasutam eakāra
adyāpi kurvanti diśām adhīśāḥ śatrūn dahantas tu bhuvo 'bhirakṣāṃ
caturbhir ebhiḥ pṛṭhunāmadheyo nṛpo 'bhiṣiktaḥ prathamaṃ pṛṭhivyāṃ
manvantare cādhigate ta eva vaivasvataṃ cakrur imaṃ pṛṭhivyāṃ
gate 'ntare cākṣuṣanāmadheye vaivasvatākhye ca punaḥ pravṛtte
prajāpatiḥ so 'sya carācarasya babhūva sūryānvayavaṃśacihnaḥ
ṛṣaya ūcuḥ bhiṣma uvāca bahubhir dharaṇī bhuktā bhūpālaiḥ śrūyate purā
pārthivāḥ pṛthivīyogāt pṛthivī kasya yogataḥ
kimarthaṃ ca kṛtā saṃjñā bhūmeḥ kiṃ pāribhāṣikī
gaur itīyaṃ ca vikhyātā sūta pulastya uvāca vaṃśe svāyaṃbhuvasyāsīd aṅgo nāma prajāpatiḥ
mṛtyos tu duhitā tena pariṇītātīdurmukhī
sunīthā nāma tasyās tu veno nāma sutaḥ purā
adharmanirataḥ kāmī oalavān vasudhādhipaḥ
lokasyadharmakṛc cāpi parabhāryāpahārakaḥ
dharmācārasya siddhyarthaṃ jagato 'ṛthe maharṣibhiḥ
anuṇīto 'pi na dadāv aśuddhātmabhayaṃ tataḥ
śāpena mārayitvainam arājakabhayārditāḥ
mamanthur brāhmaṇās tasya balād deham akalmaṣāḥ
tatkāyān mathyamānāt tu nipetur mlecchajātayaḥ
śārīre mātur aṃśena kṛṣṇāñjanasamaprabhāḥ
pitur aṃśasya cāṃśena dhārmiko dharmakārakaḥ
utpanno dakṣiṇād dhaṣtāt sadhanuḥ saśaro gadī
divyatejomayavapuḥ saratnakavacāṅgadaḥ
pṛthor evābhavad yatnāt tataḥ pṛthur ajāyata
sa viprair abhiṣiktas tu tapaḥ kṛtvā suduṣkaraṃ
viṣṇor vareṇa sarvasya prabhutvam agamat prabhuḥ
niḥsvādhyāyavaṣaṭkāraṃ nirdharmaṃ vikṣya bhūtalaṃ
veddhum evodyataḥ kopāc chareṇāmitavikramaḥ
tato gorūpam āsthāya bhūḥ palāyitum udyatā
pṛṣṭhato 'nugatas tasyāḥ pṛthur diptaśarāsanaḥ
tataḥ sthitvaikadeśe tu kiṃ karomīti cābravīt
pṛthur apy avadad vākyam īpsiṭaṃ dehi suvrate
sarvasya jagataḥ śīghraṃ sthāvarasya carasya ca
tatheti cabravīd bhūmir dudoḥa sa narādhipaḥ
svake pāṇau pṛthur vatsaṃ kṛtvā svāyaṃbhuvaṃ manuṃ
tad annam abhavad dugdhaṃ prajā jīvanti yena tu
tatas tu ṛṣibhir dugdhā vatsaḥ somas tadābhavat
dogdhā bṛhaspatir abhūt pātraṃ vedas tapo rasaḥ
devaiś ca vasudhā dugdhā dogdhā mitras tadābhavat
indro vatsaḥ samabhavat kṣīram ūrjasvaḷaṃ balaṃ
devānāṃ kāñcanaṃ pātraṃ pitṝṇāṃ rājataṃ tathā
antakaś cābhavad dogdhā yamo vatsaḥ svadhā rasaḥ
alābupātraṃ nāgānāṃ takṣako vatsako 'bhavat
viṣaṃ kṣīraṃ tato dogdhā dhṛtarāṣṭro 'bhavat punaḥ
asurair api dugdhāyam āyase śatrupīḍanaṃ
pātre māyām abhūd vatsaḥ prāhlādis tu virocanaḥ
dogdhā trimūrdhā tatrāsīn māyā yena pravartitā
yakṣaiś ca vasudhā dugdhā purāntardhānam īpsubhiḥ
kṛtvā vaiśravaṇaṃ vatsam āmapātre mahīpate
pretarakṣogaṇair dugdhā dharā rudhiram ulbaṇaṃ
raupyanābho 'bhavad dogdhā sumālī vatsa eva tu
gandharvaiś ca punar dugdhā vasudhā sāpsarogaṇaiḥ
vatsaṃ cītrarathaṃ kṛtvā gandhān padmadale tathā
dogdhā vararucir nāma nātyavedasya pāragaḥ
evaṃ śrutvā manuḥ prāha punar eva janārdanam
pūrveṣāṃ caritaṃ brūḥı manūnāṃ madhusūdana
manvantarāṇi sarvāṇi manūnāṃ caritaṃ ca yat
pramāṇaṃ caiva kālasya tat ṛṣṭiṃ ca samāsataḥ
ekacittaḥ prasannātmā śṛṇu mārtanḍaandana
yāmā nāma purā devā āsan svāyambhuvāntare
saptaiva ṛṣayaḥ pūrvaṃ ye marīcyādayaḥ smṛtāḥ
agnīdhraś cāgnibāhuś ca vibhuḥ savana eva ca
jyotiṣmān dyutimān havyo medhā medhātithir vasuḥ
svāyambhuvasyāsyā manor daśāite vaṃśavardhanāḥ
pratisargam ime kṛtvā jagmus te paramaṃ padaṃ
etat svāyambhuvaṃ proktaṃ svārociṣam ataḥ paraṃ
svārociṣasya tanayāś catvāro devavarcaṣaḥ
nabhonabhasyaprasṛtibhānavaḥ kīrtivardhanāḥ
datto 'gniścyavanas tambhaḥ prāṇaḥ kaśyapa eva ca
aurvo bṛhaspatiś caiva saptaite ṛṣayaḥ smṛtāḥ
devāś ca tuṣita nāma smṛtāḥ svārociṣe 'ntare
hastīndraḥ sukṛto mūrtir āpo jyotir ayaḥ smayaḥ
vasiṣṭhasya sutāḥ sapta ye prajāpatayaḥ smṛtāḥ
dviṭīyam etat kathitaṃ manvantaram ataḥ paraṃ
auttamiyaṃ pravakṣyāmi tathā manvantaraṃ śubhaṃ
manur nāmauttamir yatra daśa putrān ajījanat
īṣa ūrjas tanūrjaś ca śuciḥ śukras tathaiva ca
madhuś ca mādhavaś caiva nabhasyo 'tha nabhās tathā
sahaḥ kanīyān eteṣām udārāḥ kīrtivardhanaḥ
bhānavas tatra devāḥ syur ūrjāḥ saptarṣayaḥ smṛtāḥ
kaukuruṇḍiḥ kuruṇḍiś ca dālbhyaḥ śaṅkhaḥ pravāhiṇaḥ
mitaś ca saṃmitaś caiva saptaite yogavardhanāḥ
manvantaraṃ caturthaṃ tu tāmasaṃ nāma viśrutaṃ
kaviḥ pṛthus tathaivagnir akapiḥ kapir eva ca
tathaiva janyadhāmānau munayaḥ sapta nāma te
sādhyā devagaṇā yatra kathitās tāmase 'ntare
akalmaṣas tathā dhanvī tapomūlas tapodhanaḥ
taporatis tapasyaś ca tapodyutiparaṃtapau
tapobhāgī tapoyogī dharmācāraratāḥ sadā
tāmasasya sutāḥ sarve daśa vaṃśavivardhanāḥ
pañcamasya manos tadvad raivatasyāntaraṃ śṛṇu
devabāhuḥ subāhuś ca parjanyaḥ somapo muniḥ
hiraṇyaromā saptāśvaḥ saptaite ṛṣayaḥ smṛtāḥ
devāś ca bhūttarajasas tathā prakṛtayāḥ smṛtāḥ
avaśas tattvadarśī ca vītimān havyapaḥ kapiḥ
yukto nirutsukaḥ sattvo nirmoho 'tha prakāśakaḥ
sādhyā viśve ca rudrāś ca maruto vasavo 'śvinau
ādityāś ca surās tadvat sapta devagaṇāḥ smṛtāḥ
ikṣvākupramukhāś cāsyā daśa putrāḥ smṛtā bhuvi
manvantareṣu sarveṣu sapta sapta maharṣayaḥ
kṛtvā dharmavyavasthānaṃ prayānti paramaṃ padaṃ
bhaviṣyā daśa sāvarṇer manoḥ putrāḥ prakīrtitāḥ
raucyādayas tathānye 'pi manavaḥ saṃprakīrtitāḥ
ruceḥ prajāpateḥ putro raucyo nāma bhaviṣyati
manur bhūtisutas tadvad bhautyo nāma bhaviṣyati
tatas tu merusāvarṇir brahmasūnur manuḥ smṛtaḥ
ṛtaś ca ṛtudhāmā ca viṣvakseno manus tathā
atītānāgatāś caiva manavaḥ parikīrtitāḥ
varṣāṇāṃ yugasāhasram ebhir vyāptaṃ narādhīpa
sve sve 'ntare sarvam idam utpādyā sacarācaraṃ
kalpakṣaye vinirvṛtte mucyante brahmaṇā saha
ete yugasahasrānte vinaśyanti punaḥ punaḥ
brahmādyā viṣṇusāyujyaṃ tato yāsyanti vai dvijāḥ
ādityavamśam akhilaṃ vada sūta yathākramaṃ
somavamśaṃ ca tattvajña yathāvad vaktum arhasi
sūta pulastya uvāca vivasvān kaśyapāt pūrvam adityām abhavat sutaḥ
tasya patnītrayaṃ tadvat samjñā rājñī prabhā tathā
raivatasya sutā rājñī revataṃ sušuve sutaṃ
prabhā prabhātaṃ sušuve tvāṣṭrī samjñā tathā manuṃ
yamaś ca yamunā caiva yamalau tu babhūvatuḥ
tatas tejomayaṃ rūpam asahantī vivasvataḥ
nārīm utpādayāmāsa svaśarīrād aninditāṃ
tvāṣṭrī svarūparūpeṇa nāmnā chāyeti bhāminī
purataḥ samsthitāṃ dṛṣṭvā samjñā tāṃ pratyabhāṣata
chāye tvaṃ bhaja bhartāraṃ madīyaṃ ca varānane
apatyāni madīyāni māṭṛṣneheṇa pālaya
tathety uktvā ca sā devam agāt kāmāya suvratā
kāmayāmāsa devo 'pi samjñeyam iti cādarāt
janayāmāsa śāvarṇiṃ manuṃ manusvarūpiṇaṃ
savarṇatvāc ca sāvarṇir manor vaivasvatasya ca
tataḥ śanīṃ ca tapatīṃ viṣṭīṃ caiva krameṇa tu
chāyāyāṃ janayāmāsa samjñeyam iti bhāskarāḥ
chāyā svaputre 'bhyadhikaṃ snehaṃ cakre manau tadā
pūrvo manus tac caksāma yamas tu krodhamūrchitaḥ
samtarjayāmāsa tadā pādam udyāmya dakṣiṇaṃ
śāśāpa ca yamaṃ chāyā savraṇaḥ kṛmisaṃyutaḥ
pādo 'yam eko bhavitā pūyaśoṇitavisrāvaḥ
nivedayāmāsa pitur yamaḥ śāpena dharṣitaḥ
niṣkāraṇam ahaṃ śapto mātrā deva sakopayā
bālabhāvān mayā kiṃcid udyataś carāṇaḥ sakṛt
manunā vāryamāṇāpi mama śāpam adād vibho
prāyo na mātā sāsmākam asamā snehato yataḥ
devo 'py āha yamaṃ bhūyaḥ kiṃ karomi mahāmate
saukhyāt kasya na duḥkhaṃ syād athavā karmaśaṃtatiḥ
anivāryā bhavasyāpi kā kathānyeṣu jantuṣu
kṛkāvākus tava pade sa kṛmīn bhakṣayiṣyati
kledaṃ ca rudhiraṃ caiva vatsāyam apaneṣyati
evam uktas tapas tepe yamas tīvraṃ mahāyaśāḥ
gokarṇatīrthe vairāgyāt phalapatrānilāsanaḥ
ārādhayan mahādevaṃ yāvad varṣāyutāyutaṃ
varaṃ prādān mahādevaḥ saṃtuṣṭaḥ śūlabhṛt tadā
vavre sa lokapālatvaṃ pitṛlokaṃ tathākṣayaṃ
dharmādharmātmakasyasya jagatas tu parīkṣaṇaṃ
evaṃ sa lokapālatvaṃ agamac chūlapāṇinaḥ
yaḥ karoti sa pāpiṣṭhāṃ gatim āpnoti ninditāṃ
kuṣṭharogam avāpnoti loke 'smīn duḥkhasaṃyutaṃ
tasmāc ca dharmakāmārthī citreṣv āyataneṣu ca
na kvacit kārayet pādau devadevasya dhīmataḥ
tataḥ sa bhagavān gatvā bhūrlokam amarādhīpaḥ
kāmayāmāsa kāmārto mukha eva divākaraḥ
aśvarūpeṇa mahatā tejasā ca samanvitaḥ
saṃjñā ca manasā kṣobham agamad bhayavihvalā
nāsāpuṭābhyām utsṛṣṭaṃ paro 'yam iti śaṅkayā
tasyātha retaso jātāv aśvināv iti naḥ śrutaṃ
dasrau sutatvāt saṃjātāu nāsatyau nāsikāgrataḥ
jñātvā cirāc ca sā devaṃ saṃtoṣam agamat paraṃ
vimānenāgamat svargaṃ patyā saha mudānvitā
sāvarṇo 'pi manur merāv adyāpi tapate tapaḥ
śanis tapobalāc cāpi grahāṇāṃ samatāṃ gataḥ
yamunā tapatī caiva punar nadyau babhūvatuḥ
viṣṭir ghorātmikā tadvat kālatvena vyavasthitā
manor vaivasvatasyasan daśa putrā mahābalāḥ
ilas tu prathamas teṣāṃ putreṣṭyāṃ samajāyata
ikṣvākuḥ kuśanābhaś ca ariṣṭo dhṛṣṭa eva ca
nariṣyantaḥ karūṣaś ca śaryātiś ca mahābalāḥ
pṛṣadhraś cātha nābhāgaḥ sarve te divyamānuṣāḥ
abhiṣicya manuḥ putram ilaṃ jyeṣṭhaṃ sa dhārmikaṃ
jagāma tapase bhūyaḥ sa mahendranālayaṃ
athājagāma siddhyarthaṃ tasya brahmā varapradaḥ
varaṃ varaya bhadraṃ te mānaveya yathepsitaṃ
uvāca sa tadā devaṃ padmākṣaṃ padmajaṃ vibhum
vaśe me dharmaśamyuktāḥ pṛthivyāṃ sarvapārthivāḥ
bhaveyur iśvarāḥ svāmin prasādāt tava kañjaja
tathety uktvā ca deveśas tatraivāntaradhīyata
tato 'yodhyāṃ samāgatya samatiṣṭhad yathā purā
athaikadā rathārūḍha ilo nijasuto manoḥ
atha digjayasiddhyarthaṃ ilaḥ prāyān mahīm imāṃ
bhraman dvīpāni sarvāṇi kṣmābhṛtaḥ saṃprasādhayan
jagāmopavanaṃ śambhor aśvākṛṣṭaḥ pratāpavān
kalpadrumalatākīrṇaṃ nāmnā śaravaṇaṃ mahat
ramate yatra deveśaḥ śambhuḥ somārdhaśekharaḥ
umayā samayas tatra purā śaravaṇe kṛtaḥ
pumṛnāma sattvaṃ yat kiṃcid āgamiṣyati no vanaṃ
strītvam eṣyati tat sarvaṃ daśayojanamaṇḍale

saptamaṃ devakī putraṃ madanam suṣuve sutam
gaveṣaṇaṃ mahābhāgaṃ samgrāmeṣv aparājitaṃ
śraddbādevyā vihāre tu vane hi vicaran purā
vaiśyāyāṃ adadhāc chauriḥ putraṃ kauśikam agrajaṃ
sutanū vanarājī ca śaurer āstām parigrahau
puṇḍraś ca kapilaś caiva vasudevātmajau balau
jarā nāma niṣādo 'bhūt prathamāḥ sa dhanurdharāḥ
saubhadraś ca bhavaś caiva mahāsattvau babhūvatuḥ
devabhāgasutaś cāpi nāmnasāv uddhavaḥ smṛtaḥ
paṇḍitaṃ prathamaṃ prāhur devaśravasam uttamam
aikṣvāky alabhatāpatyam anādhṛṣṭer yaśasvinī
nivṛttaśatruṃ śatrughnaṃ śrāddhas tasmād ajāyata
gaṇḍūśāyānapatyāya kṛṣṇas tuṣṭaḥ sutaṃ dadau
sucandraṃ tu mahābhāgaṃ vīryavantaṃ mahābalaṃ
jāmbavatyāḥ sutāv etau dvau ca satkṛtalakṣaṇau
cārudeṣṇaś ca sāmbaś ca vīryavantau mahābalau
rantipālaś ca rantiś ca nandanasya sutāv ubhau
śamīkaputrāś catvāro vikrāntāḥ sumahābalāḥ
virajaś ca dhanuś caiva śyāmaś ca śṛñjayas tathā
anapatyo 'bhavac chyāmaḥ śamīkas tu vanaṃ yayau
jugupsamāno bhojatvaṃ rājarṣitvam avāptavān
kṛṣṇasya janmābhyudayaṃ yaḥ kīrtayati nityaśaḥ
śṛṇoti vā naro nityaṃ sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate
