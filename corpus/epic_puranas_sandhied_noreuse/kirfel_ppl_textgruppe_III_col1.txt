tat sarvaṃ vistareṇa tvaṃ dharmaṃ vyākhyaṭum arbasī
matsya uvāca mahāpralayakālānta etad āsīt tamomayaṃ
prasuptam iva cātarkyam aprajñātam alakṣaṇaṃ
tadantar bhagavān eṣa sūryaḥ samabhaveḍ purā
tam avehī rajorūpaṃ mahat sattvam udāhṛtaṃ
manur uvāca caturmukhatvam agamat kasmāl lokapitāmahaḥ
aṅgirās cābhavat paścāt pulastyas tadanantaraṃ
tataḥ pulahanāmā vai tataḥ kratur ajāyata
putro bhṛgur abhūt tadvan nārado 'py acirād abhūt
bhrūmadhyād abhavat krodho lobhas cādharasambhavaḥ
buddher mohaḥ samabhavad ahaṃkārad abhūn madaḥ
bharataḥ karamadhyāt tu brahmasūnur abhūt tataḥ
manur uvāca buddher mohaḥ samabhavad iti yat parikīrtitaṃ
ahaṃkāraḥ smṛtaḥ krodho buddhir nāma kim ucyate
matsya uvāca sattvaṃ rajas tamaś caiva guṇatrayam udāhṛtaṃ
kecit pradhānam ity āhur avyaktam apare jaguḥ
savikārāt pradhānāt tu mahattattvaṃ prajāyate
triguṇaṃ tadvikāreṇa tac chabdasparśarūpavat
tataḥ samjapatas tasya bhittvā deham akalmaṣaṃ
strīrūpam ardhāṃ akarod ardhāṃ puruṣarūpavat
sarasvaty atha gāyatrī brahmāṇī ca paraṃtapa
putrebhyo lajjitasyāsyā tadrūpālokanecchayā
tato 'nyad abhavat tasya kāmāturāyā tathā
tat sarvaṃ nāśām agamat svasutopagamecchayā
tenordhvaṃ vaktram abhavat pañcamaṃ tasya dhīmataḥ
tatas tān abravīd brahmā pūtrān ātmasamudbhavān
salajjāṃ cakame devaḥ kamalodaramandire
auttamipramukhās tadvad yeṣāṃ tvaṃ saptamo 'dhunā
parasparaṃ ca saṃbandhaḥ sagotrānām abhūt kathaṃ
divyatejomayī bhūpa divyajñānasamudbhavā
viriūcir yatra bhāgavāṃs tatra devī sarasvatī
bhārātī yatra yatraiva tatra tatra prajāpatiḥ
tasmāt tvaddeham acirād rudro bhasmīkariṣyati
indriyakṣobhajanakaḥ sarveṣām eva dehināṃ
kṣobhyaṃ manaḥ prayatnena tvayaivoktaṃ purā vibho
brahmovāca vaivasvate 'ntare prāpte yādavānvayasambhavaḥ
tadbhratus tatsamasya tvaṃ tada putratvaṃ cśyasi
sukhani dharmataḥ prapya matsamipaṃ gamiśyasi
śokapramodabhiyuto jagama sa yathagataṃ
manur uvaca ko 'sau yadur iti prokto yadvaṃśe kamasaṃbhavaḥ
kathaṃ ca dagdho rudreṇa kimarthaṃ kusumayudhaḥ
matsya uvaca ya sa dehardbasamṛbhuta gayatri brahmavadini
ratir manas tapo buddhir mahan dik samṛbhramas tatha
vamadevas tu bhagavan asṛjan mukhato dvijan
rājanyan asṛjad bahvor vicchudran ūrupadayoḥ
chandaṃśi ca sasarjadau parjanyaṃ ca tataḥ paraṃ
vamo 'sṛjad aḥmartyaṃś tan brahmaṇa vinivaritaḥ
tāny ahaṃ bhavate samyak kathayāmi yathākrāmam
vaivasvato 'yaṃ yasyai'tat saptamaṃ vartate 'ntaraṃ
manvantarādhipān samyag devarṣiṃs tatsutāṃs tathā
tṛṭiye tv antare caiva auttamir nāma vai manuḥ
rajo gātro 'ṛdhabāhuś ca vaśiṣṭhatanayās tatra savanaś cānaghas tathā sapta saptarṣayo 'bhavan
sutapāḥ śakra ity ete ajāḥ paraśudivyādyās sapta saptarṣayo 'bhavan tasyauttamimanoḥ sutāḥ
babhūva śaikare bhakto saptarṣayaś ca ye teṣāṃ mahādevārcane rataḥ tatra nāmāni me śṛṇu
jyotirdhāmā pṛthuḥ kāvyaś caitro 'gnir vanakas tathā
pivarās carṣayo hy ete sapta tatrāpi cāntare
ete devagaṇās tatra caturdaśa caturdaśa
ete saptarṣayo vipra tatrāsan raivate 'ntare
narendrāḥ sumahāviryā babhūvur munisattama
manojavas tathaivendro devān api nibodha me
puramdaras tathaivātra maitreya tridaśeśvaraḥ
viṣṇuśaktir anaupamyā sattvodriktā sthitaḥ sthitā
tadaṣabhūtā rājānaḥ manvantareṣv aṣeṣeṣu sarve ca tridivaukasaḥ devatvenādhitīṣṭhati
auttame tv antare caiva tuṣitas tu punaḥ sa vai
tāmasasyāntare caiva samprāpte punar eva hi
manvantare tu samprāpte tathā vaivasvate dvija
puramdarāya trailokyaṃ dattaṃ nihatakaṇṭakaṃ
ity etās tanavas tasya saptamanvantareṣu vai
sarve ca devā manavaḥ samastāḥ saptarṣayo ye manusūnavas ca
maitreya uvāca proktāny etāni bhavatā sapta manvantarāni vai
manur yamo yamī caiva tadapatyāni vai mune
tadānyeyam asau buddhir ity āsīd yamasūryayoḥ
janayāmāsa revantaṃ retaso 'nte ca bhāskaraḥ
bhrānim āropya sūryaṃ tu tasya tejoviśātanaṃ
jājvalyamānam apatat tad bhūmau munisattama
tasya manvautaraṃ hy etat sāvarṇakam athaṣṭamaṃ
sāvarṇis tu manur yo 'sau maitreya bhavitā tataḥ
saptarṣin api vakṣyāmi bhaviṣyān munisattama
teṣām indro mahāvīryo bhaviṣyaty adbhuto dvija
savano dyutimān bhavyo vasur medhādhṛtis tathā
jyotiṣmān saptamaḥ satyas tatraite ca maharṣayaḥ
daśamo brahmāsāvarṇir bhaviṣyati mune manuḥ
haritā lohitā devās tathā sumanaso dvija
tapasvi sutapās caiva tapomṛtis taporatiḥ
trayodaśo raucyanāmā bhaviṣyati mune manuḥ
nirmohas tattvadarsī ca niṣprakampo nirutsukaḥ
saptarṣayas tv ime tasya putrān api nibodha me
citrasenavicitrādyā bhaviṣyanti mahīkṣitāḥ
bhautyaś caturdaśaś cātra maitreya bhavitā manuḥ
pravartayanti tān etya bhuvi saptarṣayo divaḥ
devā yajñabhujas te tu yāvan manvantaraṃ tu tat
bhavanti ye manuḥ putrā yāvan manvantaraṃ tu taiḥ
tadanvayodbhavaīś caiva tāvad bhūḥ paripālyate
caturdaśabhir etais tu gatair manvantarair dvija
sahasrayugaparyantāḥ kalpo niḥṣeṣa ucyate
trailokyam akhilaṃ grastvā bhagavān ādikṛd vibhuḥ
sātviko 'mṣaḥ sthitikaro jagato dvijasattama
yugavyavasthāṃ kurute yathā maitreya tat śṛṇu
duṣṭānāṃ nigrahaṃ kurvan paripāti jagattrayaṃ
karoti bahulaṃ bhūyo vedavyāsasvarūpadhṛk
vedāṃs tu dvāpare vyasya kaler ante punar hariḥ
kalkisvarūpi durvṛttān mārge sthāpayati prabhuḥ
evam eṣa jagat sarvaṃ paripāti karoti ca
hanti cānteṣv anantātmā nāsty asmād vyatireki yat
tad atrānyatra vā vipra sadbhāvaḥ kathitas tava
