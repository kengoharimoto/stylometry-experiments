śrī gaṇeśāya namaḥ oṃ namaḥ śivāya
jagadārtiharaṃ devaṃ śūlapāṇinnamostute
sarvakāraṇaśeṣasya jagataḥ sarvadā śivam
gobrāhmaṇanṛpāṇāñca śivaṃ bhavatu sarvataḥ
śivamādau śivaṃ madhye śivamante ca sarvadā
sarveṣāṃ śivabhaktānāṃ manujānāñca naḥ śivam
sanatkumāraḥ sutapāḥ pṛcchati sma yathāvidhiḥ
agniṣṭomādayo yajñā bahuvittakṛyānvitāḥ
hitāya sarvasatvānāṃ śivadharmaṃ sanātanam
paramasarvadharmāṇāṃ śivadharmaṃ śivātmakam
gaṇānāṃ devamukhyānāmasmākañca viśeṣataḥ
śivadharmāntu yaḥ śrīmān uddhārārthamudāhṛtam
yena yaṃ śāntacetaskaiḥ śivabhaktaiḥ śivārthibhi
kīrtayiṣyanti ye rudraṃ sakṛd apy aśubhair narāḥ
śauryaṃśca jagataḥ khyātiṃ śivamarcayato bhavet
śivadharmasya sāro 'ayaṃ śive bhaktiḥ suniścalā
sā cāṣṭāṅgī śivenoktā kāryā nityaṃ prayatnataḥ
śrībhagavan uvāca
svayaṃ me 'abhyarcanaṃ kuryāt mamārthe cāṅgaceṣṭitam
sa māṃ parvatadurgeṣu mārgamāṇo vipadyate
yo māṃ sarvagataṃ paśyet sarvaśca mayi saṃsthitaḥ
nandikeśvaraḥ uvāca
sudurlabhaṃ śivajñānaṃ tadabhyāso 'api durlabhaḥ
sudurlabhataraṃ yajñaṃ japadhyānaṃ śivātmakam
sudurlabhamidaṃ jñātvā śavabhaktiṃ samāśrayet
nirdiṣṭaḥ prathame 'adhyāye śeṣo 'asau ca pravistaraḥ
namastebhyo manuṣyebhyo ye śivaṃ śaraṇaṅgatāḥ
yeṣāṃ divā ca rātrau ca nityaṃ śivagatasmṛtiḥ
namaḥ śivāya
nīrājanauṣadhī bīja snāne maṅgalavādane
ghṛtasnāne ca yatpuṇyaṃ ghṛtābhyaṅge ca yatphalam
muktapuṣpārcane yacca mālayā liṅgapūraṇe
pratākādhvajachatrāṇāṃ ghaṃṭāvāditraśaṃkhayā
vitānavastrapūjāyāṃ balihome kriyāsu ca
gavāṃśca bhṛtyanāgānāṃ bhūmidāne ca yatphalam
grāmakheṭapurāṇāśca daśadāne ca yatphalam
tatpālānāpahāre ca puṇyāpuṇyantu yadbhavet
yatināmannadānāni pātropakaraṇāni ca
datvā kiṃ labhate puṇyaṃ datvā cāpi pratiśrayam
iti śivadharmapraśnādhyāyo dvitīyaḥ
oṃ namaḥ śivāya
vivādaḥ sumahānāsīd brahmāviṣṇoḥ parasparam
evamāha hariṃ brahmā brahmāṇaṃśca haristathā
jvālāmālāvṛtandivyam aprameyaguṇoditam
adhovalambaye viṣṇoragādūrdhaṃ pitāmahaḥ
kṛtāñjalipuṭau bhūtvā liṅgantuṣṭavatustathā
liṅgamadhye paraṃ liṅgaṃ sthitaṃ dvādaśasammitam
naiva tatkāñcanaṃ raupyaṃ tāmraṃ sphaṭikamauktikam
lakṣamātraṃ sthitaṃ śāntaṃ kevalavtacchivātmakam
prāñjaliḥ praṇato viṣṇurbrahmāpi vedatatparaḥ
pūrvaṃ liṅgaṃ samabhyarcya tadā kāryāṇi cakratuḥ
brahmā brahmākṣarairdivyairliṅgaṃ pūjayate sadā
talliṅgānukṛtaṃ liṅgam ījire munayastathā
paraṃ bhūtaśarīrasthaṃ liṅgaṃ kṣetramanādi yat
yadādyamaiśvarantejastalliṅgaṃ pañcasaṃjñakam
ākāśaṃ liṅgamityāhuḥ pṛthivī tasya pīṭhikā
ālayaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ līyanālliṅgamucyate
sthāpitantena sarvaṃ syāt pūjitaśca na saṃśayaḥ
brahmā pūjayate nityaṃ liṅgaśailamayaṃ śubham
śakropi devarājendro liṅgaṃ maṇimayaṃ śubham
vāyutvaṃ prāptavāntena anaupamyaguṇānvitam
sphaṭikaṃ nirmalaṃ liṅgaṃ varuṇo 'arcayate sadā
ārhatastu sadākālaṃ puṣpaliṅgārcanātparam
pravālakamayaṃ liṅgaṃ yajantaḥ yanna gottamāḥ
kṛṣṇāyasamayaṃ liṅgaṃ pūjayantyasurottamāḥ
rākṣasāśca mahātmānastena te 'amitavikramāḥ
trapusīsamayaṃ liṅgaṃ piśācā pūjayanti tat
tena bhogaguṇopetāḥ prayātīśvaramandriram
liṅge devo mahādevaḥ sākṣādeva vyavasthitaḥ
anugrahāya lokānāṃ tasmālliṅgaṃ prapūjayet
evaṃ nirvahate yastu yāvajjīvaṃ pratijñayā
tasmāt sarveṣu bhūteṣu gatirdevo maheśvaraḥ
sāgarāṇāṃ kṣayopyasti kṣīyate himavānapi
rudraloke kṣayo nāsti śaṅkare śaraṅgate
liṅgadvayaṃ samākhyātaṃ sacarācarameva ca
caraṃ prāṇeti vikhyātam acaraṃ pārthivādiṣu
acara yattu saṃskāro dvayaṃ nityaṃ sadāśivam
tasmālliṅgadvayaṃ prājño nāvamayeta jātucit
yo liṅgaṃ sthāpadekaṃ vidhipūrvaṃ sadakṣiṇam
saptadvipasamudrāyāḥ kṣiteradhipatirvaśī
yaḥ kṛtvā pārthivaṃ liṅgam arcayīta sureśvaram
ihaiva dhanavān śrīmān so 'ante rudraśca jāyate
śālipiṣṭamayaṃ liṅgaṃ sarvagandhopaśobhitam
kīḍanneva ca yaḥ kuryālliṅgaṃ kaściddvijattamaḥ
pyaṅgulakamātraṃ vā liṅgaṃ kṛtvā yaḥ pūjayet
mṛdbhasmago sakṛtpiṇḍaṃ tathā vālukayā api vā
krīḍayā sāyutaṃ kalpaṃ bhavet so 'api gaṇeśvaraḥ
pāṃśunā krīḍamāno 'api liṅgaṃ kuryāttu yo naraḥ
sūkṣmenāpi prayogenāprameyaṃ puṇyasañcayam
hīnavaṃśeṣu ye jātāḥ pāpiṣṭā ye 'api mānavaḥ
paśupāśārṇavātīta varaprada namostu te
liṅgotpattiṃ mahāpuṇyaṃ yaḥ śṛṇuti naraḥ sadā
notpadyate sa saṃsāre sthānaṃ prāpnoti śāśvatam
oṃ namaḥ śivāya
alpavācolpavaktāraḥ śūrāḥ tyāgaviśāradāḥ
śaucācāreṇa sampannāḥ dayādākṣiṇyatatparāḥ
dambhamātsaryanirmuktā yathātathyaprabhāṣiṇaḥ
rudrātmāno rudaparā rudrāṃśā rudrabhāvanāḥ
ekāntabhaktimāsthāya guṇeṣveteṣu vartatāḥ
pūjanīyaḥ śivo nityaṃ parāparavibhūtaye
kiñcidvedamayaṃ pātraṃ kiñcitpātraṃ tapomayam
alpaṃ mahati vā puṇyaṃ tulyamāḍhyadaridrayoḥ
mṛṇmayaiṣṭaka śailaṃ vā yaśca kuryācchivālaye
mṛṇmayātkoṭiguṇitaṃ puṇyaṃ syādārubhiḥ kṛte
yatra tatra yataḥ kuryāt kartuḥ puṇyaṃ śileṣṭayoḥ
navaṃ purāṇe rahitaṃ tasmāt kuryācchivālayam
tatroṣitvā mahābhogaiḥ koṭikoṭyayutaṃ sukhī
nāhārapratijīrṇāyā vandhyāyāḥ kṣīṇavatsayoḥ
rogārtāyāḥ prasūtāyā gośca gomayamāharet
khasaṃsthaṅgomayaṃ grāhyaṃ sthāne vā patitaṃ śubhe
sa muniḥ sa mahāsādhuḥ sa yogī sa śivaṃ vrajet
kvacitkalaśavinyāsāṃ paṅkajairupaśobhitām
sudhāpraliptaṃ yaḥ kuryāt sarvayatnaiḥ śivālayam
tāvatpuṇyaṃ labhetso 'api yāvadāyatane kṛte
pratyantaṃ yaḥ prakurvīta śambhorāyatanaṃ budhaḥ
dhavalaṃ kṣīrasudhayā yathāśobhiśca varṇakaiḥ
kārayeccitraśāstrajñaiḥ yatnāccitraṃ śivālaye
rudrāvatārakrīḍādyaiḥ prayogairāgamoditaiḥ
yāvatsa rudrarūpāṇi surūpāṇi tu lepayat
atītaguṇasaṃghāya viśveśāya namostu te
pūjyānāmapi pūjyāya anāthāya namostu te
āyatanādhyāyaścaturthaḥ
oṃ namaḥ śivāya
triśūlavajraśaṃkhā ava śrīvatsasvastikādibhiḥ
divaseṣūttameṣu ca parvakāleṣu sarveṣu
aṣṭamyādiṣu sarveṣu puṣpapūrṇābhiparvaṇi
kuryānnīrājanī liṅgaṃ śivamuddiśya maṅgalaiḥ
snānaṃ śataguṇaṃ jñeyaṃ tarusnānānna saṃśayaḥ
kāpilyaṃ yaḥ pivecchūdraḥ śivasaṃskāravarjitaḥ
kṛṣṇāṣṭamyāṃ ghṛtasnānaṃ kṛtvā liṅge sakṛnnaraḥ
dadhnā tu snāpayelliṅgaṃ sakṛdbhaktyāpi yo naraḥ
madhunā snāpayelliṅgaṃ sakṛdbhaktyāpi yo naraḥ
candrāṃśunirmalaḥ śuddhaḥ śivavatsomamaṇḍalam
sarvakāmasu tṛptātmā vāruṇaṃ lokamāpnuyāt
karpūrāgarutoyena yo liṅgaṃ snāpayennaraḥ
snāpya liṅgaṃ sakṛddadhnā kṛṣṇāṣṭamyāmupoṣitaḥ
snāpya paścāt ghṛtasnānaṃ tata eva viśiṣyate
ekaviṃśaḥ kulopetaḥ krīḍate divi rudravat
ghṛtābhyaṅge ghṛtasnāne yatnālliṅgaṃ virukṣayet
sukhoṣṇenāmbhasā vāpi snāpayettadanantaram
arghyaṃ puṣpajalopetaṃ yaḥ śivāya nivedayet
āpaḥ kṣīrakuśāgrāṇi dadhisarpissataṇḍulam
dāraveṇārghapātreṇa datvārghyaṃ yatphalaṃ bhavet
rūpyapātreṇa vijñeyamarghaṃ śataguṇottaram
hemapātrāṇi yaddatvā puṇyaṃ sādvedapārage
śivāya raupyapātreṇa tasya puṇyaṃ tatodhikam
hemapātrāṇi yo bhaktyā śivāya vinivedayet
tayorapyadhikaṃ puṇyaṃ yaḥ śive bhāvanādhikaḥ
datvā śivāya cāmaraṃ maṇidaṇḍavibhūṣitam
ketakañcātimuktāñca kundayūthīmadantika
pratyekamuktaṃ puṣpāṇāṃ dānātsauvarṇikaṃ phalam
mālābhirvā sugandhībhiryo liṅgaṃ pūjayennaraḥ
bilvapatrairakhaṇḍaiśca sakṛlliṅgaṃ prapūjayet
kuśāpāmārgakumudavandhūkapuṣpajairapi
śobhitaṃ puṣpasragdāmairāpīṭhāntapralambibhiḥ
atyāścaryairmahāyānaiḥ divyapuṣpopaśobhitaiḥ
sarvaiḥ paricariḥ śrīmān āste śivapure sukhī
anekākārasaṃyuktaṃ yaḥ śive kusumaṃ gṛham
mālābhirveṣṭayedvediṃ liṅgaṃ sampūjya yatnataḥ
lakṣā prāṇyaṅganirmuktaṃ dhūpaṃ datvā sakṛcchive
priyaśca guggulordhūpa ājyayukto viśeṣataḥ
na meruṃ ghṛtasaṃyuktaṃ dagdhvā bilvamathāpi vā
yaḥ kuryāt sakṛdapyevaṃ puṇyantasyāvadhārayaḥ
sarvakāmasusampūrṇairmahāyānaiḥ sahasraśaḥ
āpūrayandiśaḥ sarvā āmodaiśca sugandhibhiḥ
trivṛcchuklaṃ supītaṃ vā padmasūtrādinirmitam
dhūpitāni śive dadyādakleśyāni navāni ca
haridrāṃ kṛsarāṃ śuklāṃ pāyasaṃ yāvasaṃ tathā
guḍakhāṇḍājyasammiśram evamannaṃ prakalpayet
bharjitāyānnakādīnāṃ bhakṣyārdhaṃ phalamiṣyate
śivāya tatphalaṃ tasya bhakṣāṇāṃ vinivedayet
dīpavṛkṣaṃ susamṛddhaṃ śarvasyāyatanaṃ śubham
dīpavṛkṣaṃ susamṛddhaṃ śobhayucca śivālaye
lalāṭe vāpi hastābhyāmudyuktaśca tathorasi
sa chatreṇa vimānena dhāryamāṇena śobhinā
yadi vā rājyamākāṃkṣetsadeho kālaparyayāt
ghaṇṭākarṇagaṇaḥ śrīmān śivasyātīvavallabhaḥ
lambakaiḥ sūtradāmaiśca ghaṇṭārutavibhūṣitam
maṇḍapaṃ mauktikaṃ śrīmān tasya puṇyaphalaṃ śṛṇu
ābhūtasampravaṃ yāvadāste śivapure sukhī
śarvasyāyatane kuryādvastrapūtāṃ suśobhanām
ya kuryādiṣṭakāgāraṃ rudrāgneratiśobhanam
tatra kṛtvāgnikāryañca śivaloke mahīyate
kṛtvā śivālayaṃ prānte daśamaṇḍalakāni tu
rudramātṛgaṇādīnāṃ divyatīnāñca sarvataḥ
chatracāmaradhūpādyairyaṣṭidīpādibhistathā
puṣpākṣatādisammiśraṃ sarvadikṣu baliṃ kṣipet
etena mātaro raudrā śivomāṣaṇmukhādayaḥ
śāntiṃ kurvanti suprītā raudreṇa balinā tadā
śivamālyopanayanaṃ pūjānteṣu pramārjanam
śivamaṇḍalakaṃ vṛttaṃ hastamātraṃ śivāgrataḥ
avakīryākṣataiḥ puṣpaiḥ patrairvāpyatha komalaiḥ
surupaḥ subhagaḥ śrīmān tejasvī vedavitsukhī
evaṃ svastikapadmādyairbhūriśobhāṃ śivālaye
kṛtvā varṇakapiṣṭādyaiḥ tasmācchataguṇaṃ labhet
yadiṣṭañca viśiṣṭañca nyāyena ca yadātam
karma śāṭhyena yaḥ kuryānmūlenāpi śivālaye
liṅgāddhastaśatasāgraṃ śivakṣetraṃ samantataḥ
pāpācāro 'api yastasminnāyācchivapuraṃ smṛtam
nyāyārjitāni dravyāṇi arpayanti svaśaktitaḥ
śivaṃ śivakaraṃ śāntaṃ śivātmānaṃ śivottamam
śāśvataṃ śobhanaṃ śuddhaṃ śāsakaṃ doṣaśoṣakam
viśvaṃ viśveśvaraṃ devaṃ śaṅkaraṃ praṇamāmyaham
asthānaṃ sthānasatsthānaṃ saṃsthitaṃ sthitibhāvanam
sthiramātmathayogasthaṃ sthāṇvīśaṃ praṇamāmyaham
īśānaṃ sarvabhūtānāmīśānaṃ praṇamāmyaham
oṃ namaḥ śivāya
ataḥ paramidaṃ guhyaṃ rudrādgītaṃ mahodayam
sarvaśāntyādhikārākhyaṃ dharmaṃ vakṣyāmi śāśvatam
śaśāṅkārdhadharastryakṣo nāgayajñopavītakaḥ
caturmukhaścaturbāhuḥ sitabhasmāvaguṇṭhitaḥ
trailokye namitaḥ śrīmān śāntimāsśu karotu me
sarvāvayavapūrṇena gātreṇa tanumadhyamā
śāntiṃ karotu me prītā bhaktānāṃ bhaktavatsalā
kṛttikomāgnirudrāṃśasamudbhūtaḥ surārcakaḥ
karotu me mahāśāntiṃ karmasiddhiñca sarvadā
karotu me mahāśāntiṃ mahākālo mahābalaḥ
pītavastraparīdhānā kanyārūpāsvalaṅkṛtā
gaṇamātāmbikāstryakṣā puṇyā gaurīśvareśvarī
śāntiṃ karotu me mātā siddhaścānu prayacchatu
niemāṃsena śarīreṇa snāyvasthisunibandhanaḥ
rudrātmajo mahāvīro rudraikāhitamānasaḥ
sopi me śāntabhāvena śāntimāśu prayacchatu
caṇḍapāpāpaharaṇo brahmahatyādiśodhanaḥ
śaṅkhakundendutulyābhaḥ kaṇṭhe marakataprabhaḥ
akṣamālī śivāgrasthaḥ svayaṃ jñānavyavasthitaḥ
īśaṃ vahati pṛṣṭhena tasmāddharmo jagatprabhuḥ
vṛṣo vṛṣavaraḥ śrīmān karotu mama śāntikam
sarvadā śāntabhāvena viṣṇuḥ śāntiṃ karotu me
ārhantaḥ śāntacetaṣka viśvātman viśvayātitaḥ
digvāsāmalapaṅkaśca saumyacittasamāhitaḥ
saṃvartalocanaḥ śāntaḥ śivajñānaikacintakaḥ
śāntiṃ karotu me śāntaḥ śivayogena bhāvitaḥ
jitendriyaḥ samādhisthaḥ pātracīvarabheṣitaḥ
varadābhayapāṇiśca jñānadhyānarataḥ sadā
yogadṛṣṭiḥ sadāyuktaḥ śivajñānena bhāvitaḥ
pītavarṇena dehena hāreṇa suvicitriṇā
dharitrī lokamātā ca nityaṃ rakṣāṃ karotu me
prīṇayanti sadā devān viprāṃścaiva viśeṣataḥ
nityaṃ tu devatātmānaḥ kurvantu mama śāntikam
brahmāṇi saumyavadanā śivapūjāparāyaṇā
śāntiṃ karotu me prītā brahmaśabdena sarvadā
himaśailanibhā devi mahāvṛṣabhavāhanā
ārtiharatu me prītā rudrāṇī nityamujvalā
śaṃkhacakragadāhastā śyāmā pitāmbaraprīyā
śivārcanaratā nityaṃ śivaikāhitamānasā
netrāṇāntu sahasreṇa bhūṣitā kanakaprabhā
aindrī devī sadākālaṃ śāntimāśu karotu me
tarjayanī sadā vighān arcayantī sadā śivam
vārāhī varadā devī kṣamārogyaṃ dadātu me
nānānāgopavītāṅgī pretasthānanivāsinī
cāmuṇḍā caṇḍarūpeṇa mahārakṣāṃ karotu me
apītena śarīreṇa nānābharaṇabhūṣitā
ātarjayantī vighnāni jvalatkhaṭvāṅgadhāriṇī
rudrabhaktyā mahāvīryā rudrārcā hitamānasā
saumyāścaiva tu ye kecit sthāṇusthānanivāsinaḥ
siddhimāśu prayacchantu bhayebhyaḥ pāntu māṃ sadā
suśvetākṣāḥ śvetanibhāstathā vai śvetalohitāḥ
kṛṣṇaḥ kṛṣṇanibhāḥ kruddhāstathā vai kṛṣṇalohitāḥ
rudraikāhitacetaskā śāntiṃ kurvantu me sadā
nairṛtyāntu gaṇāḥ krūrā rākṣasā mṛtyurūpiṇaḥ
sunīlākṣā nīlanibhāḥ tathā vai nīlalohitāḥ
vāruṇyāṃ vai gaṇā ye tu satataṃ pāśapāṇayaḥ
suśyāmākṣāḥ śyāmanibhāsthā vai śyāmalohitāḥ
supītākṣāḥ pītanibhāstathā vai pītalohitāḥ
śivabhaktiparāḥ sarve kṣamaṃ kurvantu me sadā
uttarasyāṃ gaṇā ye tu satataṃ nidhipāṇayaḥ
śavalākṣā śavalanibhāstathā vai śavalalohitāḥ
śivapūjāsamudyuktā kṣemaṃ kurvantu me sadā
sūkṣmāḥ susūkṣmasadṛśāstathā vai sūkṣmalohitāḥ
śivapūjāsamudyuktā kṣemaṃ kurvantu me sadā
adhobhāge gaṇā ye tu satataṃ śūlapāṇayaḥ
dhūmrā dhūmranibhāssarve tathā vai dhūmralohitāḥ
śivaṃ kurvantu me nityamalakṣmīmalanāśanam
ardhabhāgebhāge gaṇā ye tu mahābalaprarākramāḥ
susūkṣmākṣāḥ sūkṣmanibhāstathā vai sūkṣmalohitāḥ
śivapūjāsamādyukta aśubhaṃ nāśayantu me
śivaṃ sampūjya yatnena balinteṣāṃ vinikṣipet
netrāṇāntu sahasreṇa śobhatena virājate
śivapraṇāmaparamaḥ karotu mama śāntikam
nānādevasamākīrṇā ratnajvālāsamujjvalā
bhūmikṛddehināṃ devo jvalanaḥ pāpanāśanaḥ

nityaṃ praṇamyate bhaktyā hṛṣṭenātyantacetasā
sa śivaḥ śāśvato devo goṣu mārīṃ vyapohatu
ghaṇṭākarṇagaṇo devo harājñānuvidhāyakaḥ
śivatejo 'anubhāvena goṣu mārīṃ vyapohatu
śivāya daivadevāya mahādevāya bhāvine
rudrāya sthāṇave nityaṃ harāyograya te namaḥ
ya idaṃ paṭhate goṣu rogārtāsu viśeṣataḥ
gosāvitrīṃ sadā kālaṃ gavāṃ śāntirbhaviṣyati
prātarutthāya satataṃ gosāvitrīmimāṃ paṭhet
gavāmutsavakāleṣu prasthāne vā samāgame
āyuṣmān balavān śrīmān gomānarthapatirbhavet
prabhūtatoyeṣu bṛhattṛṇeṣu sapuṅgavānityamarogavatsāḥ
yo 'alaṅkṛtaṃ śivāyāśvamuttamaṃ vinivedayet
suvinītāṃ striyaṃ dāsīṃ bhṛtyaṃ vāpi nivedayet
nivedayati yo nāgān bhaktyā bhadramalaṅkṛtān
prakīḍati mahāyānaiḥ śatasaṃkhyairanekaśaḥ
bhavedindrasamaḥ śrīmān rūpavīryasamanvitaḥ
śivāya yaḥ phalopetāṃ sarvasasyaprarohaṇīm
yāvaddaṇḍā bhaved bhūmirmīyamānā samantataḥ
evaṃ sarvatra vijñeyaṃ phalaṃ daṇḍapramāṇataḥ
puṇyaṃ nagaradānasya grāmadānācchatādhikam
deśadānena cānantaṃ na śakyaṃ tatprakīrtitum
ratnānnapānagotrājārdya sarvabhūmeḥ prajāyate
yo 'anyadattāṃ svadattāṃ vā hared bhūmiṃ narādhamaḥ
prapatyakṣayaṃ kālaṃ narakeṣu krameṇa saḥ
sarvaṃ vā bhūmiharaṇe hṛtameva na saṃśayaḥ
devasvāni harantīha narāṃ narakanirbhayāḥ
yatpradānātvaset svargaṃ yāvatkālasya saṃkhyatā
tasmācchataguṇaṃ kālaṃ taddhṛtvā narake vaset
viṃśatyaṣṭau ca yā koṭyo narakāṇāṃ sudāruṇāḥ
kalpānte ca samuttīrṇā vṛkṣaphīṭādiyiniṣu
bhavanti suciraṃ kālaṃ kṣutpipāsādipīḍitāḥ
evaṃ bhramantaḥ suciram īśvarājñāpracoditāḥ
tato nirdhūtapāpāste narāḥ syuḥ śivatejasā
divaṃ prayānti santyajya śarīraṃ pātanātmakam
iti śivadharme śivadānaphalādhyāyo 'aṣṭamaḥ
oṃ namaḥ śivāya
brahmaṇā viṣṇunā devyā skandenendreṇa yamena ca
dharmaśrīśukranakṣatravilohitaśanaiścaraiḥ
viśvāmitravasiṣṭhātribṛhaspatibudhādibhiḥ
bhṛṅgimātṛmahākālacaṇḍeśvaragaṇādhipaiḥ
vṛṣavāsukikarkoṭakulikānantatakṣakaiḥ
śivā gatiryathā prāptā sarvagatyatiśāyinī
mahādevaprasādena tathā vidhipraṃ śṛṇu
munayo mokṣamāpanno mātaro mātṛtāṃ gatāḥ
oṃ namaḥ śivāya
abdamekatra bhuñjīta śivārcanarataḥ śuciḥ
yatpuṇyamakṣayaṃ proktaṃ satataṃ satrayājinām
satyavādiṣu yat puṇyaṃ yat puṇyaṃ tīrthagāminām
agnihotṛṣu yat puṇyaṃ yat puṇyaṃ yajñayājinām
sopavasaḥ sugandhāṅgaḥ śayīta priyayā saha
bhavabhaktiḥ sa pūtātmā yadyapi syātpāpakṛt
śivaloke vasennityaṃ śivavat saṃvṛto gaṇaiḥ
saṃvatsarānte viprendrān śivabhaktān samāhitān
bhojayitvā tato brūyāt prīyatāṃ bhagavān śivaḥ
na ca mānuṣyakaṃ lokamadhruvaṃ prāpnuyānnaraḥ
prāśanād bilvapatrāṇām anantaṃ phalamāpnuyāt
kṛtvā snānaṃ mahāpūjāṃ śive yatnāt prakalpayet
bhojayīta dvijānaṣṭau śivabhaktān sadakṣiṇān
śivadharmādbhavejjñānaṃ śivadharmaḥ śivārcanāt
ityeṣa vaḥ samākhyātaḥ saṃsārārṇavavartinām
mokṣapāyaḥ śivadharmaḥ śivāñcamaniveśinām
śeṣaṃ kṛtvā yathoddiṣṭaṃ pūrvoktaṃ tu phalaṃ labhet
tvagbhiśca kṣīravṛkṣāṇāṃ dhātrīgandhādibhistathā
dadyāt gomithunaṃ bhaktyā tāmrābhaṃ parmeṣṭhine
śeṣamanyadyathoddiṣṭaṃ kṛtvāpnoti mahat phalam
dadyād gomithunaṃ cātra pāṭalaṃ samalaṅkṛtam
śivāyātisurūpañca śeṣaṃ pūrvavadācaret
puṣparāgaprabhiaryānair divyāśvarathasaṃjñayā
gacchecchivapuraṃ ramyaṃ duṣprāpyam akṛtātmabhiḥ
goṣṭhaśāyī śivādhyāyī niśāyām ekavastradhṛk
niyamañca yathoddiṣṭaṃ sāmānyaṃ sarvadācaret
vaiśākhe paurṇamāsyāṃ yaḥ kuryāt snānaṃ ghṛtādibhiḥ
śivāyālaṅkṛta śvetaṃ dadyāt gomithunaṃ śubham
haṃsakundenduvarṇābhairmahāyānairalaṃkṛtam
suśvetavṛṣasaṃyuktaiḥ sa yātīśvaramandiram
sarvātiśayarūpābhiḥ strībhiḥ saṃparivāritaḥ
śālyannaṃ payasā dhautam ājyakṣīreṇa saṃyutam
vīrāsanī niśārddhaṃ syād divā grāmannagacchati
hitakārī gavāṃ nityamahaṅkāravivarjitaḥ
paurṇamāsyāṃ ca pūrvoktaṃ kuryāt snānādikaṃ vidhim
deyaṃ gomithunaṃ cātra dhūmavarṇamalaṅkṛtam
mahāsiṃhanibaddhaiśca krīḍate kālamakṣayam
bhūrikhaṇḍājyasammiśrān śaktūn dadyāt sagorasān
dadyād gomithunaṃ gauraṃ śivāyālaṅkṛtaṃ śubham
sāmānyañca vidhiṃ kuryāt sarvaṃ vā prākpracoditam
nīlaskandhaṃ vṛṣaṅgāñca śivāya vinivedayet
ghṛtāśanaṃ prabhuñjānaḥ prasannātmā jitendriyaḥ
vimucya dadyādrudrāya gomekāṃ samalaṅkṛtām
vidhiśeṣañca pūrvoktaṃ yaḥ kartā samupācaret
prāṇānte ca paraṃ sthānaṃ prayāti śivavadvaśī
kṣīrodanaṃ prabhuñjīta satyavādī jitendriyaḥ
pūrvoktaṃ ca vidhiṃ kṛtvā śivatulyaguṇo bhavet
dadyādgomithunaṃ divyaṃ pāṇḍuraṃ samalaṅkṛtam
śeṣaṃ śivāya pūrvoktaṃ vidhinā samupakrame
naktamabdaṃ prakurvīta haviṣo brahmacāriṇī
bhojayecchivabhaktāṃśca dīnānāthaṃśca tarpayet
śaktyā ca dakṣiṇāndadyācchivamantraiḥ kṣamāpayet
tāmrakāṃsyādipātraṃ vā sitabhasmāvaguṇṭhitam
dhvajaśaṃkhādivibhavaiḥ śivasyāyatanaṃ nayet
samāpya tadvrataṃ puṇyaṃ śṛṇuyācca phalaṃ labhet
tataḥ karmāvaśeṣeṇa sarvakāmasamanvitā
brahmalokāt paribhraṣṭā śrīvatsukhavatī bhavet
prājāpatyaṃ samāsādya lokālokanamaskṛtam
yakṣalokaminaṃ prāptā yathā kāmaṃ pramodate
yakṣalokāt paribhraṣṭā krīḍate merumūrdhanī
sthānāni lokapālānāṃ kramādāgatya modate
yuvānaṃ rūpasampannamaśeṣapṛthivīpatim
dharmārthakāmamokṣajñam imamāsādya modate
tataḥ sā dharmaśeṣeṇa bhavatīśvarabhāvitā
śubhācca punarapyevaṃ yoniṃ yāti sahasraśaḥ
snādyantadvrataṃ prāpya pūrvoktāṃśca guṇāṃllabhet
kāruṇyāt sarvanārīṇānnarāṇāñca viśeṣataḥ
umādevīpriyārthantu tuṣṭena paramārthataḥ
nārī caivopavāsena abda kṛṣṇācaturdaśīm
yā kārttikaṃ śubhaṃ māsamekabhaktena yāpayet
ekabhaktopavāsasya phalañca sadṛśaṃ viduḥ
vṛṣayuktairmahāyānair apsarogaṇasaṃyutaiḥ
pauṣamāse śubhe caiva śūlaṃ kṛtvā pinākine
tāmarakāṃsyādipātraṃ vā kṛtvā dadyāt pinākine
rathañcāśvayutaṃ māghe dīpamālyopaśobhitam
anubhūyātulān bhogān aśeṣapurasambhavān
phālgune pratimāṃ pauṣṭīṃ kṛtvā agurusamanvitām
sarvāmaranikāyeṣu prāpya bhogān sudurlabhān
caitrebhayaṃ kumārañca kṛtvā paiṣṭamalaṅkṛtam
kailāsavratamityevaṃ vaiśākhyaṃ yā samācaret
liṅgaṃ piṣṭamayaṃ kṛtvā jyeṣṭhamāse prayojayet
upaśobhāviśeṣaiśca tatra jāgaramācaret
bhuktvā bhogān yathākāmān aśeṣapurasambhavān
krīḍāviraktabhāvaiśca modate śaṅkarājñayā
gṛhaṃ piṣṭamayaṃ kuryādāṣāḍhe pitribhūṣaṇam
sarvaratnādigośvādvairdāsīśayādyalaṅkṛtam
etaiḥ piṣṭamayaiḥ sarvaiḥ pradīpādyupaśobhitam
sarvabhakṣyasamākīrṇaṃ gandhamālyairalaṅkṛtam
āṣāḍhe paurṇamāsyāṃ vā gṛhaṃ sthāpya śivāgrataḥ
prāpya taṃ sarvabhogāḍhyaṃ saptabhaumagṛhaṃ labhet
sarvadhātukṣamākīrṇaṃ vicitradhvajaśobhitam
svacchendranīlasaṅkāśaiyānairapratimaiḥ śubhaiḥ
samprāpte 'asmin kramālloke rājānaṃ patimāpnuyāt
daityagandharvabhūtāṃśca siddhayakṣagaṇāstathā
vidyādharāpsaronāgān ṛṣīṃśca sarvadevatāḥ
śālipiṣṭamayān kṛtvā sarvarūpān vicakṣaṇaḥ
evaṃ sarveṣu deveṣu kuryādastraṃ yathākramam
pūjayet sarvadevāṃśca daśadikṣu baliṃ haret
sarvāgameṣu yatpuṇyaṃ munibhiḥ kathitaṃ purā
gītanṛtyādivādyaiśca apsarobhiśca śobhitaiḥ
svacchandā sarvagā bhūtvā prayātīśvaramandiram
surūpā subhagā nityaṃ bhavatīśvarabhāvitā
mṛṇmayaṃ dārujaṃ śailamaiṣṭakaṃ vā sukalpitam
śivāya tadgṛhaṃ datvā sarvakāmamavāpnuyāt
prabhāte snāpanaṃ kṛtvā tadbhaktānāñca bhojanam
dīnānāthakṛpaṇāṃśca yathāśaktisadakṣiṇān
rathaṃ śobhāsamāyuktaṃ śivāya vinivedayet
kalpakoṭyayutaṃ sāgraṃ tasyānte sa mahīpatiḥ
pañcaśūlīsamāyuktaṃ mṛdvīṃ khaṭvāmalaṅkṛtām
śivavedisamāyuktāṃ pauṣṭīṃ śayyānnivedayet
daśa gāvaḥ savṛṣabhā vṛṣabhaikādaśī smṛtā
candraikādaśatulyātmā balabhogādibhirguṇaiḥ
gatvā śivapuraṃ divyamaśeṣādhipatirbhavet
sarvagaḥ paripūrṇāśca śivavatpariravyayaḥ
dadyādubhayamukhīṃ gāṃ śivāyātīvaśobhanām
vānaprastho gṛhastho vā yaścānye vā gṛhāśramī
svāśramāduttare kuryāt puṣpārāmaṃ suśobhanam
agnyagārasamāyuktam aiśānyāmīśvarālayam
śivāddakṣiṇataḥ kuryāt tadbhaktābhyāgatālayam
kāryātipātasaṃrodham ādyādinakakādiṣu
manasā pūjayed bhaktyā dehānte cātyaye 'api vā
śivārcāgnipato nityaṃ tadbhaktānāśca bhojanam
devāgnyatithibhaikṣyārthaṃ pacennaivātmakāraṇam
ātmārthaṃ yaḥ pacenmohān narakāryaṃ sa jīvati
svargārthaṃ jīvitanteṣāṃ narakārthaviparyaye
yaḥ śivabrahmacārī syāt sa śivārcāgnitatparaḥ
śivavaikhānaso jñeyaḥ śivārcāgniparo bhavet
rudrākṣakaṅkaṇo haste jaṭiakā caiva mastake
liṅgaṃ śivāśramasthānaṃ bhasmanā ca tripuṇḍrakam
haste mūrdhnodvyupavīte vā rudrākṣandhārayedvratī
rudrabhaktaiḥ śivasyaikā dhāryā rudrajaṭā sadā
tasmād vāruṇamutsṛjya snānamāgneyam ācaret
uktaṃ devamunirdevaiḥ snāto devaḥ svayaṃ śivaḥ
bhūtiśāsanasaṃyogāt sa pūjyo rājapātravat
śāsanaṃ pūjayet tasmād avicāraṃ śivājñayā
śivājñāpūjanāttasmācchivo bhavati pūjitaḥ
udakumbhasahasreṇa mṛdāḍhakaśatena yaḥ
api varṣasahasreṇa bhāvaduṣṭo na śuddhyati
bhāvaśuddhaścarecchaucaṃ vastrapūtaṃ jalaṃ pivet
jaṭākalāpadhārī syāt pakṣārdhād vapanaṃ bhavet
yadeva puṇyaṃ dīkṣāyāṃ tatpuṇyaṃ vapane punaḥ
jaṭī muṇdī śikhī vāpi bhikṣāśī vigataspṛhaḥ
maunī bhūtānukampī ca yatiḥ śāntamatiścaret
upavāsātparaṃ bhaikṣam ekānnaṅgṛhiṇāmmalam
sarvasaṅgavinirmuktaḥ śuddhaḥ śivaparāyaṇaḥ
yo 'apīha vapanaṅkṛtvā yogīndrānucaro bhavet
vasedāyatane nityaṃ sagaṇaḥ śivadhārmikaḥ
puṣpārāmaikakarmātmā puṣpavāṭīkriyāparaḥ
trisnānapūjāsaṃyuktaḥ kaupīnācchāditaḥ sadā
sarva evāśramā jñeyāḥ śivabhaktāḥ śivāśramāḥ
mahāvratāṣṭakaṃ dhāryam īśenoktaṃ śivārthibhiḥ
śive bhaktiḥ sadā kāryā tadvacca śivayogiṣu
yat kṛtaṃ śivabhaktānāṃ tatkṛtaṃ hi śive bhavet
arcayitvā yathānyāyaṃ gāṇapatyaṃ labhennaraḥ
śivāśramaṃ gataṃ bhaktyā śivabhaktaṃ prapūjayet
prāptāya śivabhaktāya yo dadyāt svayamāsanam
śivabhaktaṃ gṛhe dṛṣṭvā tuṣṭiṃ vrajati yo naraḥ
śivabhaktāya viprāya pādau prakṣālya yatnataḥ
śivabhaktaṃ dvijaṃ prāptaṃ madhuparkādyanukramāt
śrāntasaṃvāhanaṃ kṛtvā somalokamavāpnuyāt
janantu śītalaṃ datvā sarvakāmamavāpnuyāt
mṛduśayyāpradānena siṃhāsanapatirbhavet
rogārtasya śivaṅkṛtvā bhaiṣajyādyaiḥ prayatnataḥ
kālātpunarihāyataḥ samastajñānapāragaḥ
sarūpaḥ subhagaḥ śrīmān nīrujaḥ suprajaḥ sukhī
śaraṇāgatamudvignam āghrātaṃ riputaskaraiḥ
samuddhṛtya yathāśakti sarvakāmamavāpnuyāt
patite śastrasaṅkīrṇe svacaṇḍālādipakṣiṇām
tasmādvai sarvabhūteṣu kāruṇyāddattamakṣayam
idaṃ vaḥ svargasopānam alaṃ yat priyaṃ vacaḥ
smpṛcchedāgataṃ bhaktyā cāyāte svāgataṃ vdet
śivaṃ bhavatu vo nityam aśeṣārthaprasādhakam
vācā yo 'avikṛtaḥ svasthaḥ kṣānitirekhā sunirmalā
sarveṣāmeva tīrthānāṃ tīrthaṃ jñānasya pāragaḥ
jñānatīrthāt paraṃ tīrthaṃ ye snātāḥ kṣāntivāriṇā
sa śūraḥ sāttviko vidvān sa tapasvī jitendriyaḥ
yenāśu kṣāntiśastreṇa krodhaśatrurvivarjitaḥ
nirmalaiḥ kṣāntisalilaiḥ samāpyāyanaśītalaiḥ
sarvaśāstrāṇi vedāśca yajñāścaiṣāntu yatphalām
ye na hiṃsanti bhūtāni teṣāṃ puṇyantato 'adhikam
carettapāṃsi sarvāṇi na tattulyam ahiṃsayā
ahiṃseyaṃ paro dharmaḥ śaktānāṃ parikīrtitam
aśaktānāmayandharmo dānayajñādipūrvakaḥ
nātaḥ paraṃ prapaśyāmaḥ kvacidapyāgame vayam
sarvabhūtābhayandatvā yaścarecchāntamānasaḥ
te bhuñjate duḥkhahīnāḥ parāṃ śāntimanuttamām
pāpā ramanti pāpeṣu atikrūrā narādhamāḥ
svadeśe yo nṛpaḥ kuryāt prāṇidhātanivāraṇam
yastu svārthaṃ parārthaṃ vā samastendriyasaṃyamaḥ
nākārye sampravarteta tamāhuḥ śāntalakṣaṇam
yaḥ praśāntendriyagrāmaḥ sadācandrāṃśunirmalaḥ
sa prāpnoti paraṃ sthānaṃ śāntaḥ śāntena cetasā
utpannārthena yastoṣaḥ santoṣaḥ sa prakīrtitaḥ
atītānāgatārtheṣu vartamāne nu jīvati
nānusmaratyatītārthaṃ santoṣo 'ayamudāhṛtaḥ
yena tṛṣṇāpadantyaktvā santoṣaḥ samyagāśritaḥ
devāsuramanuṣyāṇāṃ kṛtaḥ svapne 'api tatsukham
svānubhūtaṃ svadṛṣṭañca yo dṛṣṭārthanna gūhati
parapīḍāvinirmuktaṃ yāvat syadvacanaṃ śivam
parasvānāmanādānam etadsteyalakṣaṇam
brahmacarye sthitaṃ jñānaṃ brahmacarye sthitā kṣamā
ekarātroṣitasyāpi yā gatirbrahmacāriṇaḥ
gṛhibhiḥ sā na samprāptuṃ śakyā yajñaśatairapi
aho yativaraḥ śrīmān sva sva sevā striyāmṛtau
śivāgnigurukāryeṣu ya udyuktaḥ sadā naraḥ
eṣa darmadrumaḥ śrīmān aṣṭaśākhaḥ śivodbhavaḥ
na śakto vistarān vaktum upaśākhāprabhedataḥ
oṃ namaḥ śivāya
atha saṃkṣepato vakṣye śivabhaktānukampayā
ādyaśākhopaśākhārthaṃ śivabhaktisamudbhavam
tadā tat pūjya yo gacchet sa rudro nātr saṃśayaḥ
snānaivedyavastrādyan nādadīta priyāṇyapi
catuṣpathaṃ nadīṃ śailaṃ śivayoginameva ca
avatīrya namaskuryād dṛṣṭvā liṅgaṃ dṛdhavrataḥ
hastiyānāśvayānebhyaḥ śivayogitapasvinam
mṛgāśvoṣṭrapayo matsyamāṃsalaśunagṛñjanam
sarvadoṣāśrayaṃ madyamatidūrād vivarjayet
garbhādijanmasaṃskāraśmaśānāntādibhojanam
rajasvalābhisaṃspṛṣṭaṃ śivabhakto vivarjayet
na gobrāhmaṇabhasmāgniliṅgacchāyāṃ padaiḥ spṛśet
na laṅghayeta nirmālyaṃ nāpsu tiṣṭeta nagnakaḥ
na tatra snānapūjādyaṃ pratigṛhṇāni śaṅkaraḥ
bhūpradeśaṃ yathā kaścit svadehamaladūṣitam
nāśrayeta tathā rudro na taṃ nirmālyadūṣitam
asaṃspṛśyo bhavet so 'api yathā svāṅgamalaspṛśī
tasmācca na spṛśelliṅgaṃ na te nirmālyadūṣitaḥ
yatra naivedyanirmālyaṃ saṅkīrṇaṃ kalaśādibhiḥ
snānantu kriyate bhaktyā na taṃ gṛhṇāti śaṅkaraḥ
mūrtayo yāḥ śivasyāṣṭau tāsu nindāṃ vivarjayet
śmaśrukarmākṣunābhyaṅgaprasthānāni vivarjayet
kuryād viśeṣataḥ pūjāṃ naktaṃ cātra samācaret
trikālamekakālaṃ vā nityaṃ snānaṃ samācaret
yo 'aśnīyādannamasnātaḥ pāpamadyāt sa kevalam
tasmāt snātvā prabhuñjīta bhasmanā salilena vā
prātaḥ snātvāmbhasā gacched bhasmanā vā śivaṃ padam
bhojanaṃ ca pradātavyaṃ śaktyā dānaśca kalpayet
jñānavairāgyasampannaṃ bhasmaniṣṭhaṃ jitendriyam
yadā svarāditattvānāṃ parijñānaṃ vivekataḥ
nāniṣṭaṃ dveṣṭi samprāptanna ceṣṭam abhinandati
praītitāpaviṣādānāṃ vairāgyamativartanam
jñānavairāgyasampannaḥ śivaśāntena cetasā
yuktaḥ ṣaḍaṅgayogena śivayogī prakīrtitaḥ
nānātattvajñakoṭibhyo viśiṣṭāḥ śivayoginaḥ
yogināṃ śatakoṭibhyaḥ paramaḥ śivayogirāṭ
yaścāgnirudrakālātmā dakṣiṇāhavanistathā
śive bhaktiḥ kṣamā satyam aśeṣendriyasaṃyamaḥ
śivayogī śivajñānī śivadharmarataśca yaḥ
śivam uddiśya dātavyam anapekṣya gṛṇāśrayam
vittamīśvarabhaktena śivadānasya lakṣaṇam
samparkādarthalābhādvā pāpī rudraṃ prapūjayet
aurṇaṃ prāvaraṇaṃ datvā vikīrṇaṃ śivayogine
mṛgadvīpyādikaṃ dharma tilapiṣṭavimarditam
yāvattadromasaṃkhyānaṃ mṛgadvīpyādicarmaṇi
trāṇārthaṃ sarvajantūnāṃ so 'abdalakṣaṃ vaseddivi
triveṣṭāmbhaḥ samāyuktaḥ yaḥ pradadyātkamaṇḍalum
mahāsarāṃsi yatkṛtvā puṇyaṃ tatsakalaṃ labhet
mṛdamānīya saślakṣṇaṃ prāṇidoṣavivarjitām
bhṛdbhasmādhārapātrāṇi vaṃśālābukṛtāni ca
mṛṇmayālābukādyādyaṃ dambhṛtaṃ bhaikṣabhājanam
śivabhaktāya yo dadyādupānatkāṣṭhapāduke
kālmāṣadaṇḍaṃ yo dadyādannaṃ vā śivayogine
balāvaṣṭambhayuktābhyāṃ sa syāddaṇḍapraṇāyakaḥ
tālavaṃśātapatraṃ vā yo dadyācchivayogine
ekacchatrāṃ sa bhuñjīta pṛthivīñca narādhipaḥ
pātrāntaraviśeṣāṇi viśeṣācchivayogine
bhavet kṣitipatiḥ śrīmān kulādhāro guṇānvitaḥ
sarvajñānārthavaktā ca bṛhaspatisamo dhiyā
śivayogī śivajñānī śivadharmarataśca yaḥ
śivasamprāptitulyatvāt sarvatulyaguṇaḥ smṛtāḥ
yaḥ kuryāt kārayedvāpi vapanaṃ śivayoginām
tanmālāṃ yaḥ sadā haste rudraśca kramate kṣitau
rudrākṣadhāraṇāttasmādiha rudraḥ paratra ca
dhatte rudrākṣamālāṃ yaḥ śobhāyuktāṃ sadā kare
ya evaṃ cālayenmālāṃ tajjapyāñca na saṃśayaḥ
japaśca rudramantrāṇāṃ koṭikoṭiguṇottaram
mūrdhni hastopavītena kṛtvā rudrākṣamālikām
yaśca bhuñjīta bhasmāṅgī rudrairbhuktanna saṃśayaḥ
kuryādāvasathaṃ saumyaṃ suguptaṃ śivayoginām
śivālayakṛte yāvat tāvat puṇyaṃ samaṃ bhavet
vyākhyāne śivadharmasya kṛtvā maṇḍalakaṃ mahat
śobhitaṃ puṣpapatrādyaiḥ śivadharmāsanaṃ mahat
sarveṣāṃ śivabhaktānām ayaṃ vācyo vijānatā
śivāśrameṣu yo bhaktaḥ śivadharmaṃ prakīrtayet
śṛṇuyādvāpi labhate tatroktamakhilaṃ phalam
śrutvaivamakhilaṃ dharmam ākhyātaṃ brahmasūnunā
sārātsāraṃ samuddhṛtya candrātreyeṇa dhīmatā
uktañca dvādaśādhyāyaṃ dharmaśāstraṃ śivātmakam
tāvattsyāpi tatpuṇuam upadeśanna saṃśayaḥ
upadeśaṃ vinā yasmāddharmojñātuṃ na śakyate
na cākartumavijñāya tasmāttulyaṃ phalaṃ tayoḥ
upadeṣṭānumantā ca kartā kārayitā ca yaḥ
kṛtānupālakaścaiva pañca tulyaphalāḥ smṛtāḥ
yasmādāyatanaṃ kṣipraṃ nāśaṅgacchatyapālitam
bhūmiratnāśvanāgānāṃ gohiraṇyādivāsasām
dāturabhyādhikaṃ puṇyaṃ dattaṃ yaścānupālayet
tasmādupadiśeddharmaṃ svayaṃ vāpi samācaret
gatvā tu puruṣaḥ śrīmān prāpnoti śivamandiram
madhyamañ ca mahākālaṃ kedāraṃ bhairavan tathā
oṃ mahādevāya candramūrtaye namaḥ
oṃ paśupataye yajamānamūrtaye namaḥ
mūrtyayoṣṭau śivasyetāḥ pūrvādikramayogataḥ
āgneyāntāḥ prayojyāḥ syustābhyaḥ śivāṅgapūjanam
