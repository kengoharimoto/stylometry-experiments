namo'stu tasmai śakalendudhāriṇe phaṇīndraratnadyutikaṇṭharāgiṇe
śivamastu sarvajagatāṃ parahitaniratābhavantu bhūtaguṇāḥ
jñānaśaktidharaṃ śāntaṃ kumāraṃ śaṅkarātmajam
tenāsi nātha bhūtānāṃ sarveṣām anukampakaḥ
ataḥ sarvahitaṃ dharmaṃ saṃkṣepāt prabravīhi me
te ca śrutāstvayā sarve pṛcchāmi tvāmahantataḥ
vidyādānañca dānānāṃ sarveṣām uttamaṃ kila
kāni puṇyāni kṛtveha gṛhiṇaḥ svargiṇaḥ punaḥ
manuṣyalokasambhūtā yogaṃ vindanti śaṅkaram
etad yajñaratānāñ ca pradāne kīdṛśaṃ phalam
tatsādhanāḥ katividhā gatayaś ca tadātmikāḥ
kāni cihnāni jāyante bhuktaśeṣeṇa karmaṇā
saṃsārasāgarād ghorād dharmādharmormisaṅkulāt
iti sañcoditaḥ skandaḥ sarvapraśnārthabhāṣakaḥ
sarvajñaḥ paripūrṇatvād yathālokena hetunā
brūyād vākyaṃ śivaḥ śāntaḥ sarvadoṣavivarjitaḥ
tanniyogād ayaṃ lokaḥ śuciḥ syād dharmatatparaḥ
dharmaniṣṭhaḥ kṛto rājā dharmapādaikahrāsataḥ
yugatrayaṃ sa vijñeyas tasmād rājā caturyugaḥ
prajāsu dharmayuktāsu caturthāṃśaṃ bhajen nṛpaḥ
dharmaśīlanṛpe tasmāt prajāḥ syud dharmatatparāḥ
upāyena bhayāl lobhān mūrkhān sadānubodhayet
evaṃ yaḥ sarvayatnena rājānaṃ bodhayed budhaḥ
śivajñānātmahastena kas tena sadṛśaḥ pitā
svargāpavargasiddhyarthaṃ bhāṣitaṃ yat subhāṣitam
viduṣāṃ śāstrasambhāraḥ sadyogabhyāsavighnakṛt
gāvaḥ śivapurāt pañca kṣitiṃ prāptāḥ śivecchayā
gomātaraḥ sthitā bhūmau snānārcārthaṃ śivasya ca
ghṛtād amṛtam utpannam amarāṇām atipriyam
nivedayīta śarvāya bhaktāṃś ca ghṛtapācitān
ṣaḍaṅgavidhinā tasmān nṛpatiḥ pūjayec chivam
aśaṭhāḥ sarvabhṛtyāś ca devakārye niyojitāḥ
pūjitaṃ pūjyamānaṃ vā yaḥ paśyed bhaktitaḥ śivam
śivapuṇyam ahiṃsādyam aśeṣajanasambhavam
asaṃśayamatiḥ śrāddhas tatsamaṃ yaḥ puṇyam āpnuyāt
oṃ namaḥ śivāya
atha vidyāvidā deyaṃ vidyādānaṃ śivātmakam
adhyāpayan śanaiḥ śiṣyān śivabhaktān prabodhayet
śivavidyānusāreṇa vidyādānaṃ taducyate
saṃskṛtaiḥ prākṛtairvākyaiḥ yaḥ śiṣyān anurūpataḥ
deśabhāṣādyupāyaiśca bodhayet sa guruḥ smṛtaḥ
tathā vidyāpradānasya nāntaḥ sarvaguṇātmakaḥ
śivāṣṭaguṇamaiśvaryavidyādānaphalaṃ labhet
iha kīrtiṃ śivāṃ brāhmīṃ prajñāṃ vṛddhiṃ dhanaṃ sukham
yo'śuddhamātmanādhītya jñānamadhyāpayecchivam
śivajñānasya kālena vinaṣṭasya pramādataḥ
pramādādhītapāṭhasya nāśitasyālpabuddhibhiḥ
jñānāvalepamānāndhairācāryaiḥ śodhitasya ca
chandasātīva naṣṭasya śabdārtharahitasya ca
yaḥ karoti punaḥ samyak saṃskāraṃ pūrvavadguruḥ
śivatantrārthaviddhīmān sa vidyāparameśvaraḥ
na cāsya puṇyamāhātmyaṃ śakyaṃ vaktuṃ hi kena cit
yathā śivastathaivāyam asminnityaṃ śivaḥ sthitaḥ
aṣṭahastaṃ tadardhaṃ vā vṛttaṃ vā caturasrakam
tadbahirvarṇakaiścitrairnānā śobhāḥ prakalpayet
lambanaiḥ sūtradāmaiśca muktāhāraiśca kandukaiḥ
śobhitaṃ sitamṛtpātrair yavaśālyaṅkurodbhavaiḥ
vidyāsiṃhāsanaṃ śrīman nāgadantādinirmitam
sthāpya puṣpaṃ gṛhasyānte gandhapuṣpaiḥ prapūjya ca
rocanācandanādyaiśca puṣpairbhūpaiśca pūjayet
dadyāt pañcavidhaṃ dhūpaṃ samutthāpya punaḥ punaḥ
sampūjyaivaṃ śivajñānaṃ guruṃ sampūjayettataḥ
dvitīye tu puṣpagṛhe śrīmadāsanasaṃsthitam
śivavat pūjayed bhaktyā śivajñānaprakāśakam
tatheti samanujñātaḥ śāstraṃ puṇyāhamācaret
maṅgalaistūryaghoṣaśca kṛtasvastyayanaḥ śuciḥ
snātaḥ śuklāmbaradharaḥ stragvī gandhādyalaṅkṛtaḥ
hemāṅgulīyapāṇiśca kaṭakādyairalaṅkṛtaḥ
mātā pitṛsuhṛdbandhūn naktaṃ sarvaśca dakṣayet
kṣamāpya ca guruṃ paścād bhuñjīyād bhṛtyasaṃyutaḥ
datvā gaṇabaliṃ rātrau brahmacārī niśi svapet
apare'hani pūrvāhṇe vidyāmaṇḍalakaṃ budhaḥ
kṛtvā dvihastamātrantu nānāvarṇakaśobhitam
yavāṅkuraśarāvādyaiścitrapuṣpairalaṅkṛtam
evaṃ likhed vācayedvā kṛtvā pūjāṃ dine dine
caturasraiḥ saumyaśīrṣairnātisthūlainna vā kṛśaiḥ
mātrānusvārasaṃyogahrasvadīrghādilakṣitaiḥ
śivāgniguruvidyānāṃ sopacārāṃ prayatnavān
gītanṛtyairbahuvidhairākhyātaiśca śivātmakaiḥ
vedaghoṣaiśca vividhaiḥ prekṣaṇakaiśca śobhanaiḥ
kriḍāyantraprayogādyairityevaṃ kṣapayenniśām
śivavidyāvimānañca kuryāt prātaḥ suśobhanam
pañcāṇḍakaṃ tribhūmañca dāruvaṃśādinirmitam
pārśve carmasamāyuktaṃ dṛḍhasūtranibandhanam
sampūjya gandhapuṣpādyaiḥ pūrvoktavidhinā budhaḥ
samutkṣipyānayed bhaktyā tadvimānaṃ śivāśramam
maṅgalairvedaghoṣādyaiḥ sudhūpaiḥ kalaśaiḥ sitaiḥ
cāraṇairvandibhirvādyaiḥ strīsaṅgītairvibhūṣitam
cārucāmarahastābhiścitradaṇḍaiśca darpaṇaiḥ
athavā hastiyānasthaṃ kṛtvā pustakam ānayet
rājamārgeṇa mahatā nagarāntaṃ pradakṣiṇam
sarvāyatanapūjāñca svadhanaiḥ kārayennṛpaḥ
gandhapuṣpākṣatonmiśramudakañca tadānugam
agrepaścācca gaccheyuḥ sarvāyatanavāsinaḥ
purataḥ śivavidyāyāḥ śivamantrasmarāḥ śubhāḥ
ucchreyeyuḥ patākāśca janāste deśavāsinaḥ
gṛhadevāśca sampūjyā kāryaścāpyutsavo gṛhe
brāhmaṇān bhojayeyuśca gṛheṣu gṛhamedhinaḥ
annapānairjānapadāyātrāṃ kuryuḥ śivāśrame
acchedyāstaravaḥ kāryāḥ sarvāṃ hiṃsānnivārayet
bandhanasthāśca bhoktavyā baddhāḥ krośena śatravaḥ
akālakaumudīṃ kuryād divasadvayamīśvare
puṇyāhajayaśabdaiśca mahatā tumulena vā
sthāne susaṃskṛte ramye śivasya purataḥ śanaiḥ
śāntyarthamekamadhyāyaṃ gobrāhmaṇamahībhṛtām
rāṣṭriyanagarāṇāñca vācayed vācakottamaḥ
gāndharvavidvidagdhaśca śreṣṭhaḥ pustakavācakaḥ
śāntitoyena rājānaṃ samutthāya gurustataḥ
ācāryabhojanañcātra nṛpaḥ kuryāt sadakṣiṇam
ītayaśca praśāmyanti na ca mārī pravartate
śāmyanti sarvaghorāṇi praśamanti bhayāni ca
unmūlyante grahāḥ sarve praṇaśyanti ca śatravaḥ
vināyakāśca naśyanti saubhāgyaṃ paramaṃ bhavet
rājyavṛddhiśca vipulā nityañca vijayī nṛpaḥ
yo vā'nyaḥ puruṣaḥ kaścichraddhāvittānusārataḥ
aśāṭhyena acaretpuṇyam āḍhyatulyaphalaṃ labhet
yāvadakṣarasaṃkhyānaṃ śivajñānasya pustake
daśa pūrvān samuddhṛtya daśaṃ vaṃśyāśca paścimān
mātāpitṛdharmapatnīḥ svarge sthāpya śivaṃ vrajet
rājā śivapuraṅgacched vidyādānaprabhāvataḥ
paramāṣṭaguṇaiśvaryairupetaḥ sarvataḥ prabhuḥ
anenaiva vidhānena yaḥ śivaṃ jñānapustakam
kusumagṛhake sthāpya pūjayīta śivāśrame
āṣāḍhyāṃ pūrṇimāsyāṃ tu dīpamālāvibodhanaiḥ
vaiśākhyāñcāpi sampūjya vidyādānaphalaṃ labhet
yāvat tatpatrasaṃkhyānaṃ mañcaketīvaśobhanam
yatpatraṃ yantrasūtrādyaṃ maṣībhājanalekhanī
tāvadyugasahasrāṇi mahābhogānavāpnuyāt
śivajñānābhiyuktāya bhaktācchādan dadāti yaḥ
pādasnehāñjanābhyaṅgaṃ snānabhojanabheṣajam
yatimātropakaraṇaṃ maṭhasammārjanāñjanam
dīpāgnitoyapuṣpādyaṃ śivapustakavācanam
yaḥ kuryādetadakhilaṃ śivajñānaratātmakam
pañcānāṃ pañcavarṣāṇi vidyādānaphalaṃ labhet
vidyāmaṇḍalakaṃ vṛttaṃ yadi vā caturastrakam
dvihastamadhikaṃ vāpi kṛtvā sadgomayāmbhasā
sitaraktādibhiścūrṇaiḥ samantādupaśobhitam
vicitrakusumākīrṇaṃ pradīpākṣatasaṃyutam
sampūjyaivaṃ śivajñānaṃ śraddhayā vācayīta yaḥ
ādyantaṃ tatkramātsarvam ekarūpaṃ śanaiḥ śanaiḥ
tatsamāptau śivaṃ vidyām ācāryañca prapūjayet
śṛṇuyādekacittaśca tadarthañca vicārayet
yaḥ pradadyānmahīpūrṇāṃ sarvayatnaistu yatphalam
ślokārdhaṃ śivajñānasya pāṭhanat phalamāpnuyāt
yaḥ śrīparṇīsamudbhūtanimnakhātaṃ susañcayam
dadyāt sampuṭakaṃ kṛtvā carmaṇā vāpi nirmitam
suślakṣṇaphalakaṃ vāpi vidyādānaphalaṃ labhet
surūpaḥ daddvijo vāpi sarvavidyārthapāragaḥ
yaḥ sauvarṇaṃ susampūrṇaṃ sarvaratnopaśobhitam
sapidhānaṃ samañjūṣaṃ vidyākoṣasamāśrayam
kāṃsyārakūṭalauhairvā dāruvaṃśādinirmitam
sarvāśmakaṭakopetaṃ dṛdhasūtranibandhanam
kuryāttālakasaṃyuktaṃ vidyāratnakaraṇḍakam
evaṃ vittānusāreṇa kārayitvānurūpataḥ
kṛtvā vastravaraṃ divyamntaḥpuṣpaṃ gṛhānvitam
tasmin puṇyāhaśabdena vidyākoṣagṛhanyaset
bhavet suśobhanantasya cāṣṭahastapramāṇataḥ
śileṣṭakāmṛṇmayaṃ vā saddārukṛtameva vā
kapāṭārgalasaṃyuktaṃ prākārāsanaśobhitam
patākādhvajasaṃyuktaṃ sadvitānasuśobhanam
ānīya sthāpayedbhaktyā vidyāratnakaraṇḍakam
śivavidyāṃ sadākālaṃ trisandhyamupacārataḥ
dīnāndhakṛpaṇādyāṃśca dakṣayitvā kṣamāpayet
evaṃ yaḥ śivavidyāyāḥ kuryādāyatanaṃ śubham
kulaikaviṃśaduttārya sabhṛtyaparibāritaḥ
yastatra jñānamāsādya viṣayān viṣavat tyajet
sa prāpnoti parāṃ muktiṃ rāgī bhogānavāpnuyāt
yaḥ kārayenmahāramyaṃ suvedīsaṃsthitapuram
pañcabhaumaṃ tribhaumaṃ vā śivajñānaratātmanām
vāstuvidyāvibhaktañca prākāraparivāritam
prākārācca bahiḥ kuryāt sarvadikṣu samantataḥ
priyaṅguśirīṣāśokapuñjīvakarañjakaiḥ
punnāganāgabakulaiḥ pāṭalābilvacampakaiḥ
śvetamandāravijayairjātītagarakuñjakaiḥ
suvarṇaśvetayūthībhirbāṇāmrātakuruṇṭakaiḥ
karavīrakarṇikārair nānāvarṇairanekaśaḥ
sthānavinyāsaracitaissanmārgādhārasaṃyutaiḥ
kadalīstambhaṣaṇḍaiśca dāḍimādyairvirājitam
kapāṭagopuropetaṃ parikhāvaṃśasaṃyutam
tṛtīyāñca parādūrdhvaṃ vidyāvyākhyānamaṇḍapam
parārdhaṃ pañcamaṅkāryaṃ śivasya vaḍabhīgṛham
ṣaḍḍhastamaṣṭhapaṭṭaṃ vā cārupaṭṭavinirmitam
tatpratiṣṭhāntataḥ kuryāt pūjāsaṃskāralakṣaṇam
pañcabhiḥ pañcagavyena sthāpyaiśānyāṃ śivāṅkramāt
tūryaghoṣaistamānīya sthāpayet puṣpamaṇḍape
tataścandanapuṣpādyaiḥ pūjayitvā'dhivāsayet
japtvā pañcavidhaṃ stotraṃ śivaṃ vijñāpayet tataḥ
adyādhivāsanaṃ deva śvaḥ pratiṣṭhāvidhistava
pavitraiḥ sthāpayed vedyāṃ pādādārabhya pañcabhiḥ
śivaśca pūjayet puṣpaiḥ snānaṃ varjyaṃ sugandhibhiḥ
saśiṣyastaṃ praṇamyeśaṃ gururvyākhyāṃ pravartayet
dakṣiṇābhirvicitrābhirbhojanādyaiśca pūrvavat
nivedayet tataḥ sthānaṃ sarveṣāṃ śivayoginām
sagotraṃ triṃśakopetaḥ sabhṛtyaparivāritaḥ
śivavat krīḍate bhogairaṇimādiguṇānvitaḥ
sthapatiḥ sthāpakā vṛkṣā valīvardāḥ sukarmiṇaḥ
yānti rudrapuraṃ sarve tadadhyakṣāśca ye narāḥ
ye cāpi vṛttibhṛtakāḥ śivāyatanakarmaṇā
te'pi yānti mṛtāḥ svargā śivakarmānubhāvataḥ
śivadāsatvamāpannā naranārīnapuṃsakāḥ
dattāḥ krītāḥ praviṣṭāśca daṇḍopannā balāhṛtāḥ
vijñeyā rudragaṇikāḥ śivāyatanayogataḥ
sāpi yāti mṛtā svargaṃ māturevānubhāvataḥ
te'pi yānti śivaṃ lokaṃ kiṃ punastatsamāśritāḥ
atyalpamapi yaḥ kuryādatiramyaiḥ śivāśramam
kṛtvā sukuṭṭimāṃ bhūmim īśvarāyatanāgrataḥ
tatra maṇḍalakaṃ kuryāt suvṛttaṃ gomayāmbhasā
sūtreṇa sumitaṅkṛtvā nānāvarṇakaśobhitam
vicitrakusumākīrṇaṃ madhye paṅkajaśobhitam
pradīpākṣatasaṃyuktam udakaṃ bhrāmya pāṇinā
mūrdhnā praṇamya medinyāṃ śivāya vinivedayet
yaḥ kuryāt sakṛdapyevaṃ śivamaṇḍalakannaraḥ
jñānavān rūpavān bhogī supriyaḥ sudvijaḥ sukhī
vidyāmaṇḍalakaṃ kṛtvā vidyāvyākhyānamaṇḍape
tatrābhyarcya śivajñānaṃ tadvyākhyāṃ śṛṇuyāttataḥ
śivamaṇḍalakādyena vidhinātīva śobhanam
gurumaṇḍalakaṃ kṛtvā śivaloke mahīyate
śivavidyāgurūṇāñca kṛtvā maṇḍalakaṃ trayam
anena vidhinā jñānaṃ yaḥ śṛṇoti pravakti ca
tasmādārogyadānena dattaṃ syāttaccatuṣṭayam
apyekamārtaṃ vidvāṃsaṃ susthīkṛtya prayatnataḥ
jñānayogarataṃ śāntaṃ rogārtaṃ śivayoginam
brahmaviṣṇusurāḥ sarve vyādhayaḥ svajanā nṛpāḥ
ākāśasya yathā nāntaḥ surairapyupalabhyate
tadvādārogyadānasya nāntaḥ saṃvidyate tathā
puṇyamānena mahatā gatvā śivapurannaraḥ
ekaviṃśakulapetaḥ sabhṛtyaparivāritaḥ
jñānād viraktaḥ saṃsāre śivadhyānamupāśritaḥ
svadehaṃ tṛṇavat tyaktvā sarvaduḥkhāntamāpnuyāt
tasmād bhogāpavargārthaṃ rogārtaṃ samupācaret
viśeṣeṇa ca yogīndramātmanā ca dhanena ca
rogiṇo nodvijet prājñastanmalānāśca sarvataḥ
yo'nugṛhītamātmānaṃ manyamāno dine dine
upasarpati rogārtaṃ tīrṇastena bhavārṇavaḥ
jñānayogo nāma tṛtīyo'dhyāyaḥ
oṃ namaḥ śivāya
yaḥ sthānagātraśuśrūṣāṃ prakuryācchivayoginām
bhogaiḥ suvipulaiḥ sūkṣmaistāvatkālamanuttamaiḥ
sa dvitīye tṛtīye vā dehe yogamavāpnuyāt
śrutād vivardhate prajñā prajñātaścintayet punaḥ
ārambhakālaḥ śravaṇaṃ kriyākālaśca cintaram
śabdādiviṣayāmbhobhis tadvajjñānī ca lipyate
viśuddhaḥ sādhanaiḥ sūkṣmairdhyānayajñaḥ prasādhyate
jñānadhyānātmakaḥ sūkṣmaḥ śivayogamahāmakhaḥ
variṣṭaḥ sarvayajñānāmasaṃkhyātairmahāguṇaiḥ
pūjayā vipulaṃ rājyam agnikāyeṇa sampadaḥ
japena pāpaśuddhiḥ syājjñānadhyāne na mucyate
dhanuṣāṃ koṭibhiryogaḥ samantāt parirakṣyatekṣati
sahasraiḥ ṣaṣṭibhirgaṅgā dharmatīrthāni cārdhataḥ
ajñānāmbhāvanārthāya pratimā parikalpitā
haste piṇḍaṃ samutsṛjya lihet kūrparamātmanaḥ
jñānacakṣurvihīnatvādandhāḥ sūryaṃ yathoditam
karasthaṃ sa mahāratnaṃ tyaktvā kācaṃ hi mārgati
jñānadhyānarataiḥ śāntairyadyastyevaṃ sasto yaṃ niṣevitam
pāpaśuddhyartham ajñānāt tattīrthaṃ na tu yoginām
adhyeyam aiśvaraṃ jñānaṃ alpakleśaṃ mahāphalam
syānmahāratnavāhibhyaḥ phalaṃ pāṣāṇavāhinām
pāṣāṇaśatasāhasraṃ yaḥ skandhenābhivāñchati
voḍhuṃ śramavināśaṃ vā naiva tatphalamāpnuyāt
aṅguṣṭhodaramātrepi sukhaṃ saṃvāhya yatphalam
na tatphalamavāpnoti hitaṃ pāṣāṇavāhinaḥ
evaṃ hi kaṣṭāni tapāṃsi yajñān kleśātmakānalpaphalapradāśca
jñānayogaḥ pradhānatvādantaraṅgo vimuktidaḥ
bahiraṅgapradhānaṃ yajñādyaṃ bhogasādhanam
yathāntaḥpuracāreṇa naraḥ strīṣu prasīdati
ardhayāmaṃ muhūrtaṃ vā dhyāyataḥ paramaṃ śivam
iti śivadharmottare jñānayogo nāma tṛtīyo'dhyāyaḥ
oṃ namaḥ śivāya
dhyāyamānaḥ śivaṃ yogī bhuṅkte'nnaṃ satataṃ yataḥ
sa gacched īśvarasthānam evaviṃśakulānvitaḥ
evaṃ śāliviśeṣeṇa phalaṃ syāduttarottaram
vyañjanāni vicitrāṇi svādūni surabhīṇi ca
koṭiphalādhikānyāhuḥ snigdhāni madhurāṇi ca
kṣīrabhaktena naivedyaṃ daśakoṭiguṇādhikam
pānakānāṃ sugandhānāṃ phalaṃ tulyaṃ rasālayā
tathā phalaviśeṣaiśca svādubhiḥ sadṛśaṃ phalam

yaḥ pradadyātsudhavalaṃ śivasandhyāmahāvṛṣaṃ
vistīrṇā raktamuccāgraṃ samunnatavṛhatkaṭiṃ
ūrdhvaṃ kuñcitaśṛṅgāgramahāśṛṅgāntarantathā
sthūlāgraśṛṅgayuktaṃ vā sarvāvayavaśobhanaṃ
raktapītaiś ca kusumaiḥ kuṅkumādyaiś cavarṇakaiḥ
yathāśobhaṃ samālabhya vibhavaiḥ pūjayed vṛṣaṃ
kiṅkiṇīravakopetaṃ ghaṇiṭakā cāmarānvitaṃ
aṣṭāṅgulapravīstīrṇāṃ hemapaṭṭavinirmitāṃ
rājatīṃ cārmaṇīṃ vāpi mālāmurasi vinyaset
tadvidhāṃ pṛṣṭhamālāṃ ca pucchāntakakudāśritāṃ
nānābharaṇasaṃyuktām udarārdhapralambinīṃ
paṭṭaṃ śirasi sauvarṇaṃcandracandrika bhūṣitaṃ
cāmarāśvatthapatraiś ca badhnīyād dravyasaṃyutaṃ
ghaṇṭāṅkāṃsyamayī śuddhāṃ gale vadhnīta susvanām
khuraiḥ suvarṇakai raupyaiḥ keyūrāṅgadabhūṣitaṃ
paṭṭavastrairvicitraiś ca yathāśobhāmalaṅkṛtaṃ
dodhūyamānaś camaraiḥ śivāya vinivedayet
evaṃ vidhaṃ yo vṛṣabhaṃ gopatiṅgrāhaṇakṣamaṃ
vatsikājananaprāyaṃ bahukṣīrāvahaṅgamaṃ
evaṃ yaḥ sampradyāt tu vṛṣṣendraṃ śivagokulaṃ
sa pūrvoktaṃ labhet puṇyaṃ yatsandhyāvṛṣadānataḥ
nivedya sadguṇopetamanaḍvāhaṃ śivāśrame
daśa dhenupradānasya phalaṃ śobhanamāpnuyāt
utpādya sasyāni tṛṇaś caranti tadevabhūyaḥ savalaṃ vahanti
na bhārakhinnāḥ pravadanti kiñcidaho vṛṣairddhāryati jīvalokaḥ
vṛṣāṇāmpṛṣṭhamārūḍhā ye gacchanti narādhamāḥ
vṛṣapṛṣṭhena gacchantaṃ jñānaṃ jñātvā yadā nṛpaḥ
na vārayedupekṣeta pāpaṃ tasyāpi tatsamaṃ
ye vṛṣāḥ sādhubhir muktās triśūlāṅkāḥ śivapriyāḥ
tān vāhayanti ye mūḍhāḥ pāpācārānarādhamāḥ
te mahāpralayaṃ yāvadaśeṣanarakāgniṣu
rājñaś ca viṣaye yasya jānataś ca narādhamaiḥ
vṛṣā vāhyanti pāpiṣṭhaiḥ pāpantasyāpi tatsamaṃ
tasmān nivārayed rājā vāhyamānān khalairvṛṣān
vāhyamānān vṛṣān vārya nṛpatiḥ svargamāpnuyāt
ihārogyaṃ dhanaṃ saukhyamāyuḥ kīrtiṃ śriyaṃ sutān
yaś ca pāpaviśuddhyarthaṃ prāyaścittaṃ samīhate
savṛṣaikādaśīṃ datvā varṣaṃ rakṣec ca mātaraṃ
ekaikarakṣaṇaṃ kuryād gorūpāṇāṃ śataṃ śataṃ
tasmāt praviralā gāstu cārayet pāyayīta ca
gobhirnnaikatra rakṣāḥ syurddanyānaḍvāhapadrahaḥ
śaradgrīṣme ca madhyānhe chāyāyāṃ viniveśayet
chāyāsu ghanavṛkṣāṇāṃ punarutthāya cārayet
ya evaṃ viralā gāś ca sadākṛtvā'nupālayet
te vindanti mahābhogānnarāḥ śivapure ciraṃ
te krīḍanti mahābhogairdivyaiḥ śivapure narāḥ
na śivaḥ paripūrṇatvāt kañcidarthaṃ samīhate
kintu tannāmamātreṇa dattambhevatī cākṣayaṃ
śimuddiśya nirvāpaṃ yaḥ pradadyāttadarthine
kalaśaṃ karkkarīyuktaṃ bastraputānupūritaṃ
samprāpya sa śive loke varṣaṃ koṭiṃ pramodate
raupyaṃ suvarṇapātraṃ vā nivedya śivayogine
apyekaṃ śivamuddiśya yoginaṃ śivadhārmikaṃ
śivāpravrajitāṃ śāntāṃ śaktyā bhaktyā ca bhojayet
triśūlapāṇiryaḥ śaktyā vahate śivaguggulaṃ
vipulaiḥ sa mahābhogaiḥ krīḍan śivapure baset
sarve'pi tiṣṭhanti mahāntakālambhaveyurante pṛthivīśvarāś ca
golokasya prasaṅgena tadbhaktānāṃ samāsataḥ
śivalokādvahirjñeyamaṇḍasyāvaraṇāṣṭakaṃ
aṇḍādūrdhvaṃ punarjñeyaṃ prakṛterdharmataḥ sthitaṃ
brahmalokātparaṃ jñeyaṃ viṣṇoḥ sthānaṃ sanātanaṃ
aprameyaguṇaṃ śuddhamanaupamyaṃ sanātanaṃ
gaṇairadhyuṣitaṃ sthānamasaṃkhyairyāgatatparaiḥ
samprāpya tatpadaṃ siddhāḥ samastalkeśavarjjitāḥ
dhyāyanti devatāṃ nityaṃ te siddhā yānti tatpuraṃ
tatprāptāstadguṇairyuktā devatārūpadhāriṇaḥ
bhavanti ca śivāḥ siddhāḥ svabhogā nāma īśvarāḥ
abhyasya yaḥ śivaṃ jñānaṃ kiñcitkālaṃ tyajetpunaḥ
kāmatṛṣṇābhibhūtatvāt tasyāpīdaṃ mahatphalaṃ
manuṣyalokāmāsādya śivayogī bhavetpunaḥ
tataḥsa yogavidhanā punardhyāyati śaṅkaraṃ
samprāpya paramaṃ sthānaṃ śivayogādvasennaraḥ
tasmādantarbahiś cintāṃ paribhājya vicakṣaṇaḥ
sahasraṃ śiva bhaktānāṃ bhojayīta daśottaraṃ
tataḥ sahasra dogdhrīṇāṅgavāṃ śvetaṃ śitaṃ tathā
pūjitaṃgandhapuṣpādyaiś cailakaṇṭhamalaṅkṛtaṃ
gavāṃ mantraṃ samuccārya japettāsāṃ puraḥ sthitaḥ
gandhapuṣpākṣatonmiśrāṃ gṛhītvā tāmrakarkkarīṃ
śṛṅge pucchaṃ pradhānāyā goś ca snāpyatadambhasā
jñānapuṇyaṃ mahāśāntaṃ tattasmādakṣayaṃ phalaṃ
bandubhiḥ svajanaiḥ snigdhair bhṛtyaiḥ dāsaiḥ samāśritaiḥ
tatra bhuṅkte mahābhogānicchayā śivavadvaśī
pralayānte tataḥ prāpya jñānayogamanuttamaṃ
tatra deśe bhaven nityaṃ sarveṣaṃ dehināṃ śivaṃ
śivāya namaḥ
