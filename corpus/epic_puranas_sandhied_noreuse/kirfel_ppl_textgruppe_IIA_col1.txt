arḍhanārīnaravapuḥ pracaṇḍo 'tiśarīravān
vibhajātmānam ity uktvā taṃ brahmāntardadhe tataḥ
tathokto 'sau dvidhā strītvaṃ puruṣatvaṃ tathākarot
bibheda puruṣatvaṃ ca daśadhā caikadhā ca saḥ
saumyāsaumyais tathā rūpaiḥ śāntaiḥ strītvaṃ ca sa prabhuḥ
bibheda bahudhā devaḥ svarūpair asitaiḥ sitaiḥ
tato brahmatmasaṃbhūtaṃ pūrvaṃ svāyaṃbhuvaṃ prabhum
ātmānam eva kṛtavān prajāpālye manuṃ dvija
śatarūpāṃ ca tāṃ nārīṃ taponirdhūtakalmaṣāṃ
svāyaṃbhuvo manur devaḥ patnītve jagṛhe vibhuḥ
tasmāc ca puruṣād devī śatarūpā vyajāyata
priyavratottānapādau prakhyātāv ātmakarmabhiḥ
kanye dve ca tathā ṛddhiṃ prasūtiṃ ca tataḥ pitā
dadau prasūtiṃ dakṣāya tathā ṛddhiṃ ruceḥ purā
priyavratottānapādau prasūtyākūtiṣaṃjñitam
kanyādvayaṃ ca dharmajñā rūpaudāryaguṇānvitaṃ
dadau prasūtiṃ dakṣāya ākūtiṃ rucaye purā
prajāpatiḥ sa jagrāha taylor jajñesadakṣiṇaḥ
putro yajño mahāhāga dampatyor mithunaṃ tataḥ
yajñasya dakṣiṇāyāṃ tu putrā dvādaśa jajñire
yāmā iti samākhyātā devāḥ svāyaṃbhuve manau
tasya putrās tu yajñasya dakṣiṇāyāṃ sabhāsvarāḥ prasūtyāṃ ca tathā dakṣaś catasro viṃśatiṃ tathā
sasarja kanyās tāsāṃ tu samyaṅ nāmāni me śṛṇu
śraddhā lakṣmīr dhṛtis tuṣṭiḥ puṣṭir medhā kriyā tathā
buddhir lajjā vapuḥ śāntir ṛddhiḥ kīrtis trayodaśī
patnyarthaṃ pratijagrāha dharmo dākṣāyaṇīḥ prabhuḥ
tābhyaḥ śiṣṭā yaviyasya ekādaśa sulocanāḥ
khyātiḥ saty atha saṃbhūtiḥ sṃṛtiḥ prītiḥ kṣamā tathā
saṃnatiś cānasūyā ca ūrjā svāhā svadhā tathā
bhṛgur bhavo marīciś ca tathā caivāngirā muniḥ
pulastyaḥ pulahaś caiva kratuś ca ṛṣivaras tathā
atrir vaśiṣṭho vahniś ca pitaraś ca yathākramaṃ
khyātyādyā jagṛhuḥ kanyā munayo munisattamāḥ
śraddhā kāmaṃ calā darpaṃ niyamaṃ dhṛtir ātmajaṃ
saṃtoṣaṃ ca tathā tuṣṭir lobhaṃ puṣṭir asūyata
manus tāsu ākūtiṃ rucaye dadau prasūtiṃ caiva dak ṣāya devahūtiṃ ca kardame medhā śrutaṃ kriyā daṇḍaṃ nayaṃ vinayam eva ca
bodhaṃ buddhis tathā lajjā vinayaṃ vapur ātmajaṃ
vyvasāyaṃ prajajñe vai kṣemaṃ śāntir asūyata
sukhaṃ ṛḍdhir yaśaḥ kīrtir ity ete dharmaśūnavaḥ
kāmān nandi sutaṃ harṣaṃ dharmapautram asūyata
hīṃsā bhāryā tv adharmasya tasyāṃ jajñe tathānṛtaṃ
kanyā ca nikṛtis tābhyāṃ bhayaṃ narakam eva ca
māyā ca vedanā caiva mithunaṃ tv iḍam etayoḥ
tayor jajñe 'tha vai māyā mṛtyuṃ bhūtāpahāriṇaṃ
vedanā svasutaṃ cāpi duḥkhaṃ jajñe 'tha rauravāt
mṛtyor vyādhijarāśokatṛṣṇākrodhāś ca jajñire
duḥkhottarāḥ smṛṭā hy ete sarve cādharmalakṣaṇāḥ
naiṣāṃ bhāryāsti putro vā te sarve hy ūrdhvaretasaḥ
raudrāṇy etāni rūpāṇi viṣṇor munivarātmaja
nityaṃ pralayahetutvaṃ jagato 'sya prayānti vai
mārkaṇḍeyap viṣṇup
ity eṣa tāmasaḥ sargo brahmaṇo 'vyaktajanmanaḥ
rudrasargaṃ pravakṣyāmi tan me nigadataḥ śṛṇu
tanayāś ca tathaivāṣṭau patṇyaḥ putrāś ca te tathā
kalpādā ātmanas tulyaṃ sutaṃ pradhyāyatas tataḥ
prādur āsit prabhor aṅke kumāro nīlalohitaḥ
rudan vai susvaraṃ so 'tha dravaṃś ca dvijasattama
kiṃ rodiṣiti taṃ brahmā rudantaṃ pratyuvāca ha nāma dehiti taṃ so 'tha pratyuvāca prajāpatiṃ
rudras tvaṃ deva nāmnāsi mā rodīr dhairyam āvaha
evam uktaḥ punaḥ so 'tha saptakṛtvo ruroda ha
tato 'nyāni dadau tasmai sapta nāmāni vai prabhuḥ
sthānāni caiṣām aṣṭānāṃ patniḥ putrāṃs ca vai prabhuḥ
bhavaṃ śarvam atheśānaṃ tathā paśupatiṃ dvija
bhīmaṃ ugraṃ mahādevam uvāca sa pitāmahaḥ
cakre nāmāny athaitāni sthānāny eṣāṃ cakāra saḥ
sūryo jalaṃ mahī vaḥ nir vāyur ākāśam eva ca
dīkṣīto brāhmaṇaḥ soma ity etās tanavaḥ kramāt
suvarcalā tathaivomā vikeśī cāparā śivā
svāhā dīśas tathā dīkṣā rohiṇī ca yathākramaṃ
sūryādīnāṃ naraśreṣṭha rudrādyair nāmabhiḥ saha
patnyaḥ smṛtā mahābhāgās tadapatyāni me śṛṇu
yeṣāṃ sūtiprasūtaiś ca idam āpūritaṃ jagat
śanaiścaras tathā śukro lohitāṅgo manojavaḥ
skandaḥ svargo 'tha saṃtāno budhaś cānukramāt sutāḥ
evaṃprakāro rudro 'sau satīṃ bhāryām aviudata
dakṣakopāc ca tatyāja sā satī svaṃ kalevaraṃ
hīmavadduhitā sābhūn menāyāṃ dvijasattama
tasyā bhrātā tu mainākāḥ sakhāmbhodher anuttamaḥ
upayeme punaś comām ananyāṃ bhagavān bhavaḥ
devau dhātṛvidhātārau bhṛgoḥ khyātir asūyata
śriyaṃ ca devadevasya patuī nārāyaṇasya yā
maitreya uvāca kathitaṃ me tvayā sarvaṃ yat pṛṣṭo 'si mahāmune
bhṛgusargāt prabhṛty eṣa sargo me kathyatāṃ punaḥ
parāśara uvāca bhṛgoḥ khyātyāṃ samutpannā lakṣmīr viṣṇuparigrahaḥ
tathā dhātṛvidhātārau khyātyāṃ jātau sutau bhṛgoḥ
āyatir niyatiś caiva meroḥ kanye mahātmanaḥ
dhātṛvidhātros te bhārye tayor jātau sutāv ubhau
prāṇas caiva mṛkaṇḍuś ca pitā mama mahāyaśāḥ
manasvinyāṃ ahaṃ tasmāt putro vedaśirā mama
dhūmravatyāṃ samabhavat prāṇasyapi nibodha me
prāṇasya dyutimān putra utpannas tasya cātmajaḥ
ajarāś ca tayoḥ putrāḥ pautrāś ca bahavo 'bhavan
patnī marīceḥ sambhūtīḥ paurṇamāsam asūyata
virajāḥ parvataś caiva tasya putrau mahātmanaḥ
tayoḥ putrāṃs tu rakṣiṣye vaṃśasaṃkirtane dvija
prāṇasyāpi sutaṃ śṛṇu
prāṇasya dyutimān putra rājavāṃś ca tato 'bhavat
tato vaṃśo mahābhāga vistāraṃ bhārgavo gataḥ
vaṃśasaṃkirtane putrān vadiṣye 'haṃ taylor dvija
smṛteś cāṅgirasaḥ putrāḥ prasūtāḥkanyakāṣ tathā
sinīvālī kuhūś caiva rākā cānumatis tathā
anusūyā tathaivatrer jajñe putrān akalmaṣān
somaṃ durvāśasaṃ caiva dattātreyaṃ ca yoginaṃ
prityāṃ pulastyabhāryāyāṃ dattolis tatsuto 'bhavat
pūrvajanmani so 'gastyaḥ smṛtaḥ svāyamṛbhuve 'ntare
kardamaś cavarīyāmś ca sahiṣṇuś ca sutatrayaṃ
kṣamā tu suṣuve bhāryā pulahasya prajāpateḥ
kratoś ca samnatir bhāryā bālakhilyān asūyata
ṣaṣṭir yāni sahasrāṇi yatinām ūrdhvaretasāṃ
aṅguṣṭhaparvamātrāṇāṃ jvaladbhāskaratejasāṃ
ūrjāyāmṛ ca vasiṣṭhasya saptājāyanta vai sutāḥ
rajogātrordhvabāhuś ca savanaś canaghas tathā
sutapāḥ śukra ity ete sarve saptarṣayaḥ smṛtāḥ
yo 'sāv agnir abhimānī brahmaṇas tanayo 'grajaḥ
tasmāt svāhā sutān lebhe trīn udāraujaso dvija
pāvakaṃ pavamānaṃ ca śuciṃ cāpi jalāśinaṃ
teṣāṃ tu santatāv anye catvārimśac ca pañca ca
evam ekonapañcāśad vahnayaḥ parikīrtitāḥ
kathyante vahnayaś caite pitā putratrayamṛ ca yat
pitaro brahmaṇā sṛṣṭā vyākhyātā ye mayā tava
agniṣvāttā barhiṣado 'nagnayaḥ sāgnayaś ca ye
tebhyaḥ svadhā sute jajñe menāṃ vai dhāriṇīṃ tathā
te ubhe brahmavādinyau yoginyau cāpy ubhe dvija
uttamajñānasaṃpanne sarvaiḥ samudite guṇaiḥ
ity eṣā dakṣakanyānāṃ kathitāpatyasaṃṭatiḥ
