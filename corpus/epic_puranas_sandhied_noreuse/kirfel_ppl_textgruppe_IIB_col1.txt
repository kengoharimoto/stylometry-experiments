tasyādityas tanur nāmnaḥ prathamā prathamasya te
ity ukte tasya yat tejaś cakṣus tv āṣit prakāśakaṃ
udyantam astamyantaṃ ca varjayed darśane raviṃ
tasmād bhaven nābhyudito vā hy astamita eva ca
na rudram prati meheta sarvāvastham kathamcana
evam yuktāñ dvijāñ devo rudras tāñ na hinasti vai
dvitiyam nāmadheyam te mayā proktam bhaveti yat
ity ukte tv atha tasyāsic charīrastham rasātmakam
na niṣṭhīven nāvagāhen na snāyed apsu nagnās ca naiva gacchec ca maithunam va niṣṭhīvet kadācana
maithunam naiva sevisa śiraḥsnānam ca varjayet
medhyāmedhyās tv apām etās medhyāmedhyaśarīratvān tanavo munibhiḥ smṛtaḥ naiva duṣyanty apaḥ kvacit
na hinasti bhavo devaḥ sadaivam yo 'psu vartate
ity ukte yat sthiram tasya śarīre hy asthisamjñitam
ya evaṃ vartate bhūmau taṃ śarvo na hinasti vai
caturthasya caturthī syād vāyur nāmna tanus tava
yajñaair vyavaharanty enaṃ ye vai paricaranti ca
evaṃ yuktaṃ maheśāno naiva devo hinasti taṃ
tasmāt paśupates tasya tanur agnir nirucyate
tasmād amedhyaṃ na dahen na ca pādau pratāpayet
adhastān nopadadhyāc ca na cainam atilaṅghayet
nainaṃ paśupatir deva evaṃ yuktaṃ hinasti vai
ity ukte suśiraṃ tasya śarīrastham abhūc ca yat
maithunaṃ vāpi na cared ucchiṣṭāni ca notkṣipet
na hinasti ca taṃ devo yo bhīme hy evam ācaret
saptamaṃ yan mayā proktaṃ nām ogreti tava prabho
tasya nāmnaṣ tanus tubhyaṃ dviṣo bhavati dikṣitaḥ
evam ukte tu yat tasya caitanyaṃ vai śarīragaṃ
te haranty asya pāpmānaṃ ye vai parivadanti taṃ
evaṃ yuktān dvijān ugro devas tān na hinasti vai
ity ukte yan ināṣ tasya saṃkalpakam abhūt prabhoḥ
tasmād vibhāvyate hy eṣa mahādevas tu candramāḥ
na hanti taṃ mahādevo ya evaṃ veda taṃ prabhuṃ
śuklātmā saṃsthito rudraḥ pibaty ambho gabhastibhiḥ
yat tu samkalpakaṃ tasya prajāsv iha samāsthitaṃ
avijñātagatiś caiva dvau putrau cānilasya tu
tasya vai rohiṇī patnī putras tasyā budhaḥ smṛtahṛ
bhaktaiṣ tathā candramasi bhaktyā vandyas tu nāmabhiḥ
evamṛ yo veti taṃ devaṃ tanubhir nāmabhiṣ ca ba
balasya tejaḥ putras tu unmādasya tu samśayaḥ
merukanye smṛte bhārye vidhātur dhātur eva ca
āyatir niyatīś caiva tayoḥ putrau dṛdhavratau
unnataś ca dyutimataḥ svanavātaś ca tāv ubhau
tayor gotrakarau putrau jātau saṃnyāsaniścitaḥ
tathaiva bharatāgniṃ ca kirtimantaṃ ca tāv ubhau
madhyamo devabāhus ca vinito nāma te trayaḥ
kardamasya śrutiḥ patnī ātreyy ajanayat svayam
daśa kanyādvayaṃ caiva yaiḥ kṣatraṃ sampravartitaṃ
aruṇasyāgrato yānti parivārya divākaraṃ
svasārau tu yaviyasyau puṇyā satyavati ca te
parvasasya snuṣe te vai pūrṇamāsasutasya tu
tasyãs tu ye yaviyãmso vãsiṣthãh sapta viṣrutãh
pavamãnatmajaś caiva kavyavãhana ucyate
viṣruto laukikãgnis tu prathamo brahmanaḥ sutaḥ
brahmodanãgnis tatputro bharato nãma viṣrutaḥ
sa jñeyo gārhapatyo 'gnis tasya putradvayaṃ smṛtaṃ
kṛtikācāriṇi dhiṣṇi dhiṣṇyād avyabhicāriṇyas jajñire tāś ca dhiṣṇayaḥ tāṣūtpannās tu dhiṣṇayaḥ
pratakkān yo nabho nāma catvare 'sau vibhāvyate
viśvavyacāḥ samudro 'gnir brahmasthāne sa kīrtyate
brahmajyotir vasur nāma brahmasthāne sa ucyate
anuddesyo hy ahirbudhnyāḥ so 'gnir gṛhapatiḥ smṛtaḥ
hotrīyas tu smṛto hy agnir vahni yo havyavāhanāḥ
tato 'gnir vaiśvadevas tu brahmanācchāṃsir ucyate
agniḥ so 'vabhṛtho jñeyo vārunena saheṭyate
parasparocchritaḥ so 'gnir bhūtāniha vinirdahet
putras tv agner manyumato ghoraḥ samvartakaḥ smṛtaḥ
tataḥ śucis tu vai sauro gandharvair āyur āhutaḥ
mathito yas tv araṇyāṃ vai so 'gnir agniḥ samidhyate
vivices tu suto hy arkas tasya cāgneḥ sutā ime
surabhir vasur annādo praviṣṭo yaḥ sa rukmarāṭ
kāmyanaimittikāyajñeṣv ete karmasv avasthitāḥ
devair mahātmabhiḥ punyaiḥ prathamasyāntare manoḥ
sarve tapasvino hy ete sarve brahmabhṛtas tathā
