asthi samjñitam yaḥ evam vartate bhūmau tam śarvaḥ na hinasti vai caturthasya caturthī syāt vāyuḥ nāmna tanuḥ tava yajñaaiḥ vyavaharanti enam ye vai paricaranti ca evam yuktam maheśānaḥ nā eva devaḥ hinasti tam tasmāt paśupateḥ tasya tanuḥ agniḥ nirucyate tasmāt amedhyam na dahet na ca pādau pratāpayet adhastāt na upadadhyāt ca na ca enam atilaṅghayet na enam paśupatiḥ devaḥ evam yuktam hinasti vai iti ukte suśiram tasya śarīra stham abhūt ca yat maithunam vā api na caret ucchiṣṭāni ca na utkṣipet na hinasti ca tam devaḥ yaḥ bhīme hi evam ācaret saptamam yat mayā proktam nām ogra iti tava prabho tasya nāmnaḥ tanuḥ tubhyam dviṣaḥ bhavati dikṣitaḥ evam ukte tu yat tasya caitanyam vai śarīra gam te haranti asya pāpmānam ye vai parivadanti tam evam yuktān dvijān ugraḥ devaḥ tān na hinasti vai iti ukte tasya saṃkalpakam abhūt prabhoḥ tasmāt vibhāvyate hi eṣa mahādevaḥ tu candramāḥ na hanti tam mahādevaḥ yaḥ evam veda tam prabhum śukla ātmā saṃsthitaḥ rudraḥ pibati ambhaḥ gabhastibhiḥ yat tu samkalpakam tasya prajāsu iha samāsthitam avijñātagatiḥ ca eva dvau putrau c-ānilasya tu tasya vai rohiṇī patnī putraḥ tasyāḥ budhaḥ smṛta hṛ bhaktaiḥ tathā candramasi evam ṛ yaḥ veti tam devam tanubhiḥ nāmabhiḥ ca balasya tejaḥ putraḥ tu unmādasya tu samśayaḥ meru kanye smṛte bhārye vidhātuḥ dhātuḥ eva ca āyatiḥ niyatīḥ ca eva tayoḥ putrau dṛdhavratau unnataḥ ca dyutimataḥ svanavātaḥ ca tau ubhau tayoḥ gotra karau putrau jātau saṃnyāsa niścitaḥ tathā eva bharata agnim ca kirtimantam ca tau ubhau madhyamaḥ devabāhuḥ ca vinitaḥ nāma te trayaḥ kardamasya śrutiḥ patnī ātreyī ajanayat svayam daśa kanyā dvayam ca eva yaiḥ kṣatram sampravartitam aruṇasya agratas yānti parivārya divākaram svasārau tu yaviyasyau puṇyā satyavati ca te parvasasya snuṣe te vai pūrṇamāsa sutasya tu tasyas tu ye vãsiṣthãh sapta viṣrutãh ca eva kavyavãhanaḥ ucyate viṣrutaḥ laukikaḥ agniḥ tu prathamaḥ brahmanaḥ sutaḥ brahmodana agniḥ tad putraḥ bharataḥ nãma viṣrutaḥ sa jñeyaḥ gārhapatyaḥ agniḥ tasya putra dvayam smṛtam kṛtikā cāriṇi dhiṣṇi dhiṣṇyāt avyabhicāriṇyaḥ jajñire tāḥ ca dhiṣṇayaḥ tāṣu utpannāḥ tu dhiṣṇayaḥ yaḥ nabhaḥ nāma catvare asau vibhāvyate viśvavyacāḥ samudraḥ agniḥ brahma sthāne sa kīrtyate brahmajyotiḥ vasuḥ nāma brahmasthāne saḥ ucyate an uddesyaḥ hi ahirbudhnyāḥ saḥ agniḥ gṛhapatiḥ smṛtaḥ hotrīyaḥ tu smṛtaḥ hi agniḥ vahni yaḥ havyavāhanāḥ tatas agniḥ vaiśvadevaḥ tu brahmanācchāṃsiḥ ucyate agniḥ saḥ avabhṛthaḥ jñeyaḥ vārunena saha iṭyate paraspara ucchritaḥ saḥ agniḥ bhūtān iha vinirdahet putraḥ tu agneḥ manyumataḥ ghoraḥ samvartakaḥ smṛtaḥ tatas śuciḥ tu vai sauraḥ gandharvaiḥ āyuḥ āhutaḥ mathitaḥ yaḥ tu araṇyām vai saḥ agniḥ agniḥ samidhyate viviceḥ tu sutaḥ hi arkaḥ tasya ca agneḥ sutāḥ ime surabhiḥ vasuḥ anna ādaḥ praviṣṭaḥ yaḥ sa rukma rāj kāmya naimittika ayajñeṣu ete karmasu avasthitāḥ devaiḥ mahātmabhiḥ punyaiḥ prathamasya antare manoḥ sarve tapasvinaḥ hi ete sarve brahma bhṛtaḥ tathā
