tārayet pitṝn iṣṭim cakre aśvamedha ākhyam vasiṣṭaḥ muni sattamaḥ iṣṭitaḥ nirgataḥ śambhuḥ varam vṛṇu vasiṣṭhakam prāha iti tam vasiṣṭaḥ api śiva tuṣṭaḥ asi me yadi vastavyam ca atra deveśa tathā iti dāyakāḥ pāṭhayet vā gayā ākhyānam viprebhyaḥ puṇya kṛt naraḥ gayā śrāddham kṛtam tena kṛtam tena su niścitam gayāyāḥ mahimānam ca hi abhyaset yaḥ samāhitaḥ tena iṣṭam rājasūyena aśvamedhena nāradaḥ
