sākṣāt paśyadhvam mām śuci smitāḥ dadāni vaḥ vara aniṣṭāni hṛdaya saṃsthitāḥ tāḥ evam uktāḥ pārvatyāḥ vaidikyaḥ apsarasaḥ śubhāḥ devīm dṛṣṭvā praṇamya eva śirasā pādayoḥ natāḥ aśru pūrṇa īkṣaṇāḥ dīnāḥ saṃstabhya ātmānam ātmanā vāruṇyai ca śaraṇyāyai gauryai kālyai namaḥ namaḥ namaḥ tūṇīra dhāriṇyai dhāriṇyai jagataḥ namaḥ viśākha śākhayoḥ ca eva naigameṣasya ca eva hi strīṇām saubhāgya dāyinyai dhāriṇyai ca namaḥ namaḥ namaḥ prabhāyai śuddhāyai śuddhaye śucaye namaḥ dīkṣāyai dakṣiṇāyai ca jvālāyai ca namaḥ namaḥ indriyāṇām pravṛttyai ca nivṛttyai ca eva karmaṇām śrutaye sarva vedānām bhavānyai ca namaḥ namaḥ mṛkaṇḍvai mārdabāhvai ca tathā surataye namaḥ diśa naḥ sumanāḥ sarvam yat kiṃcid hṛdaye sthitam yaḥ idam paṭhate nityam naraḥ strī vā samāhitaḥ sa rātriṣu kṛtam pāpam tyajet sarvam aśeṣatas śayānaḥ japate yaḥ ca prayataḥ vyāsa nityaśas divākṛtam sa jahyāt tu mṛtaḥ ca sugatim vrajet sa dehabhedam āsādya pañcacūḍā priyaḥ bhavet yaḥ ca enam prajahan prāṇān japet martyaḥ su dustyajān sa bhūtvā anucaraḥ devyāḥ puṇye loke mahīyate uvāca harṣamāṇa āsyā pañcacūḍāḥ tadā vacaḥ śṛṇuta apsarasaḥ sarvāḥ tapasaḥ asya mahat phalam yat ca api parayā bhaktyā mām prapannāḥ stha śobhanāḥ brūta yat ca api kiṃcid vaḥ hṛdi sthitam aśaṅkitāḥ sarvam dāsyāmi tat vaḥ aham mā cirāya tat ucyatām pañcacūḍāḥ ūcuḥ devi puṃsām striyaḥ sarvāḥ kārya artham viditam ca te sarva strīṇām mahādevi bhavantu puruṣāḥ vaśāḥ adya prabhṛti lokeṣu sarva strīṇām narāḥ sadā sarve vaśyāḥ bhaviṣyanti sarva kārya karāḥ ca ha ādau paścāt ca sarvābhyaḥ hiraṇyam paśavaḥ striyaḥ sarva bhogān ca dāsyantu vaśagāḥ sarvathā api ca akāryāṇi kariṣyantu strīṇām vaśya tvam āgatāḥ paśyantaḥ api vyalīkāni doṣān vaikṛtyam eva ca mālya dāmam gṛhītvā tu bhrāmayantī śubha ānanā kusumāni abhavan tāni nāryaḥ kamala locanāḥ tāḥ uvāca amarāḥ yūyam jarā kṣaya vivarjitāḥ jagat moha karāḥ yūyam puṃsām hṛdaya bandhanāḥ dṛṣṭi sparśa vilāseṣu āviśya jagati striyaḥ saṃmohayiṣyatha narān strī vaśān ca kariṣyatha narāḥ sarve ca yuṣmāsu bhaviṣyanti sadā ratāḥ vṛttiḥ śubhā bhavitrī ca sarvāsām mama tejasā puṃsām strī bhoga siddhi artham strīṇām rati artham eva ca kriyāḥ śubha aṅga saṃskārāḥ divyāḥ ye mānuṣāḥ ca ha bahu rūpāḥ ca tāḥ bhūtvā viviśuḥ sarvadā aṅganāḥ evam devī tadā vyāsa sṛṣṭvā tāḥ vai visṛjya ca uvāca apsarasaḥ brūta kim vaḥ bhūyas karomi aham tāḥ tuṣṭa manasaḥ ca api ūcuḥ vyāsa bhavemahi tāḥ uvāca tatas devī evam loke bhaviṣyatu gaṇaiḥ sarvaiḥ ca sahitaḥ gṛhān svān āviśat prabhuḥ jalada vṛṣabha yāyī sarva duḥkhānta kārī samara vṛṣabha ketuḥ candramauliḥ jagāma vyāsaḥ uvāca svayambhuvā yathā sṛṣṭā sā eva vṛttiḥ praśasyate harikeśaḥ uvāca duṣṭā ca eva apraśastā ca garhitā sādhubhiḥ sadā vṛttiḥ svayambhuvā sṛṣṭā tyaktavyā yadi no bhavet atha tasya evam aniśam tad parasya tad āśiṣaḥ kvacid su puṣpaiḥ sahakāra vṛkṣaiḥ latā upagūḍhaiḥ tilakaiḥ ca gūḍham atas parataram nā asti kṣetram guhyam iti īśvari jarā maraṇa saṃtyaktaḥ sarva śoka vivarjitaḥ
