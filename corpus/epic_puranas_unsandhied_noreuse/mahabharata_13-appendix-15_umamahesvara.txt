patiḥ jātiṣu api ca vai strī tvam viśiṣṭam iti me matiḥ kāya kleśena mahatā puruṣaḥ prāpnuyāt phalam yathāsukham patimatī sarvam pati anukūlataḥ īdṛśam dharma saukaryam paśyadhvam pramadāḥ prati tapaḥ caryā ca dānam ca patau tasyāḥ samarpitau rūpam kulam yaśaḥ svargaḥ sarvam tasmin pratiṣṭhitam evaṃvṛtta samācārā sva vṛttena eva śobhanā patinā ca samam gacchet puṇya lokān sva karmaṇā evaṃbhūtaḥ api bhartā vai strīṇām bhūṣaṇam uttamam āḍhyam vā rūpa yuktam vā virūpam dhana varjitam yā patim toṣayet sādhvī sā nārīṇām pativratā daridrān ca virūpān ca pramūḍhān kuṣṭha saṃyutān evaṃvidhān patīn deva toṣayitvā pativratā evam pravartamānāyāḥ patiḥ pūrvam mriyeta ced umā etat evam mayā proktam strīṇām dharma āśritam hitam saumya śīlāḥ śubha ācārāḥ sarvāḥ tāḥ saṃbhavanti hi atha aśubham pravakṣyāmi strīṇām vṛttam maheśvara āsuryaḥ ca eva paiśācyaḥ rākṣasyaḥ ca bhavanti yāḥ tāsām vṛttam aśeṣeṇa śrūyatām loka kāraṇāt nyāyataḥ vā anyathā proktāḥ bhāva doṣa samanvitāḥ bhartṝn apacaranti eva rāga dveṣa balātkṛtāḥ pravṛddha viṣayāḥ nityam pratikūlam vadanti ca sva buddhi niratāḥ bhūtvā jīvanti ca yathā tathā punar bhavanti pāpiṣṭhāḥ viṣamam vṛttam āśritāḥ anavasthita maryādāḥ bahu veṣāḥ vyaya priyāḥ asaṃtuṣṭāḥ ca lubdhāḥ ca īrṣyā krodha yutāḥ bhṛśam bhoga priyāḥ hita dveṣyāḥ kāma bhoga parāyaṇāḥ patīn paribhavanti eva pratikūla parāyaṇāḥ prāyaśas anṛta bhūyiṣṭhāḥ gurūṇām pratilomakāḥ evam yukta samācārāḥ āsuram bhāvam āśritāḥ aparāḥ pāpa kāriṇyaḥ satatam kalaha priyāḥ niḥsnehāḥ kopanāḥ ca eva bhartṛ putra sva bandhuṣu bandhubhiḥ bhartsitāḥ bhūtvā gṛha kāryāṇi kurvate atha vā bhartsitāḥ deva nīca vṛttāḥ sva bandhuṣu nirdayāḥ niranukrośāḥ kuṭumba artha vilopanāḥ dharma artha rahitāḥ ghorāḥ satatam kurvate kriyāḥ anartha nipuṇāḥ pāpāḥ para prāṇeṣu nirdayāḥ evam yukta samācārāḥ striyaḥ paiśācyam āśritāḥ aparāḥ moha saṃyuktāḥ nirlajjāḥ rodana priyāḥ aśuddhāḥ mala digdha aṅgāḥ pāna māṃsa ratāḥ bhṛśam duṣprasādāḥ mahā krodhāḥ svapna śīlāḥ nirantaram tāmasyaḥ naṣṭa tattva arthāḥ manda śīlāḥ mahā udarāḥ bhuñjanti vividham siddham bhojanam tīvra saṃbhramāḥ hitvā anyān eva gacchanti sarvathā bhṛśa pāpikāḥ nirlajjāḥ dharma saṃdigdhāḥ pratikūlāḥ samantataḥ evam yukta samācārāḥ striyaḥ rākṣasyam āśritāḥ evaṃvidhānām sarvāsām na paratra iha vā sukham etāsām eva ku strīṇām mṛtānām ca maheśvara asaṃśayam ciram kālam narake vāsaḥ iṣyate narakāt hi vimuktānām katham cit kāla paryayāt iṣyate janma mānuṣyam kaṣṭam tatra api bhuñjate tāsām duṣkṛta saṃyogāt duḥkham janma antareṣu api daridrāḥ kleśa bhūyiṣṭhāḥ virūpāḥ kutsitāḥ paraiḥ vidhavāḥ durbhagāḥ vā api labhante duḥkham īdṛśam śata varṣa sahasra antam ātmānam vyabhicāriṇī abhisaṃyānti tāḥ lokān puṇyān parama śobhanān avamatya tu yāḥ pūrvam patim duṣṭena cetasā vartamānāḥ ca satatam bhartṝṇām pratikūla tām bhartrā anumaraṇam kāle yāḥ kurvanti tathāvidhāḥ kāmāt krodhāt bhayāt mohāt apahāsyāḥ bhavanti tāḥ ādi prabhṛti ku strīṇām tathā anumaraṇam vṛthā ādi prabhṛti yā sādhvī bhartuḥ priya parāyaṇā ūrdhvam gacchati sā tatra bhartrā anumaraṇam gatā evam mṛtāyāḥ vai lokān aham paśyāmi cakṣuṣā spṛhaṇīyān sura gaṇaiḥ yān gacchanti pativratāḥ mṛtam ca patim uddiśya kuryāt ca evaṃvidham tapaḥ evam gacchati sā nārī patīnām lokam uttamam ramaṇīyam anirdeśyam duṣprāpam deva mānuṣaiḥ prāpnuyāt tādṛśam lokam kevalam yā pativratā nāradaḥ evam vadantīm rudrāṇīm lajjā bhāva samanvitām toṣayām āsa deveśaḥ vācā saṃpūjayan priyām daivata pramadāḥ ca api deva nadyaḥ ca saṃgatāḥ praṇamya śirasā devīm stutibhiḥ ca api tuṣṭuvuḥ pūjayām āsuḥ apare devadevam mudā yutāḥ saṃprahṛṣṭa manāḥ śarvaḥ saṃvādam cintayan muhur śṛṇu kalyāṇi mad vākyam yat idam tu tvayā mayā kṛtam saṃvādam abhavat tat trilokam gamiṣyati yaḥ idam śrāvayet vidvān saṃvādam tu āvayoḥ priye śuciḥ bhūtvā narān yuktān sa yajña phalam āpnuyāt ye tu enam śṛṇuyuḥ nityam dharmyam tad gata mānasāḥ kathayet śṛṇuyāt vā api yaḥ kaḥ cit nityam āhnikam nāradaḥ devīm devam vayam tatra na apaśyāma prabhāvataḥ evam purā mayā āścaryam dṛṣṭam haimavate vane caratā tīrtha yātrā artham purā keśiniṣūdana bhīṣmaḥ vaiśaṃpāyanaḥ
