nārāyaṇam namaskṛtya naram ca eva narottamam pāṇḍavāḥ tu mahābhāgāḥ yuktāḥ tu yaśasā anaghāḥ etat bhavantam pṛcchāmi saṃmānya abhipraṇamya ca yena vṛṣṇi andhaka kule puruṣāḥ bhasmasāt kṛtāḥ tat cūrṇam sāgare ca api prākṣipan puruṣāḥ nṛpa jajñe sāverakāḥ cūrṇam prakḷptam taiḥ mahā udadhau karaṭaḥ muṇḍaḥ puruṣaḥ kṛṣṇa piṅgala locanaḥ tam aghnanta mahā iṣvāsāḥ śaraiḥ śata sahasraśas na ca aśakyata veddhum sa sarva bhūta atyayaḥ tadā keśāḥ nakhāḥ ca suptānām adyante mūṣakaiḥ niśi aśrūyanta śunām śabdāḥ sarvatas karṇa vedhakāḥ puṇya dāna pradāt ca api śānti mohāt ca kevalāt vṛṣṇi andhakān akhādanta svapne gṛdhrāḥ bhayānakāḥ kurvantaḥ bhīṣaṇān nādān karālāḥ ca api asaṃgatāḥ strīṇām garbheṣu jāyante rākṣasāḥ guhyakāḥ tathā divaḥ praveśana artham te vimānaiḥ divam āyayuḥ na tat mṛṣyati hārdikyaḥ yat avocat tadā ruṣā vyāyacchamānau tau vīrau bāhu draviṇa śālinau śvetaḥ yayau ca api mahā anubhāvaḥ mahā arṇavam yena tam īkṣamāṇaḥ saṃdṛśya tam sāgara antam viśantam saṃkarṣaṇam bhūdharam śuddha buddhim kulastrīṇām suvratānām gavām ca dvijendrāṇām balam ādāya dharme kṛṣṇam śayānam sa vikṛṣya bāṇam vivyādha pāde mṛga śaṅkayā jarā tathā anutāpāt karmaṇaḥ janmanaḥ ca dṛṣṭvā tathā devam ananta vīryam gaṇaiḥ munīnām pūjitaḥ tatra kṛṣṇaḥ āśvāsayām āsa tatas ca kṛṣṇa ūrdhvam gacchan vyāpya lokān sva lakṣmyā devaiḥ sarvaiḥ ṛṣibhiḥ cāraṇaiḥ ca saṃstūyamānaḥ sura siddha saṃghaiḥ dharma sthityā prādurāsīḥ dharāyām kaṃsa ākhya ādīn deva śatrūn ca sarvān hatvā bhūmiḥ sthāpitā bhāra taptā divyam sthānam ajaram ca aprameyam śriyā yuktam puṣpa vṛṣṭyā vavarṣa vāṇī hi āsīt saṃśritā rūpiṇī sā rūpam viśiṣṭam divi saṃsthitam ca bhūmau gatam pūjayata aprameyam sadā hi tasmin nivasāmi devāḥ devāḥ nivṛttāḥ tat padam na āpnuvantaḥ buddhyā devam saṃsmarantaḥ pratītāḥ brahma ādyāḥ te tad guṇān kīrtayantaḥ śubhān lokān svān prapeduḥ sura indrāḥ arjunam tāḥ sma nātham ca dṛṣṭvā anātham vicukruśuḥ tasya mūrdhānam āghrātum iyeṣa ānakadundubhiḥ svasrīyasya mahā bāhuḥ na śaśāka ca śatru han tvam hi tam nāradaḥ ca eva munayaḥ ca sanātanam gāndhāryāḥ vacanam yat tat ṛṣīṇām ca paraṃtapa aśvatthāmnā hataḥ ca api jīvitaḥ tasya tejasā imān tu nā aicchat svān jñātīn rakṣitum sa sakhā tava yathā jīvam param brahma mahā vākyasya niścayāt prāpya vṛṣṇi pravīreṇa yadubhiḥ ca eva pālitaḥ taiḥ hīnām pṛthivīm draṣṭum na ca śakṣyāmi phalguna tatas bhagavataḥ deham dṛṣṭvā śiṣyaḥ pralapya ca smṛtvā tat vacanam sarvam moha śoka upabṛṃhitam aṣṭau mahiṣyaḥ kṛṣṇasya rukmiṇī pramukhāḥ nṛpa revatī ca mahābhāgāḥ viviśuḥ jātavedasam tayoḥ śarīre saṃśliṣya tad saṅga āhlāda śītaḷe tat tat saṃplāvayām āsa salilena sa sāgaraḥ abravīt ca tadā sarvān prāṇinaḥ tatra sāgaraḥ bhājanāni ca haimāni raupyāṇi vividhāni ca sa veśmakā purī ca iyam hareḥ pālyā mayā adhunā hatvā garvam samārūḍhaḥ bhīṣma karṇa jayadrathān yaṣṭi hastān avekṣya asmān avajānāti durmatiḥ bhīṣma droṇa mukhān hatvā nivāta kavacān api jitvā śakram ca rudram ca saḥ asmān adya avamanyate ākroṣṭum nā aśakat jiṣṇuḥ sarva prāṇaiḥ ca bhārata tat ca abhūt śithilam punar tvac bhedam na śarāḥ cakruḥ astāḥ gāṇḍīvadhanvanā yudhiṣṭhirasya anumate vaṃśa kartṛ kumārakān dagdhe atra khāṇḍava araṇye rājye tam saṃnyaveśayat sarasvatīm praviviśuḥ tathā anyāḥ śuddha cetasaḥ nakha keśa daśā kumbha vāriṇā kim samukṣitaḥ paricārakeṣu yaḥ kaḥ cit tiṣṭhan eva vinidritaḥ sarva upaniṣadām artham yaḥ brūte sa mahā guruḥ tena saṃpūjitam liṅgam uddiśya prativāsaram vaiśaṃpāyanaḥ tatas pārthaḥ viniḥśvasya śrūyatām bhagavan iti proktvā yathāvat ācaṣṭe vyāsāya ātma parābhavam tat anusmṛtya saṃmoham sadā śokam mahāmate prayāmi sarvadā mahyam mumūrṣā ca upajāyate tvad vākya sparśana āloka sukham ca amṛta saṃnibham bhīṣma droṇa aṅgarāja ādyāḥ tathā duryodhana ādayaḥ yad prabhāveṇa nirdagdhāḥ sa kṛṣṇaḥ tyaktavān bhuvam tat dhanuḥ tāni ca astrāṇi sa rathaḥ te ca vājinaḥ sarvam eka pade bhraṣṭam dānam aśrotriye yathā deva aṃśāḥ devadevena saṃbhūtāḥ te gatāḥ saha dharma vyavasthā rakṣā artham devena samupekṣitāḥ striyaḥ ca tāḥ purā
