purāṇaṃ sarvaśāstrāṇāṃ prathamaṃ brahmaṇaḥ smṛtam
sargaśca pratisargaśca vaṃśo manvantarāṇi ca
vaṃśānucaritaṃ caiva purāṇaṃ pañcalakṣaṇam
hitārthaṃ sarvalokānāmumayā saha śaṃkaraḥ
ṛkṣaśailaṃ samāruhya tapastepe sudāruṇam
adṛśyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ sarvabhūtātmako vaśī
saptadvīpasamudrāntāṃ saśailavanakānanām
tasminmahārṇave ghore naṣṭe sthāvarajaṅgame
punarekārṇave ghore naṣṭe sthāvarajaṃgame
tasyāstadvacanaṃ śrutvā avatīrya mahānadīm
ṣaḍviṃśacca sahasrāṇi varṣāṇāṃ mānuṣāṇi ca
vyāptam āsīt tadā viśvaṃ trailokyaṃ sacarācaram
citādhūmākulaṃ sarvaṃ trailokyaṃ sacarācaram
pitāmahaṃ samāśvāsya tatraivāntaradhīyata
tasyāstadvacanaṃ śrutvā harṣātpīto mayā stanaḥ
ahaṃ ca pṛthivī jñeyā saptadvīpā sarvatā
ityuktvā māṃ tadā devī tatraivāntaradhīyata
tārayetsarvabhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
yojanānāṃ śataṃ sāgraṃ śrūyate sariduttamā
daśavarṣasahasrāṇi cacāra vipulaṃ tapaḥ
godāvarī vitastā ca candrabhāgā irāvatī
ityuktā sā tadā rājñī vedavedāṅgapāragān
vṛkṣagulmalatāvallyo gṛhāṇi ca samantataḥ
ṣaṣṭivarṣasahasrāṇi ṣaṣṭivarṣaśatāni ca
dārurnāma mahābhāgo vedavedāṅgapāragaḥ
yojanānāṃ śataṃ sāgraṃ nirāhāro gatodakaḥ
devadānavayakṣāṇāṃ gandharvoragarakṣasām
mithilāstho mahābhāgo vedavedāṅgapāragaḥ
tārayet sarvabhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca
tuṣṭuvurvividhaiḥ stotrair brahmādyāś cakrapāṇinam
tato dṛṣṭvā sa rājendraḥ samprahṛṣṭatanūruhaḥ
yadi tuṣṭāstrayo devā brahmaviṣṇumaheśvarāḥ
caitramāse site pakṣe ekādaśyāṃ narādhipa
tasyāstadvacanaṃ śrutvā śabarī śābarāya vai
vijñāpya brāhmaṇān sarvān idaṃ vacanam abravīt
te dṛṣṭā brāhmaṇaiḥ sarvairvedavedāṅgapāragaiḥ
brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyairvaiśyaiḥ śūdraiḥ strībhiśca śaktitaḥ
devadānavasiddhānāṃ gandharvoragarakṣasām
divyarūpadharāḥ sarvā divyābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ
sthāpitaṃ maṇināgena lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā
tīrthe tvaṃ bhava me śambho lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā
evaṃ nārada sarvaṃ tu bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
varuṇasya pure vāso yāvad ābhūtasaṃplavam
adyaprabhṛti te tīrthaṃ bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ
dharmyaṃ yaśasyamāyuṣyaṃ svargyaṃ saṃśuddhikṛnnṛṇām
tuṣṭuvurvividhaiḥ stotrair vāgīśapramukhāḥ surāḥ
āprīṇāti jagatsarvaṃ trailokyaṃ sacarācaram
tasminnekārṇave ghore naṣṭe sthāvarajaṅgame
tasmātsarve putrahīnā bhaviṣyanti na saṃśayaḥ
ayonijaṃ mahāpuṇyaṃ sarvapāpapraṇāśanam
tataḥ provāca bhagavānbrahmā lokapitāmahaḥ
vivasvānsavitā pūṣā caṇḍāṃśurbharga eva ca
ayonije mahādevaṃ snāpayed gandhavāriṇā
rūpayauvanasampannā triṣu lokeṣu viśrutā
ekasya mantrayuktasya kalāṃ nārhati ṣoḍaśīm
vasiṣṭhaṃ ca mahābhāgamityete sapta mānasāḥ
ityete brāhmaṇāḥ sapta purāṇe niścayaṃ gatāḥ
kṣamāvantaḥ prajāvanto maharṣibhir alaṃkṛtāḥ
bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyacca vartamānaṃ tathāparam
adṛśyaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ paramātmā mahattaraḥ
akṣayaṃ tatra vai sarvaṃ jāyate nātra saṃśayaḥ
ekamūrtistrayo devā brahmaviṣṇumaheśvarāḥ
brahmāṇaṃ śaraṇaṃ jagmurdevā indrapurogamāḥ
piṇḍadānātphalaṃ tacca labhate nātra saṃśayaḥ
brāhmaṇaḥ subhago loke vedavedāṅgapāragaḥ
nirmitaṃ parayā bhaktyā lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā
idaṃ yaśasyamāyuṣyaṃ dhanyaṃ duḥkhapraṇāśanam
vareṇa chandayāmāsa rākṣasaṃ vṛṣaketanaḥ
gateṣu teṣu sarveṣu sthāpayāmāsa cācyutam
devakhāte trayo devā brahmaviṣṇumaheśvarāḥ
snānaṃ samācarennityaṃ naraḥ pāpaiḥ pramucyate
brāhmaṇān bhojayed bhaktyā śivabhaktāṃśca yoginaḥ
ahaṅkārakṛtāḥ sarve bhaviṣyanti na saṃśayaḥ
nārāyaṇodare sarvaṃ trailokyaṃ sacarācaram
indro dhātā bhagastvaṣṭā mitro 'tha varuṇo 'ryamā
vivasvānsavitā pūṣā hyaṃśumānviṣṇur eva ca
marutvatī vasurjñānā lambā bhānumatī satī
saṃkalpā ca muhūrtā ca sādhyā viśvāvatī kakup
evaṃ jñātvā samaṃ sarvaṃ sadevāsuramānuṣam
prasādayāmāsa tadā śūlastham ṛṣisattamam
heṣamāṇaḥ svareṇāsau maithunāyopacakrame
mānuṣaṃ rūpamāsthāya lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā
randhrāntareṣu cotpannāḥ śataśo 'tha sahasraśaḥ
saptadvīpārṇavayutāṃ saśailavanakānanām
ṣaṣṭivarṣasahasrāṇi koṭirvarṣaśatāni ca
dharo dhruvaśca somaśca āpaścaivānilo 'nalaḥ
pratyūṣaśca prabhāsaśca vasavo 'ṣṭāvime purā
tena nāmnā svayaṃ tasthau lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā
tatastilottamāṃ sṛṣṭvā brahmā lokapitāmahaḥ
dharmārthakāmamokṣāṇāṃ mārge 'yaṃ devasevitaḥ
sarvapāpavinirmukto jāyate nātra saṃśayaḥ
