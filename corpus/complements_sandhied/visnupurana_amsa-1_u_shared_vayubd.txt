riporādhatta bṛhatī cākṣuṣaṃ sarvatejasam
ajījanatpuṣkariṇyāṃ vāruṇyāṃ cākṣuṣo manum

na yaṣṭavyaṃ na dātavyaṃ na hotavyaṃ kathañcana
