nārāyaṇaṃ namaskṛtya naraṃ caiva narottamam
pāṇḍavās tu mahābhāgā yuktās tu yaśasānaghāḥ
etad bhavantaṃ pṛcchāmi saṃmānyābhipraṇamya ca
yena vṛṣṇyandhakakule puruṣā bhasmasāt kṛtāḥ
tac cūrṇaṃ sāgare cāpi prākṣipan puruṣā nṛpa
jajñe sāverakāś cūrṇaṃ prakḷptaṃ tair mahodadhau
karaṭo muṇḍaḥ puruṣaḥ kṛṣṇapiṅgalalocanaḥ
samūhini mahāketur japāpuṣpāvataṃsakaḥ
kṛkalāsavāhanagaḥ kākapakṣavibhūṣaṇaḥ
tam aghnanta maheṣvāsāḥ śaraiḥ śatasahasraśaḥ
na cāśakyata veddhuṃ sa sarvabhūtātyayas tadā
keśā nakhāś ca suptānām adyante mūṣakair niśi
aśrūyanta śunāṃ śabdāḥ sarvataḥ karṇavedhakāḥ
puṇyadānapradāc cāpi śāntimohāc ca kevalāt
vṛṣṇyandhakān akhādanta svapne gṛdhrā bhayānakāḥ
kurvanto bhīṣaṇān nādān karālāś cāpy asaṃgatāḥ
strīṇāṃ garbheṣu jāyante rākṣasā guhyakās tathā
divaḥ praveśanārthaṃ te vimānair divam āyayuḥ
na tan mṛṣyati hārdikyo yad avocat tadā ruṣā
vyāyacchamānau tau vīrau bāhudraviṇaśālinau
śveto yayau cāpi mahānubhāvo
mahārṇavaṃ yena tam īkṣamāṇaḥ
saṃdṛśya taṃ sāgarāntaṃ viśantaṃ
saṃkarṣaṇaṃ bhūdharaṃ śuddhabuddhim
kulastrīṇāṃ suvratānāṃ gavāṃ ca
dvijendrāṇāṃ balam ādāya dharme
kṛṣṇaṃ śayānaṃ sa vikṛṣya bāṇaṃ
vivyādha pāde mṛgaśaṅkayā jarā
tathānutāpāt karmaṇo janmanaś ca
dṛṣṭvā tathā devam anantavīryaṃ
gaṇair munīnāṃ pūjitas tatra kṛṣṇaḥ
āśvāsayām āsa tataś ca kṛṣṇa
ūrdhvaṃ gacchan vyāpya lokān svalakṣmyā
devaiḥ sarvair ṛṣibhiś cāraṇaiś ca
saṃstūyamānaḥ surasiddhasaṃghaiḥ
dharmasthityā prādurāsīr dharāyām
kaṃsākhyādīn devaśatrūṃś ca sarvān
hatvā bhūmiḥ sthāpitā bhārataptā
divyaṃ sthānam ajaraṃ cāprameyaṃ
śriyā yuktaṃ puṣpavṛṣṭyā vavarṣa
vāṇī hy āsīt saṃśritā rūpiṇī sā
rūpaṃ viśiṣṭaṃ divi saṃsthitaṃ ca
bhūmau gataṃ pūjayatāprameyaṃ
sadā hi tasmin nivasāmi devāḥ
devā nivṛttās tat padaṃ nāpnuvanto
buddhyā devaṃ saṃsmarantaḥ pratītāḥ
brahmādyās te tadguṇān kīrtayantaḥ
śubhāṃl lokān svān prapeduḥ surendrāḥ
arjunaṃ tāḥ sma nāthaṃ ca dṛṣṭvānāthaṃ vicukruśuḥ
tasya mūrdhānam āghrātum iyeṣānakadundubhiḥ
svasrīyasya mahābāhur na śaśāka ca śatruhan
tvaṃ hi taṃ nāradaś caiva munayaś ca sanātanam
gāndhāryā vacanaṃ yat tad ṛṣīṇāṃ ca paraṃtapa
aśvatthāmnā hataś cāpi jīvitas tasya tejasā
imāṃs tu naicchat svāñ jñātīn rakṣituṃ sa sakhā tava
yathā jīvaṃ paraṃ brahma mahāvākyasya niścayāt
prāpya vṛṣṇipravīreṇa yadubhiś caiva pālitaḥ
tair hīnāṃ pṛthivīṃ draṣṭuṃ na ca śakṣyāmi phalguna
tato bhagavato dehaṃ dṛṣṭvā śiṣyaḥ pralapya ca
smṛtvā tad vacanaṃ sarvaṃ mohaśokopabṛṃhitam
aṣṭau mahiṣyaḥ kṛṣṇasya rukmiṇīpramukhā nṛpa
revatī ca mahābhāgā viviśur jātavedasam
tayoḥ śarīre saṃśliṣya tatsaṅgāhlādaśītaḷe
tat tat saṃplāvayām āsa salilena sa sāgaraḥ
abravīc ca tadā sarvān prāṇinas tatra sāgaraḥ
bhājanāni ca haimāni raupyāṇi vividhāni ca
saveśmakā purī ceyaṃ hareḥ pālyā mayādhunā
hatvā garvaṃ samārūḍho bhīṣmakarṇajayadrathān
yaṣṭihastān avekṣyāsmān avajānāti durmatiḥ
bhīṣmadroṇamukhān hatvā nivātakavacān api
jitvā śakraṃ ca rudraṃ ca so 'smān adyāvamanyate
ākroṣṭuṃ nāśakaj jiṣṇuḥ sarvaprāṇaiś ca bhārata
tac cābhūc chithilaṃ punaḥ
tvagbhedaṃ na śarāś cakrur astā gāṇḍīvadhanvanā
yudhiṣṭhirasyānumate vaṃśakartṛkumārakān
dagdhe 'tra khāṇḍavāraṇye rājye taṃ saṃnyaveśayat
sarasvatīṃ praviviśus tathānyāḥ śuddhacetasaḥ
nakhakeśadaśākumbhavāriṇā kiṃ samukṣitaḥ
paricārakeṣu yaḥ kaś cit tiṣṭhann eva vinidritaḥ
sarvopaniṣadām arthaṃ yo brūte sa mahāguruḥ
tena saṃpūjitaṃ liṅgam uddiśya prativāsaram
ahany ahani vedāntaśravaṇaṃ ghaṭikādvayam
vaiśaṃpāyanaḥ
tataḥ pārtho viniḥśvasya śrūyatāṃ bhagavan iti
proktvā yathāvad ācaṣṭe vyāsāyātmaparābhavam
tad anusmṛtya saṃmohaṃ sadā śokaṃ mahāmate
prayāmi sarvadā mahyaṃ mumūrṣā copajāyate
tvadvākyasparśanālokasukhaṃ cāmṛtasaṃnibham
bhīṣmadroṇāṅgarājādyās tathā duryodhanādayaḥ
yatprabhāveṇa nirdagdhāḥ sa kṛṣṇas tyaktavān bhuvam
tad dhanus tāni cāstrāṇi sa rathas te ca vājinaḥ
sarvam ekapade bhraṣṭaṃ dānam aśrotriye yathā
devāṃśā devadevena saṃbhūtās te gatāḥ saha
dharmavyavasthārakṣārthaṃ devena samupekṣitāḥ
striyaś ca tāḥ purā śaptāḥ prahāsakupitena vai
aṣṭāvakreṇa muninā tadarthaṃ tvadbalakṣayaḥ
tvayaikena hatāḥ pūrvaṃ bhīṣmakarṇādayo nṛpāḥ
tvayā yat kauravā dhvastā yadābhīrair bhavāñ jitaḥ
