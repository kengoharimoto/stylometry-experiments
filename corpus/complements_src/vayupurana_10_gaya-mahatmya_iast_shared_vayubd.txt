ata ūrddhvaṃ pravakṣyāmi gayāmāhātmyamuttamam।  
brahmajñānādyathā muktirlabhyate nātra saṃśayaḥ ।। 43.19 ।।  
iti śrīmahāpurāṇe vāyuprokte gayāmāhātmyaṃ nāma tricatvāriṃśo'dyāyaḥ ।। 43 ।।
yojanānāṃ sapādañca śataṃ tasyocchrayaḥ smṛtaḥ ।। 44.4 ।।  
yadituṣṭāḥ stha me devā brahmaviṣṇumaheśvarāḥ।  
brahmaṇo vacanaṃ śrutvā hyākṛṣya svaśarīrataḥ ।  
iti śrīmahāpurāṇe vāyuprokte gayāmāhātmyaṃ nāma catuścatvāriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ।। 44 ।। * ।। 
āsīddharmmo mahātejāḥ sarvvavijñānapāragaḥ।  
iti śrīmahāpurāṇe vāyuprokte gayāmāhātmyaṃ nāma pañcacatvāriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ।। 45 ।। 
mataṅgasya padaṃ tatra dṛśyate sarvamānuṣaiḥ ।। 46.25 ।।  
mataṅgasya padaṃ tatra dṛśyate sarvamānuṣaiḥ ।। 46.35 ।।  
gayāyāṃ cākṣayaṃ śrāddhaṃ japahomatapāṃsi ca।  
iti śrīmahāpurāṇe vāyuprokte gayāmāhātmyaṃ nāma ṣaṭcatvāriṃśo'dhyāya- ।। 46 ।।
iti śrīvāyupurāṇe vāyuprokte gayāmāhātmyaṃ nāma saptacatvāriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ।। 47 ।।
agniṣvāttā barhiṣadaḥ somapāḥ pitṛdevatāḥ ।। 48.10 ।।  
ābrahmastambaparyantaṃ devarṣipitṛmānavāḥ।  
mātā pitāmahī caiva tathaiva prapitāmahī ।। 48.23 ।।  
āvāhyābhyarcya tebhyaśca piṇḍāndadyādyathākramam ।। 48.32 ।।  
atra snāto divaṃ yāti svaśarīreṇa mānavaḥ ।। 49.6 ।।  
ātmānaṃ tārayetsadyo daśa pūrvāndaśāparān ।। 49.23 ।।  
śrāddhaṃ kṛtvāhavanīye aśvamedhaphalaṃ labhet ।। 49.60 ।।  
gayāyāṃ dharmmapṛṣṭe ca sarasi brahmaṇasta thā ।। 49.97 ।।
