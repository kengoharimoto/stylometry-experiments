varṣāṇi tasmāt daśa bhaviṣyati pañcāśatam samāḥ ṣaḍ ca sātakarṇiḥ bhaviṣyati caturvviṃśat tu varṣāṇi ṣaḍ samāḥ vai bhaviṣyati bhavitā nemikṛṣṇaḥ tu varṣāṇām pañcaviṃśatim tatas saṃvatsaram pūrṇam hālaḥ rājā bhaviṣyati iti ete vai nṛpāḥ triṃśat andhrāḥ bhokṣyanti ye mahīm sapta gardabhinaḥ ca api tatas atha daśa vai śakāḥ yavana aṣṭau bhaviṣyanti tuṣārāḥ tu caturdaśa trayodaśa maruṇḍāḥ ca maunāḥ hi aṣṭādaśa eva tu andhrāḥ bhokṣyanti vasudhām śate dve ca śatam ca vai śatāni trīṇi aśītiṃca bhokṣyanti vasudhām śakāḥ aśītiṃ ca eva varṣāṇi bhoktāraḥ yavanāḥ mahīm pañca varṣa śatāni iha tuṣārāṇām mahī smṛtā maruṇḍāḥ vṛṣalaiḥ sārddham bhāvyāḥ anyāḥ mleccha jātayaḥ śatāni trīṇi bhokṣyanti mlecchāḥ ekādaśa eva tu tad channena ca kālena tatas kolikilāḥ vṛṣāḥ samāḥ ṣaṇṇavatim trātvā pṛthivīm ca sameṣyati sadācandraḥ tu candrāṃśaḥ dvitīyaḥ nakhavān tathā dhanadharmā tatas ca api caturthaḥ viṃśa jaḥ smṛtaḥ tasya bhrātā yavīyān tu nāmnā nandiyaśāḥ kila dohitraḥ śiśukaḥ nāma purikāyām nṛpaḥ abhavat vindhyaśakti sutaḥ ca api pravīraḥ nāma vīryavān bhokṣyanti ca samāḥ ṣaṣṭim purīm vai yakṣyanti vājapeyaiḥ ca samāpta vara dakṣiṇaiḥ vindhyakānām kule atīte nṛpāḥ vai bāhlikāḥ strayaḥ śakyamāḥ nāma vai rājā māhiṣīnām mahīpatiḥ puṣpamitrāḥ bhaviṣyanti paṭṭamitrāḥ trayodaśa mekalāyām nṛpāḥ sapta bhaviṣyanti ca sattamāḥ rājānaḥ bhaviṣyanti mahā balāḥ utsādya pārthivān sarvān saḥ anyān varṇān kariṣyati kaivarttān pañcakān ca eva pulindān brāhmaṇān tathā sthāpayiṣyanti rājānaḥ nānā deśeṣu tejasā viśvasphāniḥ narapatiḥ klībā kṛtiḥ iva ucyate devān pitṝn ca viprān ca tarpayitvā sakṛt punar jāhnavī tīram āsādya śarīram yasyate balī saṃnyasya sva śarīran tu śakra lokam gamiṣyati nava nākāḥ tu bhokṣyanti purīm campāvatīm nṛpāḥ mathurām ca purīm ramyām nāgāḥ bhokṣyanti sapta vai etān janapadān sarvvān bhokṣyanti maṇidhānya jāḥ kośalān ca andhra pauṇḍrān ca tāmra liptān sa sāgarān campām ca eva purīm ramyām bhokṣyanti deva rakṣitām kāliṅgāḥ mahiṣāḥ ca eva mahendra nilayāḥ ca ye etān janapadān sarvān pālayiṣyati vai guhaḥ strī rāṣṭram bhakṣyakān ca eva bhokṣyate kanakāhvayaḥ alpa prasādāḥ hi anṛtāḥ mahā krodhāḥ hi adhārmikāḥ na eva mūrddhābhiṣiktāḥ te bhaviṣyanti narādhipāḥ yuga doṣa durācārāḥ bhaviṣyanti nṛpāḥ tu te taiḥ vimiśrāḥ janapadāḥ mleccha ācārāḥ ca sarvaśas lubdha anṛta ratāḥ ca eva bhavitāraḥ tadā nṛpāḥ teṣām vyatīte paryāye bahustrīke yuge tadā lavāt lavam bhraśyamānāḥ āyū rūpa bala śrutaiḥ tathā gatāḥ tu vai kāṣṭām prajāsu jagatī īśvarāḥ rājānaḥ sampraṇaśyanti kālena upahatāḥ tadā adhārmikāḥ ca te atyartham pāṣaṇḍāḥ ca eva sarvaśas pranaṣṭe nṛpa śabde ca sandhyā āśliṣṭe kalau yuge kiṃñcid śiṣṭāḥ prajāḥ tāḥ vai dharmme naṣṭe aparigrahāḥ asādhanāḥ hata āśvāsāḥ vyādhi śokena pīḍitāḥ anāvṛṣṭi hatāḥ ca eva paraspara vadhena ca anāthāḥ hi paritrastāḥ vārttām utsṛjya duḥkhitāḥ tyaktvā purāṇi grāmān ca bhaviṣyanti vanaukasaḥ evam nṛpeṣu naṣṭeṣu prajāḥ tyaktvā gṛhāṇi tu naṣṭe snehe durāpannāḥ bhraṣṭa snehāḥ suhṛd janāḥ varṇa āśrama paribhraṣṭāḥ saṅkaram ghoram āsthitāḥ saritaḥ sāgara anūpān sevante parvatāni ca aṅgān kaliṅgān vaṅgān ca kāśmīrān kāśi kośalān ṛṣikānta giri droṇīḥ saṃśrayiṣyanti mānavāḥ kṛtsnam himavataḥ pṛṣṭham kūlam ca lavaṇāmbhasaḥ mṛgaiḥ mīnaiḥ vihaṅgaiḥ ca śvāpadaiḥ takṣubhiḥ tathā madhu śāka phalaiḥ mūlaiḥ varttayiṣyanti mānavāḥ cīram parṇam ca vividham valkalāni ajināni ca bīja annāni tathā kāṣṭha śaṅkubhiḥ aja eḍakam khara uṣṭram ca pālayiṣyanti yatnataḥ nadīḥ vatsyanti toya arthe kūlam āśritya mānavāḥ bahu manyāḥ prajā hīnāḥ śauca ācāra vivarjitāḥ hīnāt hīnān tathā dharmmān prajāḥ samanuvarttate āyuḥ tadā trayoviṃśam na kaścid ativarttate durbalāḥ viṣaya glānāḥ jarayā saṃpariplutāḥ patra mūla phala āhārāḥ cīra kṛṣṇa ajina ambarāḥ etad kālam anuprāptāḥ prajāḥ kali yuga antake niḥśeṣāḥ tu bhaviṣyanti sārddham kali yugena tu sa sandhyāṃśe tu niḥśeṣe kṛtam vai pratipatsyate mahādeva abhiṣekāt tu janma yāvat parikṣitaḥ pramāṇam vai tathā ca uktam mahāpadma antaram ca yat antaram tat śatāni aṣṭau ṣaṭtriṃśat ca samāḥ smṛtāḥ etad kāla antaram bhāvyāḥ andhra antāḥ ye prakīrttitāḥ bhaviṣyaiḥ tatra saṅkhyātāḥ purāṇa jñaiḥ śruta ṛṣibhiḥ saptarṣayaḥ tadā prāhuḥ pratīpe rājñi vai śatam saptaviṃśaiḥ śataiḥ bhāvyāḥ andhrāṇām te tvayā punar saptarṣayaḥ tu tiṣṭhanti paryāyeṇa śatam śatam saptarṣīṇām yugam hi etat divyayā saṅkhyayā smṛtam sā sā divyā smṛtā ṣaṣṭiḥ divyā ahnāḥ ca eva saptabhiḥ saptarṣīṇām tu ye pūrvāḥ dṛśyante uttarā diśi tena saptarṣayaḥ yuktāḥ jñeyāḥ vyomni śatam samāḥ nakṣatrāṇām ṛṣīṇām ca yogasya etat nidarśanam saptarṣayaḥ maghā yuktāḥ kāle pārikṣite mohitāḥ saṃsaktāḥ ca bhaviṣyanti śūdrāḥ sārddham dvijātibhiḥ brāhmaṇāḥ śūdra yaṣṭāraḥ śūdrāḥ vai mantra yonayaḥ lavam lavam bhrasyamānāḥ prajāḥ sarvāḥ krameṇa tu kṣayam eva gamiṣyanti kṣīṇa śeṣāḥ yuga kṣaye yasmin kṛṣṇaḥ divam yātaḥ tasmin eva tadā dine sahasrāṇām śatāni iha trīṇi mānuṣa saṅkhyayā ṣaṣṭim ca eva sahasrāṇi varṣāṇām ucyate kaliḥ brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyāḥ vaiśyāḥ śūdrāḥ ca eva anvaye smṛtāḥ yuge yuge mahātmānaḥ samatītāḥ sahasraśas bahu tvāt nāmadheyānām parisaṃkhyā kule kule eva āyāntu yuga ākhyāyām yatas kṣatram prapatsyate devāpiḥ pauravaḥ rājā ikṣvākoḥ ca eva yaḥ mataḥ gotrāṇām
