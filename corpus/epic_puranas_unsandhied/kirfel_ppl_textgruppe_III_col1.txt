ca api ajījanat chāyā saṃjñā dadau śāpam yamāya kupitā yadā tadā anyā iyam asau buddhiḥ iti āsīt yama sūryayoḥ tatas vivasvān ākhyāṭe tayā eva araṇya saṃsthītām samādhi dṛṣṭyā dadṛśe tām aśvām tapasi sthitām vāji rūpa dharaḥ saḥ api tasyām devau atha āśvinau janayāmāsa revantam retasaḥ ante ca bhāskaraḥ āninye ca punar saṃjñām svasthānām bhagavān raviḥ tejasāḥ śamanam ca asyāḥ viśvakarmā cakāra ha bhramim āropya sūryam tu tasya tejaḥ viśātanam kṛtavān aṣṭamam bhāgam na vyaśātayata vyayam yat sūryāt vaiṣṇavam tejaḥ śātitam viśvakarmaṇā jājvalyamānam apatat tat bhūmau muni sattama tvaṣṭā eva tejasā tena viṣṇoḥ cakram akalpayat triśūlam ca eva rudrasya śibikām dhanadasya ca śaktīm guhasya devānām anyeṣām ca yat āyudham tat sarvam tejasā tena viśvakarmā vyavardhayat chāyā saṃjñā sutaḥ yaḥ asau dvitīyaḥ kathitaḥ manuḥ pūrvajasya savarṇaḥ asau sāvarṇiḥ tena sā ucyate tasya manvautaram hi etat sāvarṇakam atha aṣṭamam tat śṛṇuṣva mahābhāga bhaviṣyam kathayāmi te sāvarṇiḥ tu manuḥ yaḥ asau maitreya bhavitā tatas sutapāḥ c-āmitābhāḥ ca mukhyāḥ ca api tadā surāḥ teṣām gaṇaḥ tu devānām ekaikaḥ viṃśakaḥ su ṛtaḥ saptarṣīn api vakṣyāmi bhaviṣyān muni sattama dīptīmān gālavaḥ rāmaḥ kṛpaḥ drauṇiḥ tathā aparāḥ mad putraḥ tu tathā vyāsaḥ ṛṣyaśṛṅgaḥ ca saptamaḥ viṣṇu prasādāt anaghaḥ pātāla antara gocarāḥ virocana sutaḥ teṣām baliḥ indraḥ bhaviṣyati virajāḥ ca nirmoha ādyāḥ tathā apare sāvarṇasya manoḥ putrāḥ bhaviṣyanti nareśvarāḥ navamaḥ dakṣasāvarṇaḥ maitreya bhavitā manuḥ pārāḥ marīcīgarbhāḥ ca sudharmāṇaḥ tathā tridhā bhaviṣyanti tadā devāḥ ekaikaḥ dvādaśaḥ gaṇaḥ teṣām indraḥ mahā vīryaḥ bhaviṣyati adbhutaḥ dvija savanaḥ dyutimān bhavyaḥ vasuḥ medhādhṛtiḥ tathā jyotiṣmān saptamaḥ satyaḥ tatra ete ca mahā ṛṣayaḥ dhṛtaketuḥ dīptiketuḥ pañcahastaḥ nirāmayaḥ pṛthuśrava ādyāḥ ca tathā dakṣasāvarṇaka ātmajāḥ daśamaḥ brahmā asāvarṇiḥ bhaviṣyati mune manuḥ sudhāmānaḥ viruddhāḥ ca śatasaṃkhyāḥ tathā surāḥ indrāḥ ca bhavitā śāntiḥ nāma mahā balaḥ saptarṣayaḥ bhaviṣyanti ye tadā tām śṛṇuṣva ca haviṣmān sukṛtiḥ satyaḥ hi apāmmūrtiḥ tathā aparāḥ nābhāgaḥ apratimaujāḥ ca satyaketuḥ tathā eva ca sukṣetraḥ c-a uttamaujāḥ ca hariṣeṇa ādayaḥ daśa brahmāsāvarṇa putrāḥ tu rakṣiṣyanti vasuṃḍharām ekādaśaḥ ca bhavitā manuḥ vihaṃgamāḥ kāmagamāḥ nirmāṇaratayaḥ tathā gaṇāḥ tu ete tadā mukhyāḥ devānām ca bhaviṣyatām ekaikaḥ triṃśakaḥ teṣām gaṇaḥ ca indrāḥ ca vai vṛṣaḥ niścaraḥ ca agni tejāḥ ca vapuṣmān viṣṇuḥ āruṇiḥ haviṣmān anaghaḥ ca ete bhāvyāḥ saptarṣayaḥ tathā sarvagaḥ sarvadharmā ca devānīka ādayaḥ tathā bhaviṣyanti manoḥ tasya tanayāḥ pṛthiviśvarāḥ rudraputraḥ tu sāvarṇaḥ bhavitā dvādaśaḥ manuḥ ṛṭadhāmā ca tatra indraḥ bhavitā śṛṇu me surān haritāḥ lohitāḥ devāḥ tathā sumanasaḥ dvija sukarmāṇaḥ ca tārāḥ ca daśakāḥ pañca vai gaṇāḥ tapasvi sutapāḥ ca eva tapomūrtiḥ taporatiḥ tapodhṛtiḥ dyutiḥ ca anyaḥ saptamaḥ tu tapodhanaḥ devavān upadevaḥ ca devaśreṣṭha ādayaḥ tathā manoḥ tasya mahā vīryāḥ bhaviṣyanti sutāḥ nṛpāḥ trayodaśaḥ raucya nāmā bhaviṣyati mune manuḥ sutrāmāṇaḥ sudharmāṇaḥ sukarmāṇaḥ tathā aparāḥ trayastriṃśat vibhedāḥ te devānām ye tu vai gaṇāḥ divaspatiḥ mahā vīryaḥ teṣām indraḥ bhaviṣyati nirmohaḥ tattvadarśī ca niṣprakampaḥ nirutsukaḥ dhṛtimān avyayaḥ ca anyaḥ saptamaḥ sutapāḥ muniḥ saptarṣayaḥ tu ime tasya putrān api nibodha me citrasena vicitra ādyāḥ bhaviṣyanti mahīkṣitāḥ bhautyaḥ caturdaśaḥ ca atra maitreya bhavitā manuḥ śuciḥ indraḥ sura gaṇāḥ tatra pañca śṛṇuṣva tān cākṣuṣāḥ ca pavitrāḥ ca kanīṣṭhāḥ bhṛājirāḥ tathā vacaḥ vṛddhāḥ ca vai devāḥ saptarṣīn api me śṛṇu agnibāhiḥ suciḥ śukraḥ māgadhaḥ agnīdhraḥ eva ca yuktaḥ tathā jitāḥ ca anyaḥ manu putrān atas śṛṇu uruḥ gabhīra bradhna ādyāḥ manoḥ tasya sutāḥ nṛpāḥ kathitāḥ muni śārdūla pālayiṣyanti ye mahīm catur yuga aute vedānām jāyate kila saṃplavaḥ pravartayanti tān etya bhuvi saptarṣayaḥ divaḥ kṛte kṛte smṛteḥ vipra praṇetā jāyate manuḥ devāḥ yajñabhujaḥ te tu yāvat manvantaram tu tat bhavanti ye manoḥ putrāḥ yāvat manvantaram tu taiḥ tad anvaya udbhava īḥ ca eva tāvat bhūḥ paripālyate manuḥ saptarṣayaḥ devāḥ bhūpālāḥ ca manoḥ sutāḥ manvantare bhavanti ete śakraḥ ca eva adhikāriṇaḥ caturdaśabhiḥ etaiḥ tu gataiḥ manvantaraiḥ dvija sahasra yuga paryantāḥ kalpaḥ niḥśeṣaḥ ucyate tāvat pramāṇā ca niśā tatas bhavati sattama brahma rūpa dharaḥ śete śeṣa ahau ambu saṃplave trailokyam akhilam grastvā bhagavān ādi kṛt vibhuḥ sva māyā saṃṣthitaḥ vipra sarva bhūtaḥ janārdanaḥ tatas prabuddhaḥ bhagavān yathā pūrvam tathā punar sṛṣṭim karoti avyaya ātmā kalpe kalpe rajaḥ guṇaḥ manavaḥ bhūbhujaḥ sa indrāḥ devāḥ saptarṣayaḥ tathā sātvikaḥ aṃśaḥ sthiti karaḥ jagataḥ
