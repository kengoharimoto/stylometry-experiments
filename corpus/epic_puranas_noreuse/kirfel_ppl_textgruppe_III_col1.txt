## 3. Abschnitt. Manvantara | B.  (Vi. III. 2.1—60).

maitreya uvāca: proktāny etāni bhavatā sapta manvantarāni vai. |
manur yamo yamī caiva tadapatyāni vai mune. || 2 ||
tadānyeyam asau buddhir ity āsīd yama-sūryayoḥ. || 5 ||
janayāmāsa revantaṃ retaso 'nte ca bhāskaraḥ. || 7 ||
bhrānim āropya sūryaṃ tu tasya tejoviśātanaṃ |
jājvalyamānam apatat tad bhūmau munisattama. || 10 ||
tasya manvautaraṃ hy etat sāvarṇakam. athaṣṭamaṃ |
sāvarṇis tu manur yo 'sau, maitreya, bhavitā tataḥ |
saptarṣin api vakṣyāmi bhaviṣyān munisattama: || 16 ||
teṣām indro mahāvīryo bhaviṣyaty adbhuto dvija. || 21 ||
savano) dyutimān bhavyo vasur medhādhṛtis) tathā |
jyotiṣmān saptamaḥ satyas tatraite ca maharṣayaḥ. || 22 ||
daśamo brahmāsāvarṇir bhaviṣyati mune manuḥ |
haritā lohitā devās tathā sumanaso dvija |
tapasvi sutapās caiva tapomṛtis taporatiḥ |
trayodaśo raucyanāmā bhaviṣyati mune manuḥ. |
nirmohas tattvadarsī ca niṣprakampo nirutsukaḥ |
saptarṣayas tv ime; tasya putrān api nibodha me: |
citrasena-vicitrādyā bhaviṣyanti mahīkṣitāḥ. || 39 ||
bhautyaś caturdaśaś cātra maitreya bhavitā manuḥ, |
pravartayanti tān etya bhuvi saptarṣayo divaḥ. || 44 ||
devā yajñabhujas te tu, yāvan manvantaraṃ tu tat. || 45 ||
bhavanti ye manuḥ putrā, yāvan manvantaraṃ, tu taiḥ |
tadanvayodbhavaīś caiva tāvad bhūḥ paripālyate. || 46 ||
caturdaśabhir etais tu gatair manvantarair dvija |
sahasrayugaparyantāḥ kalpo niḥṣeṣa ucyate. || 48 ||
trailokyam akhilaṃ grastvā bhagavān ādikṛd vibhuḥ |
sātviko 'mṣaḥ sthitikaro jagato dvijasattama. || 52 ||
yugavyavasthāṃ kurute yathā, maitreya tat śṛṇu! || 53 ||
duṣṭānāṃ nigrahaṃ kurvan paripāti jagattrayaṃ. || 55 ||
