      ṛṣaya ūcuḥ।।  
  
  
vistarāvayavañcaiva nisargasya mahātmanaḥ।  
vistareṇānupūrvyā ca sarvameva bravīhi me ।। 38.4 ।।  
  
         sūta uvāca।।  
bhavatāṃ kathayiṣyāmi sarvametadyathātatham।  
  
manorvaivasvatasyemaṃ sāṃpratasya mahātmanaḥ।  
  
pralayañcaiva lokānāṃ bruvato me nibodhata ।। 38.7 ।।  
  
etānyuktāni vai samyak saptasaptasu vai mayā।  
  
sāvarṇasya pravakṣyāmi manorvaivasvatasya ha।  
bhaviṣyasya bhaviṣyanti samāsāttu nibodhata ।। 38.9 ।।  
  
  
  
  
  
ritastapaśca śukraśca dyutirjyotiḥ prabhākarau ।। 38.14 ।।  
  
  
prabhurvibhurvibhāsaśca jetā hantā'rihā rituḥ।  
  
  
damo dātā vidaḥ somo vittavaidyau yamo nidhiḥ।  
  
  
sāmpratasya bhaviṣyanti sāvarṇasyāntare manoḥ ।। 38.20 ।।  
  
teṣāmindro bhaviṣyastu balirvairocanaḥ purā।  
  
sāvarṇasya manoḥ putrā bhaviṣyanti navaiva tu ।। 38.22 ।।  
  
  
  
  
  
          ṛṣaya ūcuḥ।।  
bhavettu brahmaṇaścaiva dharmeṇa ca mahātmanaḥ ।। 38.27 ।।  
  
         sūta uvāca।।  
  
jajñire manavaste hi bhaviṣyānāgatāntare ।। 38.29 ।।  
  
prācetasasya dakṣasya dauhitrā manavastu ye।  
  
  
vaivasvate'ntare prāpte samutpattistayoḥ śubhā।  
  
  
  
prajābhistapasā caiva vistaraṃ teṣu vakṣyate।  
  
manvantarādhikāreṣu vartante ca sakṛt sakṛt।  
  
  
  
  
punaruktā bahutvāttu vakṣye na teṣu vistaram।  
manvantareṣu bhāvyeṣu bhūteṣvapi tathaiva ca ।। 39.40 ।।  
  
  
sarvakanyāvaśiṣṭā tu śreṣṭā dharmaparā sutā।  
  
vairājastamupāsīnaṃ dharmeṇa ca bhavena ca।  
  
dakṣa kanyā taveyaṃ vai janayiṣyati suvratā।  
  
brahmaṇo vacanaṃ śrutvā dakṣo dharmo bhavastadā।  
  
  
upabhogasamarthaiśca sadyojātaiḥ śarīrakaiḥ ।। 38.47 ।।  
  
saṃrabdhā vai vyakarṣanta mama putro mametyuta ।। 38.48 ।।  
  
abhidhyānānmanotpannānūcurvai te parasparam।  
yo yasya vapuṣā tulyo bhajatāṃ sa tu taṃ sutam ।। 38.49 ।।  
  
  
dhruvaṃ rūpaṃ pituḥ putraḥ so'nurudhyati sarvadā।  
  
  
  
ruceḥ prajāpateḥ putro raucyo nāmābhavatsutaḥ ।। 38.54 ।।  
  
bhūtyāmutpādito yastu bhautyo nāmābhavatsutaḥ।  
vaivasvate'ntare rājā dvau manū tu vivasvatoḥ ।। 38.55 ।।  
  
  
  
  
prathamaṃ merusāvarṇerdakṣaputrasya vai manoḥ।  
sambhūtāśca mahātmānaḥ sarve vaivasvate'ntare ।। 38.59 ।।  
  
dakṣaputrasya putrāste rohitasya prajāpateḥ।  
  
  
vājiyo vājijiccaiva prabhṛtiśca kakudyatha।

varṇastathāpyaṅgaviśvau muraṇyo vrajano mataḥ।  
amito dravaketuśca jambhosthājasraśakrakāḥ ।। 38.64 ।।  
  
  
  
  
sutapāḥ paulavaścaiva saptaite rohitāntare।

varyyoditastathā jiṣṭo varcasvī dyutimān haviḥ।

devairdevāstrayastriṃśatpṛthaktvena nibodhata ।। 38.103 ।।  
  
  
divaspatirmahāsattvasteṣāmindro bhaviṣyati ।। 38.105 ।।  
  
  
paulahastattva darśī ca bhārgavaśca nirutsakaḥ।  
niṣprakampastathātreyo nirmohaḥ kaśyapastathā ।। 38.107 ।।  
  
citraseno vicitraśca tapo dharmadhṛto bhavaḥ ।। 38.108 ।।  
  
anekakṣatrabaddhaśca suraso nirbhayaḥ pṛthaḥ।  
raucyasyaite manoḥ putrā hyantare tu trayodaśe ।। 38.109 ।।  
  
caturddaśe tu paryāye bhautasyāpyantare manoḥ ।  
  
  
saptaiva tāṃstān bhāgāṃśca viddhi cākṣuṣasaṃjñakān।  
  
  
trailokye yāni sattvāni gatimanti dhruvāṇi ca।  
sarvaśaḥ svairguṇaistāni indrāste'bhibhavanti vai ।। 38.114 ।।  
  
  
ojasvī subalaścaiva bhautyasyaite manoḥ sutāḥ ।। 38.116 ।।  
  
eko vaivasvataścaiva sāvarṇo manurucyate ।। 38.117 ।।  
  
raucyo bhautyaśca yau tau tu manoḥ paulahabhārgavau।  
bhautyasyaivādhipatye tu pūrṇaḥ kalpastu pūryyate ।। 38.118 ।।  
  
           sūta uvāca।।  
ante'nekayuge tasmin kṣīṇe saṃhāra ucyate ।। 38.119 ।।  
  
saptaite bhārgavā devā ante manvantare tadā।  
bhuktvā trailokyamadhyasthā yugākhyāṃ hyekasaptatim ।। 38.120 ।।  
  
pitṛbhirmanubhiścaiva sārddhaṃ saptarṣibhistu ye।  
yajvānaścaiva te'pyanye tadbhāktāścaiva taiḥ saha ।। 38.121 ।।  
  
maharlokaṃ gamiṣyanti tyaktvā trailokyamīśvarāḥ।  
tatasteṣu gateṣūrddhvaṃ kṣīṇe manvantare tadā।  
anādhāramidaṃ sarvaṃ trailokyaṃ vai bhaviṣyati ।। 38.122 ।।  
  
  
tatasteṣu vyatīteṣu trailokyasyeśvareṣviha।  
  
jitādyāśca gaṇā hyatra cākṣuṣāntāścaturdaśa।  
manvantareṣu sarvveṣu devāste vai mahaujasaḥ ।। 38.125 ।।  
  
sametya devāste sarve prāpte saṃkalane tadā ।। 38.126 ।।  
  
maharlokaṃ parityajya gaṇāste vai caturddaśa।  
  
  
  
  
tatra yugasahasrāntamaharyardbrahmaṇo viduḥ।  
  
naimittikaḥ prākṛtiko yaścaivātyantiko'rthataḥ।  
  
brāhmo naimittikastasya kalpadāhaḥ prasaṃyamaḥ।  
  
  
aharante prakurute sargasya pralayaṃ punaḥ।  
  
tato yugasahasrānte saṃprāpte ca yugakṣaye।  
  
  
tānyevātra pralīyante bhūmitvamupayānti ca।  
  
asahyaraśmirbhagavān pibannambho gabhastibhiḥ।  
  
bhūya eva vivartante vyāpnuvanto vanaṃ śanaiḥ।  
  
tasmādudakaṃ sūryyasya tapato'ti hi kathyate।  
  
tasmādapaḥ piban yā vai dīpyate ravirambare ।। 38.142 ।।  
  
tasya te raśmayaḥ sapta pibantyambho mahārṇavāt।  
  
caturlokamimaṃ sarvaṃ dahanti śikhinastadā ।। 38.144 ।।  
  
  
khaṃ samāvṛtya tiṣṭhanti nirdahanto vasundharām ।। 38.146 ।।  
  
  
adhaścordhvaśca tiryak ca saṃruddhaṃ sūryaraśmibhiḥ ।। 38.148 ।।  
  
ekatvamupayātānāmekajvālaṃ bhavatyuta ।। 38.149 ।।  
  
caturlokamidaṃ sarvaṃ nirdahatyāśu tejasā ।। 38.150 ।।  
  
  
  
tatastāni pralīyante bhūmitvamupayānti ca ।। 38.153 ।।  
  
sarvvaṃ tadbhasmasāccakre sarvvātmā pāvakastu saḥ ।। 38.154 ।।  
  
samudrebhyo nadībhyaśca pātālebhyaśca sarvataḥ।  
pibannapaḥ samiddho'gniḥ pṛthivīmāśrito jvalan ।। 38.155 ।।  
  
lokān saṃharate dīpto ghoraḥ saṃvarttako'nalaḥ ।। 38.156 ।।  
  
  
  
udatiṣṭhañchikhāstasya bahvayaḥ saṃvarttakasya tu।  
tadā dahati sandīpto golakaṃ caiva sarvvaśaḥ ।। 38.159 ।।  
  
bhūrlokantu bhuvarlokaṃ svarlokañca mahastathā।  
  
  
  
  
  
  
indragopanibhāḥ keciduttiṣṭhanti ghanā divi।  
  
  
tadā jaladharāḥ sarve pūrayanti nabhaḥsthalam ।। 38.168 ।।  
  
  
tataste jaladā varṣaṃ muñcanti ca mahodyamam।  
  
  
plāvayanti jagatsarvaṃ bṛhajjālaparisravaiḥ ।। 38.172 ।।  
  
anye tu salilaughaistu velāmabhibhavantyapi।  
sādrirdvīpāntaraṃ pṛthvī hyadbhiḥ saṃchādyate tadā ।। 38.173 ।।  
  
tasya vṛṣṭyā ca toyaṃ tatsarvvaṃ hi parimaṇḍitam।  
praviśatyudadhau viprāḥ pītaṃ sūryyasya raśmibhiḥ ।। 38.174 ।।  
  
  
  
tatastu sahasodbhrāntaḥ payodāṃstānnabhastale।  
  
  
  
prasarantyo vrajantyekaṃ salilākhyāṃ bhajantyuta ।। 38.180 ।।  
  
  
bhasma sarvvamanuprāpya tasmādambho nirucyate ।। 38.182 ।।  
  
nānātve caiva śīghre ca dhāturvai ara ucyate।  
  
tasmin yugasahasrānte divase brahmaṇo gate।  
  
  
sahasrabāhuḥ prathamaḥ prajāpatistrīyapathe yaḥ puruṣo nirucyate ।। 38.186 ।।

caturyugasahasrānte sarvataḥ salilaplute।  
  
  
janalokavivarttantastapasā labdhacakṣuṣaḥ।

tato rātrikṣaye prāpte pratibuddhaḥ prajāpatiḥ।  
  
evaṃ saloke nirvṛtte upaśānte prajāpatau।

samāhani ca rātrau ca muhūrtto vai dvinālikau ।। 38.221 ।।  
  
  
  
etaddivyamahorātramiti śāstraviniścayaḥ।  
  
  
  
etacchrutvā tu ṛṣayo vismayaṃ paramādbhutam।
