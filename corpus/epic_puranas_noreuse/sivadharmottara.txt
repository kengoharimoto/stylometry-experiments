oṃ namaḥ śivāya

deśabhāṣādyupāyaiśca bodhayet sa guruḥ smṛtaḥ
tathā vidyāpradānasya nāntaḥ sarvaguṇātmakaḥ

vyastaiḥ padair upetasya punaruktasya cārthataḥ

brahmalokātparibhraṣṭaṣṭhecchiva pure sukhī
manuṣyalokāmāsādya śivayogī bhavetpunaḥ
tataḥsa yogavidhanā punardhyāyati śaṅkaraṃ
prāptayogatanuntyaktvā śivasthānaṃ paraṃ brajet
naitattapobhiratyugrairnna ca sarvairmahāmakhaiḥ
samprāpya paramaṃ sthānaṃ śivayogādvasennaraḥ
tasmādantarbahiś cintāṃ paribhājya vicakṣaṇaḥ
atha yogaṃ vināpyetat sthānamekena labhyate
śivapriyeṇa vidhinā nānyairvidhiśatairapi
sahasraṃ śiva bhaktānāṃ bhojayīta daśottaraṃ
tataḥ sahasra dogdhrīṇāṅgavāṃ śvetaṃ śitaṃ tathā
garbhiṇyarddhena sammiśraṃ vṛṣabhair daśabhir yutaṃ
pūjitaṃgandhapuṣpādyaiś cailakaṇṭhamalaṅkṛtaṃ
gavāṃ mantraṃ samuccārya japettāsāṃ puraḥ sthitaḥ
gandhapuṣpākṣatonmiśrāṃ gṛhītvā tāmrakarkkarīṃ
śṛṅge pucchaṃ pradhānāyā goś ca snāpyatadambhasā
raupyapātre tu tattoyaṃ pratigṛhaṇa nayettataḥ
tairevasārdhdaṃ viprendrairmahatā tumulena ca
bhṛtyaputrakalatrādyairyuktaḥsvajanabandhubhiḥ
te'pi tair ādimadhyānte kutyuḥ pūjāntu pustake
pravartayati yaḥ kaś cicchivapustakavācanaṃ
sarvalokopakārārthamātmanaś ca vimuktaye
vācanānte jagacchāntimavadhārya dine dine
mitrasvajanabandhūnāmante bhṛtyajanasya ca
jñānapuṇyaṃ mahāśāntaṃ tattasmādakṣayaṃ phalaṃ
bandubhiḥ svajanaiḥ snigdhair bhṛtyaiḥ dāsaiḥ samāśritaiḥ
ity etaiḥ sahitaḥ sarvaiḥ śrīmac chivapure vaset
tatra bhuṅkte mahābhogānicchayā śivavadvaśī
pralayānte tataḥ prāpya jñānayogamanuttamaṃ
yasmād evam ataḥ kuryāc chivapustakavācanaṃ
bhogāpavargaphaladaṃ śivabhakto dine dine
tatra deśe bhaven nityaṃ sarveṣaṃ dehināṃ śivaṃ
śivāya namaḥ
