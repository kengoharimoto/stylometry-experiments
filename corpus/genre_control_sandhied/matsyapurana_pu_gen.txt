aṣṭāviṃśatisutastasya sulomā vai bhaviṣyati
navaviṃśati varṣāṇi yajñaḥ śrī śāntikarṇikaḥ
caṇḍaśrīḥ śāntikarṇastu tasya putra samādaśa
sulomā saptavarṣāṇi anyasteṣāṃ bhaviṣyati
adhārmikāścaye'pyarthaṃ pāṣaṇḍāścaiva sarvaśaḥ
praṇaṣṭe nṛpavaṃśe tu sandhyā śiṣṭe kalau yuge
kiñcicchiṣṭāḥ prajāstā vai dharme naṣṭe'parigrahāḥ
sasandhyāṃśe suniḥ śeṣe kṛtaṃ tu pratipatsyate
evaṃ vaṃśakramaḥ kṛtsnaḥ kīrtito yo mayā kramāt
atītā varttamānāśca tathaivānāgatāśca ye
niḥśeṣetu tadā tasmin kṛtaṃ vai pratipatsyate
ailaścekṣvākuvaṃśaśca sahadevaḥ prakīrttitāḥ
ikṣvākoḥ saṃsmṛtaṃ kṣatraṃ sumitrāntaṃ bhaviṣyati
ailaṃ kṣatraṃsamākrāntaṃ somavaṃśavido viduḥ
ete vivasvataḥ putrāḥ kīrtitāḥ kīrtivardhanāḥ
atītā vartamānāśca tathaivānāgatāśca ye
devāpiḥ pauravo rājā aikṣvāko yaśca te mataḥ
navaviṃśe yuge so vai vaṃśasyādirbhaviṣyati
devāpiputraḥ satyastu ailānāṃ bhavitā nṛpaḥ
evaṃ sarveṣu vijñeyaṃ santānārthantu lakṣaṇam
saptarṣayo nṛpaiḥ sārddhaṃ santānārthaṃ yuge yuge
manvantarāṇāṃ santāne santānāśca śrutau smṛtāḥ
yathā praśāntisteṣāṃ vai prakṛtīnāṃ yathākṣayaḥ
jāmadagnyena rāmeṇa kṣatreniravaśeṣite
dvivaṃśakaraṇaṃ sarvaṃ kīrtayiṣye nibodha me
ailañcekṣvākuvaṃśañca prakṛtiṃ paricakṣate
rājānaḥ śreṇibaddhāśca tathānte kṣatriyā bhuvi
ailavaṃśāstu bhūyāṃso na tathekṣvākavo nṛpāḥ
eṣāmekaśataṃ pūrṇaṃ kulānāmabhirocate
etadvaḥ rājarṣayo ye tu atītāste yugaiḥ saha
ye te yayāti vaṃśyānāṃ ye ca vaṃśā viśāmpate
āyuḥ kīrtiṃ dhanaṃ svargaṃ putravāṃścābhijāyate
dhāraṇācchravaṇāccaiva paraṃ svargasya dhīmataḥ
tulāpuruṣa yāgo'yaṃ yeṣāmādau vidhīyate
yugādiṣu parāgeṣu tathā manvantarādiṣu
saṅkrāntau tau vaidhṛtidine caturdaśyaṣṭamīṣu ca
vidhānadakṣāḥ paṭavo'nukūlā ye cāryyadeśaprabhavā dvijendrāḥ
guruśca vedāntavidāryyavaṃśa samudbhavaḥ śīlakulābhirūpaḥ
purāṇaśāstrābhirato'tidakṣaḥ prasannagambhīrasarasvatīkaḥ
grāmaṃ vā viṣayaṃ vāpi yadanyadapi sambhavet
pureṣu lokapālānāṃ pratimanvantaraṃ vaset
matimapi ca janānāṃ yo dadāti priyārthaṃ vibudhapatijanānāṃ nāyakaḥ syādamogham
bhūtānmajjyāṃśca manujāṃstārayeddoṣasaṃyutān
stūyamāno divaḥ pṛṣṭha putrapautraśrapautrakaiḥ
vimānenārkavarṇaiḥ viṣṇulokaṃ sa gacchati
athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi mahādānamanuttamam
puṇyāṃ tithiṃ samāsādya yugamanvantarādikīm
payovrataṃ trirātraṃ syādekarātramathāpi vā
śatamekottarantadvat pitṝṇāṃ tārayed budhaḥ
mātāmahānāṃ tadvacca putrapautrasamanvitaḥ
yāvatkalpaśatantiṣṭet rājarājo bhavet punaḥ
vidyādharāmaramunīndragaṇābhijuṣṭaṃ prāpnotyasau padamatīndriyamindumauleḥ
nayati sa pitṛputrān bāndhavānapyaśeṣān kṛtagajarathadānācchāśvataṃ sadma viṣṇoḥ
athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi mahādānamanuttamam
vidhinānena tasyāśu kṣīyate pāpasaṃhatiḥ
yāvanti lāṅgalakamārgamukhāni bhūmeḥ bhāsāṃpaterduhituraṅgaja romakāṇi
tāvanti śaṅkarapure sa samā hi tiṣṭet bhūmipradānamiha yaḥ kurute manuṣyaḥ
pāpakṣayakaraṃ nṛṇāmamaṅgalyavināśanam
kārayetputrivīṃ haimīṃ jambūdvīpāṃnukariṇīm
lokapālāṣṭakopetāṃ navavaṣesamanvitām
nārāyaṇapuraṃ gatvā kalpatrayamathā''vaset
pitṛnputrāṃśca pautrāṃśca tārayedekaviṃśatim
iti paṭhati ya itthaṃ yaḥ śṛṇoti prasaṅgādapi kaluṣavitānairmuktadehaḥ samantāt
trayodaśa udānastu gāruḍo'tha caturdaśaḥ
kaurmaḥ pañcadaśaḥ proktaḥ paurṇamāsyāmajāyata
ṣoḍaśo nārasiṃhastu samānastu tato'paraḥ
madanadvādaśī tadvallokapālābhipūjanam
manvantarāṇāmuddeśo vainyarājābhivarṇanam
pitṛvaṃśānukathanaṃ śrāddhakālastathaiva ca
kīrtanaṃ somavaṃśasya yayāticaritaṃ tathā
kārtavīryasya mahātmyaṃ vṛṣṇivaṃśānukīrtanam
bhṛguśāpastathā viṣṇorddaityaśāpastathaiva ca
kīrtanaṃ puruṣeśasya vaṃśo hautāśanastathā
vrataṃ nakṣatrasaṃkhyākaṃ mārtaṇḍaśayanaṃ tathā
bhuvanāni surendrāṇāṃ tripurādhoṣaṇaṃ tathā
pitṛpiṇḍadamāhātmyaṃ manvantaravinirṇayam
vajrāṅgasya tu saṃbhūtiḥ tārakotpattireva ca
