bhartuḥ śuśrūṣaṇe 'raṇyaṃ svayaṃ ca tapase yayau
samjñeyam ity athārkaś ca chāyāyām ātmajatrāyaṃ
sanaiścaraṃ manuṃ cānyaṃ tapatīṃ cāpy ajīṇat
chāyāsamjñā dadau śapaṃ yamāya kupitā yadā
tadānyeyam asau buddhir ity āsīd yamasūryayoḥ
tato vivasvān ākhyaṭe tayaivāraṇyasamsthītāṃ
samādhidṛṣtyā dadṛṣe tāṃ aśvāṃ tapasi sthitāṃ
vājirūpadharaḥ so 'pi tasyāṃ devāṃ athāśvinau
janayāmāsa revantaṃ retaso 'nte ca bhāskaraḥ
āninye ca punaḥ samjñāṃ svasthānāṃ bhagavān raviḥ
tejasāḥ śamanaṃ cāsyā viśvakarmā cakāra ha
bhrānim āropya sūryaṃ tu tasya tejoviśātanaṃ
kṛtavān aṣṭamaṃ bhāgaṃ na vyaśātatayātāvyayaṃ
yat sūryād vaiṣṇavaṃ tejaḥ śātitaṃ viśvakarmaṇā
jājvalyamānam apatat tad bhūmau munisattama
tvaṣṭaiva tejasā tena viṣṇoś cakram akalpayat
triśūlaṃ caiva rudrasya śibikāṃ dhanadasya ca
śaktiṃ guhasya devānām anyeṣāṃ ca yad āyudhaṃ
tat sarvaṃ tejasā tena viśvakarmā vyavardhayat
chāyāsamjñāsuto yo 'sau dvitiyaḥ kathito manuḥ
pūrvajasya savarṇo 'sau sāvarṇis tena socyate
tasya manvautaraṃ hy etat sāvarṇakam athaṣṭamaṃ
tac chṛnuṣva mahābhāga bhaviṣyaṃ kathayāmi te
sāvarṇis tu manur yo 'sau maitreya bhavitā tataḥ
sutapās cāmitābhās ca mukhyās cāpi tadā surāḥ
teṣāṃ gaṇas tu devānām ekaiko vimśakaḥ suṛtaḥ
saptarṣin api vakṣyāmi bhaviṣyān munisattama
diptimān gālavo rāmaḥ kṛpo drauṇis tathāparāḥ
matputras tu tathā vyāsa ṛṣyaśṛiṇgās ca saptamaḥ
viṣṇuprasādād anaghaḥ pātālāntaragocarāḥ
virocanasutas teṣāṃ balir indro bhaviṣyati
virajās carvarivāmś ca nirmohādyās tathāpare
sāvarṇasya manoḥ putrā bhaviṣyanti nareśvarāḥ
navamo dakṣasāvarṇo maitreya bhavitā manuḥ
pārā maricigarbhās ca sudharmāṇas tathā tridhā
bhaviṣyanti tadā devā ekaiko dvādaśo gaṇaḥ
teṣām indro mahāvīryo bhaviṣyaty adbhuto dvija
savano dyutimān bhavyo vasur medhādhṛtis tathā
jyotiṣmān saptamaḥ satyas tatraite ca maharṣayaḥ
dhṛtaketur dīptiketuḥ pañcahasto nirāmayaḥ
pṛthuśravādyās ca tathā dakṣasāvarṇakātmajāḥ
daśamo brahmāsāvarṇir bhaviṣyati mune manuḥ
sudhāmāno viruddhās ca śatasamkhyās tathā surāḥ
teṣam indrās ca bhavitā śāntir nāma mahābalaḥ
saptarṣayo bhaviṣyanti ye tadā tāṃ śṛṇuṣva ca
haviṣmān sukṛtiḥ satyo hy apāmṛtis tathāparāḥ
nābhāgo 'pratimaujās ca satyaketus tathaiva ca
sukṣetras cottamaujās ca hariṣeṇādayo daśa
brahmāsāvarṇaputrās tu rakṣiṣyanti vasumḍharāṃ
ekādaśas ca bhavitā dharmāsāvarṇiko manuḥ
vihaṃgamāḥ kāmagamā nirmāṇaratayas tathā
gaṇās tv ete tadā mukhyā devānāṃ ca bhaviṣyatāṃ
ekaikas trimśakas teṣāṃ gaṇas cendrās ca vai vṛṣaḥ
niścaras cāgnitejās ca vapuṣmān viṣṇur āruṇiḥ
haviṣmān anaghas caite bbāvyāḥ saptarṣayas tathā
sarvagaḥ sarvadharmā ca devānikādayas tathā
bhaviṣyanti manos tasya tanayāḥ pṛthiviśvarāḥ
rudraputras tu sāvarṇo bhavitā dvādaśo manuḥ
ṛṭadhāmā ca tatrendro bhavitā śṛṇu me surān
haritā lohitā devās tathā sumanaso dvija
sukarmāṇas ca tārās ca daśakāḥ pañca vai gaṇaḥ
tapasvi sutapās caiva tapomṛtis taporatiḥ
tapodhṛtir dyutis cānyaḥ saptamas tu tapodhanaḥ
devavān upadevas ca devaśreṣṭhādayas tathā
manos tasya mahāvīryā bhaviṣyanti sutā nṛpāḥ
trayodaśo raucyanāmā bhaviṣyati mune manuḥ
sutramāṇaḥ sudharmāṇaḥ sukarmāṇas tathāparāḥ
trayastriṃśadvibhedās te devānāṃ ye tu vai gaṇaḥ
divaspatir mahāvīryas teṣām indro bhaviṣyati
nirmohas tattvadarsī ca niṣprakampo nirutsukaḥ
dhṛtimān avyayas cānyaḥ saptamaḥ sutapā muniḥ
saptarṣayas tv ime tasya putrān api nibodha me
citrasenavicitrādyā bhaviṣyanti mahīkṣitāḥ
bhautyaś caturdaśaś cātra maitreya bhavitā manuḥ
śucir indraḥ suragaṇās tatra paṅca śṛṇuṣva tān
cāksuṣāś ca pavitrāś ca kanīṣṭhā bhṛājirās tathā
vacovṛddhās ca vai devāḥ saptarṣin api me śṛṇu
agnibāhiḥ suciḥ śukro māgadho 'gnidhra eva ca
yuktas tathā jitās cānyo manuputrān ataḥ śṛṇu
urur gabhīrabradhnādyā manos tasya sutā nṛpāḥ
kathitā munīṣārdūla pālayiṣyanti ye mahīṃ
caturyugāute vedānāṃ jāyate kila samplavaḥ
pravartayanti tān etya bhuvi saptarṣayo divaḥ
kṛte kṛte smṛter vipra praṇetā jāyate manuḥ
devā yajñabhujas te tu yāvan manvantaraṃ tu tat
bhavanti ye manuḥ putrā yāvan manvantaraṃ tu taiḥ
tadanvayodbhavaīś caiva tāvad bhūḥ paripālyate
manuḥ saptarṣayo devā bhūpālās ca manuḥ sutāḥ
manvantare bhavanty ete śakraś caivādīkārīṇaḥ
caturdaśabhir etais tu gatair manvantarair dvija
sahasrayugaparyantāḥ kalpo niḥṣeṣa ucyate
tāvatpramāṇā ca niṣā tato bhavati sattama
brahmarūpadharaḥ śete śeṣāhāv ambusaṃplave
trailokyam akhilaṃ grastvā bhagavān ādikṛd vibhuḥ
svamāyāsaṃsthito vipra sarvabhūto janārdanaḥ
tatalḥ prabuddho bhagavān yathā pūrvaṃ tathā punaḥ
sṛṣṭiṃ karoty avyayātmā kalpe kalpe rajogunaḥ
manavo bhūbhujaḥ sendrā devāḥ saptarṣayas tathā
sātviko 'mṣaḥ sthitikaro jagato dvijasattama
caturyuge 'py asau viṣṇuḥ sthitivyāpāralakṣaṇaḥ
yugavyavasthāṃ kurute yathā maitreya tat śṛṇu
kṛte yuge paraṃ jñānaṃ kapilādīsvarūpadhṛk
dadāti sarvabhūtānāṃ sarvabhūtahite rataḥ
cakravartisvarūpeṇa tretāyām api sa prabhuḥ
duṣṭānāṃ nigrahaṃ kurvan paripāti jagattrayaṃ
vedam ekaṃ caturbhedaṃ kṛtvā śākhāśatair vibhuḥ
karoti bahulaṃ bhūyo vedavyāsasvarūpadhṛk
vedāṃs tu dvāpare vyasya kaler ante punar hariḥ
kalkisvarūpi durvṛttān mārge sthāpayati prabhuḥ
evam eṣa jagat sarvaṃ paripāti karoti ca
hanti cānteṣv anantātmā nāsty asmād vyatireki yat
bhūtāṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca sarvabhūtān mahātmanaḥ
tad atrānyatra vā vipra sadbhāvaḥ kathitas tava
manvantaraṇy aṣeṣāṇi kathitāni mayā tava
manvantarādhipās caiva kim anyat kathayāmi te
