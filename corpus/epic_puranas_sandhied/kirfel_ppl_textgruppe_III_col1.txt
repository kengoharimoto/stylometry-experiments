maitreya uvāca proktāny etāni bhavatā sapta manvantarāni vai
bhaviṣyāny api viprarṣe mamākhyāṭuṃ tvam arhasi
parāśara uvāca sūryasya patnī saṃjñābhūt sutā yā viśvakarmaṇāḥ
manur yamo yamī caiva tadapatyāni vai mune
asahanti tu sā bhartus tejaś chāyāṃ yuyoja vai
bhartuḥ śuśrūṣaṇe 'raṇyaṃ svayaṃ ca tapase yayau
saṃjñeyam ity athārkaś ca chāyāyām ātmajatrāyaṃ
sanaiścaraṃ manuṃ cānyaṃ tapatīṃ cāpy ajījanat
chāyāsaṃjñā dadau śāpaṃ yamāya kupitā yadā
tadānyeyam asau buddhir ity āsīd yamasūryayoḥ
tato vivasvān ākhyāṭe tayaivāraṇyasaṃsthītāṃ
samādhidṛṣṭyā dadṛśe tāṃ aśvāṃ tapasi sthitāṃ
vājirūpadharaḥ so 'pi tasyāṃ devāv athāśvinau
janayāmāsa revantaṃ retaso 'nte ca bhāskaraḥ
āninye ca punaḥ saṃjñāṃ svasthānāṃ bhagavān raviḥ
tejasāḥ śamanaṃ cāsyā viśvakarmā cakāra ha
bhramim āropya sūryaṃ tu tasya tejoviśātanaṃ
kṛtavān aṣṭamaṃ bhāgaṃ na vyaśātayatavyayaṃ
yat sūryād vaiṣṇavaṃ tejaḥ śātitaṃ viśvakarmaṇā
jājvalyamānam apatat tad bhūmau munisattama
tvaṣṭaiva tejasā tena viṣṇoś cakram akalpayat
triśūlaṃ caiva rudrasya śibikāṃ dhanadasya ca
śaktīṃ guhasya devānām anyeṣāṃ ca yad āyudhaṃ
tat sarvaṃ tejasā tena viśvakarmā vyavardhayat
chāyāsaṃjñāsuto yo 'sau dvitīyaḥ kathito manuḥ
pūrvajasya savarṇo 'sau sāvarṇis tena socyate
tasya manvautaraṃ hy etat sāvarṇakam athāṣṭamaṃ
tac chṛṇuṣva mahābhāga bhaviṣyaṃ kathayāmi te
sāvarṇis tu manur yo 'sau maitreya bhavitā tataḥ
sutapāś cāmitābhāś ca mukhyāś cāpi tadā surāḥ
teṣāṃ gaṇas tu devānām ekaiko viṃśakaḥ suṛtaḥ
saptarṣīn api vakṣyāmi bhaviṣyān munisattama
dīptīmān gālavo rāmaḥ kṛpo drauṇis tathāparāḥ
matputras tu tathā vyāsa ṛṣyaśṛṅgaś ca saptamaḥ
viṣṇuprasādād anaghaḥ pātālāntaragocarāḥ
virocanasutas teṣāṃ balir indro bhaviṣyati
virajāś carvarīvāṃś ca nirmohādyās tathāpare
sāvarṇasya manoḥ putrā bhaviṣyanti nareśvarāḥ
navamo dakṣasāvarṇo maitreya bhavitā manuḥ
pārā marīcīgarbhāś ca sudharmāṇas tathā tridhā
bhaviṣyanti tadā devā ekaiko dvādaśo gaṇaḥ
teṣām indro mahāvīryo bhaviṣyaty adbhuto dvija
savano dyutimān bhavyo vasur medhādhṛtis tathā
jyotiṣmān saptamaḥ satyas tatraite ca maharṣayaḥ
dhṛtaketur dīptiketuḥ pañcahasto nirāmayaḥ
pṛthuśravādyāś ca tathā dakṣasāvarṇakātmajāḥ
daśamo brahmāsāvarṇir bhaviṣyati mune manuḥ
sudhāmāno viruddhāś ca śatasaṃkhyās tathā surāḥ
teṣam indrāś ca bhavitā śāntir nāma mahābalaḥ
saptarṣayo bhaviṣyanti ye tadā tāṃ śṛṇuṣva ca
haviṣmān sukṛtiḥ satyo hy apāmmūrtis tathāparāḥ
nābhāgo 'pratimaujāś ca satyaketus tathaiva ca
sukṣetraś cottamaujāś ca hariṣeṇādayo daśa
brahmāsāvarṇaputrās tu rakṣiṣyanti vasuṃḍharāṃ
ekādaśaś ca bhavitā dharmāsāvarṇiko manuḥ
vihaṃgamāḥ kāmagamā nirmāṇaratayas tathā
gaṇās tv ete tadā mukhyā devānāṃ ca bhaviṣyatāṃ
ekaikas triṃśakas teṣāṃ gaṇaś cendrāś ca vai vṛṣaḥ
niścaraś cagnitejāś ca vapuṣmān viṣṇur āruṇiḥ
haviṣmān anaghaś caite bhāvyāḥ saptarṣayas tathā
sarvagaḥ sarvadharmā ca devānīkādayas tathā
bhaviṣyanti manos tasya tanayāḥ pṛthiviśvarāḥ
rudraputras tu sāvarṇo bhavitā dvādaśo manuḥ
ṛṭadhāmā ca tatrendro bhavitā śṛṇu me surān
haritā lohitā devās tathā sumanaso dvija
sukarmāṇaś ca tārāś ca daśakāḥ pañca vai gaṇāḥ
tapasvi sutapāś caiva tapomūrtis taporatiḥ
tapodhṛtir dyutiś cānyaḥ saptamas tu tapodhanaḥ
devavān upadevaś ca devaśreṣṭhādayas tathā
manos tasya mahāvīryā bhaviṣyanti sutā nṛpāḥ
trayodaśo raucyanāmā bhaviṣyati mune manuḥ
sutrāmāṇaḥ sudharmāṇaḥ sukarmāṇas tathāparāḥ
trayastriṃśadvibhedās te devānāṃ ye tu vai gaṇāḥ
divaspatir mahāvīryas teṣām indro bhaviṣyati
nirmohas tattvadarśī ca niṣprakampo nirutsukaḥ
dhṛtimān avyayaś cānyaḥ saptamaḥ sutapā muniḥ
saptarṣayas tv ime tasya putrān api nibodha me
citrasenavicitrādyā bhaviṣyanti mahīkṣitāḥ
bhautyaś caturdaśaś cātra maitreya bhavitā manuḥ
śucir indraḥ suragaṇās tatra pañca śṛṇuṣva tān
cākṣuṣāś ca pavitrāś ca kanīṣṭhā bhṛājirās tathā
vacovṛddhāś ca vai devāḥ saptarṣīn api me śṛṇu
agnibāhiḥ suciḥ śukro māgadho 'gnīdhra eva ca
yuktas tathā jitāś cānyo manuputrān ataḥ śṛṇu
urur gabhīrabradhnādyā manos tasya sutā nṛpāḥ
kathitā munīśārdūla pālayiṣyanti ye mahīṃ
caturyugāute vedānāṃ jāyate kila saṃplavaḥ
pravartayanti tān etya bhuvi saptarṣayo divaḥ
kṛte kṛte smṛter vipra praṇetā jāyate manuḥ
devā yajñabhujas te tu yāvan manvantaraṃ tu tat
bhavanti ye manoḥ putrā yāvan manvantaraṃ tu taiḥ
tadanvayodbhavaīś caiva tāvad bhūḥ paripālyate
manuḥ saptarṣayo devā bhūpālāś ca manoḥ sutāḥ
manvantare bhavanty ete śakraś caivadhikāriṇaḥ
caturdaśabhir etais tu gatair manvantarair dvija
sahasrayugaparyantāḥ kalpo niḥśeṣa ucyate
tāvatpramāṇā ca niśā tato bhavati sattama
brahmarūpadharaḥ śete śeṣāhāv ambusaṃplave
trailokyam akhilaṃ grastvā bhagavān ādikṛd vibhuḥ
svamāyāsaṃṣthito vipra sarvabhūto janārdanaḥ
tataḥ prabuddho bhagavān yathā pūrvaṃ tathā punaḥ
sṛṣṭiṃ karoty avyayātmā kalpe kalpe rajoguṇaḥ
manavo bhūbhujaḥ sendrā devāḥ saptarṣayas tathā
sātviko 'ṃśaḥ sthitikaro jagato dvijasattama
caturyuge 'py asau viṣṇuḥ sthitivyāpāralakṣaṇaḥ
yugavyavasthāṃ kurute yathā maitreya tat śṛṇu
kṛte yuge paraṃ jñānaṃ kapilādīsvarūpadhṛk
dadāti sarvabhūtānāṃ sarvabhūtahite rataḥ
cakravartisvarūpeṇa tretāyām api sa prabhuḥ
duṣṭānāṃ nigrahaṃ kurvan paripāti jagattrayaṃ
vedam ekaṃ caturbhedaṃ kṛtvā śākhāśatair vibhuḥ
karoti bahulaṃ bhūyo vedavyāsasvarūpadhṛk
vedāṃs tu dvāpare vyasya kaler ante punar hariḥ
kalkisvarūpī durvṛttān mārge sthāpayati prabhuḥ
evam eṣa jagat sarvaṃ paripāti karoti ca
hanti cānteṣv anantātmā nāsty asmād vyatireki yat
bhūtāṃ bhavyaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ ca sarvabhūtān mahātmanaḥ
tad atrānyatra vā vipra sadbhāvaḥ kathitas tava
manvantarāṇy aśeṣāṇi kathitāni mayā tava
manvantarādhipāś caiva kim anyat kathayāmi te
