jahnus tv ajanayat putraṃ
surathaṃ nāma bhūmipaṃ
surathasya tu dāyādo
vīro rājā vidūrathaḥ
vidūrathasutās cāpi
sārvabhauma iti smṛtaḥ
sārvabhaumāj jayatsena
āradhis tasya cātmajaḥ
āradhito mahāsattva
ayutāyus tataḥ smṛtaḥ
akrodhano 'yutāyos tu
tasmād devātithiḥ smṛtaḥ
devātithes tu dāyāda
ṛkṣa eva babhūva ha
rṛksasya tu dvitīyasya
bhīmaseno 'bhavat sutaḥ
pratīpo bhīmasenāt tu
pratīpasya tu śamtanuḥ
devāpir bāhlikas caiva
traya eva mahārathāḥ
śamtanos tv abhavad bhīsmas
tasmin vamśe dvijottamāḥ
bhīmasenas tathā rṛksād
dilīpas tasya cātmajaḥ
dilīpasya pratīpas tu
tasya putrās trayaḥ smṛtāḥ
devāpiḥ śamtanus caiva
bāhlikas caiva te trayaḥ
bāhlikasya tu dāyaddaḥ sapta bāhliśvara nṛpaḥ
bāhlikasya sutas caiva somadatto mahāyasāḥ
jajñire somadattāt tu bhūrir bhūriśravāḥ śalaḥ
devāpis tu pravavrāja vanaṃ dharmaparīpsayā
upādhyāyas tu devānāṃ devāpir abhavan muniḥ
cyavanasya kṛtaḥ putra iṣṭas cāsin mahātmanaḥ
śamtanus tv abhavad rājā kauravānāṃ dhuramdharaḥ
śamtanoḥ sampravakṣyāmi
vamśaṃ trailokyaviśrutam
imaṃ codāharanty atra ślokaṃ prati mahābhiṣam
yaṃ yaṃ kārābhyāṃ spṛṣati jirṇaṃ rogiṇaṃ eva ca
punar yuvā sa bhavati tasmāt taṃ śamtanuṃ viduḥ
tat tasya śamtanutvaṃ hi prajābhir iha kīrtiyate
tato 'vṛnuta bhāryārthaṃ śamtanur jāhnaviṃ nṛpaḥ
tasyāṃ devavrataṃ nāma putraṃ so 'janayat prabhuḥ
sa tu bhīṣma iti khyātaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ pitāmahaḥ
kālī vicitravīryaṃ tu dāseyy ajanayat sutaṃ
śamtanor dayitaṃ putraṃ dharmamātmānam akalmaṣaṃ
kṛṣṇadvaipāyaṇaś caiva kṣetre vaicitravīryake
dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ ca pāṇḍuṃ ca viduraṃ cāpy ajījanat
dhṛtarāṣṭras tu gāndhāryāṃ putrān ajanayac chataṃ
teṣāṃ duryodhanaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ sarveṣām api sa prabhuḥ
mādrī rājñī pṛthā caiva pāṇḍor bhārye babhūvatuḥ
devadattāḥ sutās tābhyaṃ pāṇḍor arthe vijajñire
dharmād yudhiṣṭhīro jajñe vāyor jajñe vṛkodaraḥ
indrād dhanamjayo jajñe śakratulyaparākramaḥ
nakulaṃ sahadevaṃ ca mādṛy aśvibhyām ajījanat
pañcaiva pāṇḍavebhyaś ca draupadyāṃ jajñire sutāḥ
draupady ajanayaj jyeṣṭhaṃ prativindhyaṃ yudhiṣṭhirāt
śrutasenaṃ bhimasenāc chrutakīrtiṃ dhanamjayāt
caturthaṃ śrutakarmāṇaṃ sahadevād ajāyata
nakulāc ca śatānikaṃ draupadeyāḥ prakīrtitāḥ
tebhyo 'pare pāṇḍaveyāḥ ṣaḍ evānye mahārathāḥ
hidambā bhimasenāt tu jajñe putraṃ ghaṭotkacaṃ
kāsyā punar bhimasenāj jajñe vai sarvagaṃ sutaṃ
suhotraṃ vijayā mādrī sahadevād ajāyata
kareṇumatyāṃ caidyāyāṃ niramitras tu nākuliḥ
subhadrāyāṃ rathī pārthād abhimanyur ajāyata
yaudheyāṃ devakas caiva putro jajñe yudhiṣṭhirāt
pāṇḍor dhanamjayaḥ putraḥ
saubhadras tasya cātmajaḥ
abhimanyoḥ parīkṣit tu
pitā parīkṣitasya ha
abhimanyoḥ parīkṣit tu
putraḥ parapuramjayaḥ
parīkṣitas tu dāyādo
rājāsij janamejaḥ
parīkṣitasya kāsyāyāṃ dvau putrau saṃbabhūvatuḥ
candrāpiḍas tu nṛpatiḥ sūryāpiḍas ca mokṣavit
candrāpiḍasya putrānāṃ śatam uttamadhanvināṃ
jānamejayam ity evaṃ kṣātraṃ bhuvi parīśrutaṃ
teṣāṃ jyeṣṭhas tu tatrāsīt pure vāraṇasāhvaye
satyakarṇo mahābāhur yajvā vipuladakṣiṇaḥ
satyakarṇasya dāyādaḥ śvetakarṇaḥ pratāpavān
aputraḥ sa tu dharmātmā praviveśa tapovanaṃ
tasmād vanagatā garbhaṃ yādavī pratyapadyata
sucāror duhitā subhrūr mālini grāhamālini
saṃbhūte sa ca garbhe ca śvetakarṇaḥ prajeśvaraḥ
anvagacchat kṛtaṃ pūrvaṃ mahāprasthānam acyutaṃ
sā tu dṛṣtvā priyaṃ taṃ ca mālinī pṛṣṭhato 'nvagāt
sucāror duhitā sādhvī vane rājivalocanā
pathi sā suṣuve bālā sukumāraṃ kumārakaṃ
tam apāsyātha tatraiva rājānaṃ sānvagacchata
pativrataṃ mahābhāgā draupadīva purā satī
kumāraḥ sukumāro 'sau giripṛṣṭhe ruroda ha
dayārthaṃ tasya meghās tu prādurāsan mahātmanaḥ
śraviṣṭhāyās tu putrau dvau paippalādīs ca kauśikaḥ
dṛṣtvā kṛpānvitau gṛhya tau prākṣālayatāṃ jale
nighṛṣtau tasya pārsvau tu śilāyāṃ rudhiraplutau
ajaśyāmaḥ sa pārsvābhyāṃ ghṛṣṭābhyāṃ susamāhitaḥ
ajaśyāmau tu tatpārsvau devena samṛbabhūvatuḥ
athājapārsva iti vai cakrāte nāma tasya tau
sa tu remakāśālāyāṃ dvijābhyām abhivardhitaḥ
remakasya tu bhāryā tam udvahat putrakāraṇāt
remakyāḥ sa tu putro 'bhūd brāhmaṇau sacivau tu tau
teṣāṃ putrās ca pautrās ca yugapat tulyajīvinaḥ
sa eṣa pauravo vaṃśaḥ pāṇḍavānāṃ mahātmanām
śloko 'pi cātra gīto 'yaṃ nāhuṣeṇa yayātina
jarāsaṃkramaṇe pūrvaṃ tadā prītena dhīmatā
acandrāṛkagrahā bhūmir bhaved iyam asamṛśayam
apauravā mahī naiva bhaviṣyati kadācana
eṣa vaḥ pauravo vaṃśo vikhyātaḥ kathito mayā
