sutanur vaḍavā caiva dve ete paricārike
pauravī rohinī nāma bāhlīkasyātmajābhavat
jyeṣṭhā patnī mahābhāgā dayitānakadundubheḥ
lebhe jyeṣṭham sutaṃ rāmaṃ sāraṇam śaṭham eṣa ca
durdamaṃ damanaṃ śubhrāṃ piṇḍārakam uśīnaram
citrām nāma kumārīm ca rohinītanayā nava
vasudevāc ca devakyāṃ jajñe śaurir mahāyaśāḥ
rāmāc ca niśaṭho jajñe revatyāṃ dayitaḥ sutaḥ
pārśvī ca pārśvamardī ca śisuḥ satyadhṛtis tathā
mandabāhyo 'tha rāmāṇagirikau girir eṣa ca
śulkagulmo 'tigulmaś ca daridrāntaka eṣa ca
kumāryaś cāpi pañcānyā nāmatas tā nibodhata
arciṣmatī sunandā ca surasā suvacās tathā
tathā śatabalā caiva sāraṇasya sutās tv imāḥ
bhadrāśvo bhadraguptiś ca bhadraviṣṭas tathaiva ca
bhadrabāhur bhadraratho bhadrakalpas tathaiva ca
suparśvakaḥ kīrtimamś ca rohitaśvaḥ śatthatmajāḥ
durdamascabhibhūtas ca rohinyāḥ kulajāḥ smṛtāḥ
nandopanandau mitras ca kuksimitras tathā balaḥ
citropacitrau kṛtakas tuṣṭiḥ puṣṭir athapsaraḥ
madirāyāḥ sutā hy ete vasudevād vijajñire
upabimbo 'tha bimbas ca sattvadantamahaujasau
catvāra ete vikhyātā bhadrāputrā mahābalāḥ
vaiśākhya adadhāc chauriḥ putraṃ kauśikam uttamaṃ
devakyāṃ jajñire śaureḥ suṣenāḥ kīrtimān api
udāyī bhadrasenās ca ṛjudāyas ca pañcamaḥ
saṣṭho bhadravidehas ca kamsaḥ sarvān jaghāna tān
atha tasyām avasthāyām āyuṣmān saṃbabhūva ha
lokanāthaḥ punar viṣṇuḥ pūrvaṃ kṛṣṇaḥ prajāpatiḥ
anujātābhavat kṛṣṇā subhadrā bhadrabhāṣiṇī
kṛṣṇā subhadreti punar vyākhyātā vṛṣṇinandinī
subhadrāyāṃ rathī pārthād abhimanyur ajāyata
akrūrāt kāśikanyāyāṃ satyaketur ajāyata vasudevasya bhāryāsu mahābbhāgāsu saptasu
ye putrā jajñire śūrā nāmatas tān nibodhata
pūrvadyāḥ sahadevāyāṃ śūrad vai jajñire sutāḥ
śāntidevā janastamba śaurer jajñe kulodvahaṃ
upasaṅgaṃ vasuṃ cāpi tanayau devarakṣitā
evaṃ daśa sutās tasya kamsas tān apy aghātayāt
bhojaś ca vijayaś caiva śāntidevāsutāṅ ubhau
vṛkadevaḥ sudevāyām gadaś cāstāṃ sutāṅ ubhau
agāvaḥ mahātmānaṃ vṛkadevī vyajāyata
kanyā vigartarājasya bhāryā vai śīśirāyaṇe
saptamī devakī putraṃ suṇāmā suṣuve sutam
gaveṣaṇaṃ mahābhāgaṃ saṅgrāme citrayodhinaṃ
śrāddhadevyāṃ purodyāne vane tu vicaran dvijāḥ
vaiśyāyām adadhāc chauriḥ putraṃ kauśikam avyayaṃ
sugandhī vanarājī ca śaurer āstāṃ parigrahau
puṇḍraś ca kapilaś caiva sugandhyāś catmajau tu tau
tayo rājābhavat puṇḍraḥ kapilas tu vanaṃ yayau
anyasyām abhavat viro vasudevātmaṃ bali
jara nāma niṣādo 'sau prathamāḥ sa dhanurdharaḥ
uddhavo devabhāgasya mahābhāgaḥ suto 'bhavat
paṇḍitānāṃ param prāhur devaśravasam uttamam
aśmakyaṃ prāptavān putram anādṛṣtir yaśasvinaṃ
nivṛttaśatruṃ śatrughnaṃ śraddhadevaṃ mahābalaṃ
ajāyata śraddhadevo naiśādhir yaḥ pariśrutaḥ
ekālvyo mahāvīryo niśādhaiḥ parivardhitaḥ
ganḍūśāya tv aputrāya viśvakseno dadau sutān
cārudeṣṇaṃ sudeṣṇaṃ ca
pañcālaṃ kṛtalakṣaṇaṃ
asaṃgrāmeṇa yo viro nāvartata kadācana
raukmiṇeyo mahābāhuḥ kaniyān dvijasattamāḥ
vāyasānāṃ sahasrāṇi yaṃ yāntaṃ pṛṣṭhato 'nvayuḥ
cārūn adyopabhokṣyāmaś cārudeṣṇahatān iti
tantrījas tantrīpālas ca sutau kanavakasya tau
vṛkāya vai tv aputrāya vasudevaḥ pratāpavān
saumiṃ dadau sutaṃ vīraṃ śauriḥ kauśikam eva ca
vīruś cāśvahanuś caiva vīrau tāv atha gṛñjimau
śyāmaputraḥ śamīkas tu śamīko rājyam āvahat
jugupsamāno bhojatvād rājasūyam avāpa saḥ
ajātaśatruḥ śatrūnāṃ jajñe tasya vināśanaḥ
jugupsamāno bhojatvaṃ rājarṣitvam avāptavān jijñāsāṃ pauruṣe cakre na caskande ca pauruṣaṃ
kṛṣṇāyasasamaprakhyo varṣe dvādaśame tathā
mithyābhiśasto gārgyas tu manyunābhisamīritaḥ
gopakanyām upādāya maithunāyopacakrame
gopālī tv apsarās tasya gopastrīveṣadhārīṇī
dhārayāmāsa gārgyasya garbhaṃ durdharam acyutaṃ
maṇuṣyāṃ gārgyabhāryāyāṃ niyogāc chūlapāṇinaḥ
sa kālayavano nāma jajñe rājā mahābalaḥ
vṛṣapūrvārdhakāyās tam avahan vājino raṇe
aputrasya sa rājñas tu vavṛdhe 'ntaḥpure śiṣuḥ
yavanasya muniśreṣṭhāḥ sa kālayavano 'bhavat
sa yuddhakāmo nṛpatiḥ paryapṛcchad dvijottamān
vṛṣṇyandhakakulaṃ tasya nārado 'kathayad vibhuḥ
akṣauhiṇyā tu sainyasya mathurām abhyayāt tadā
dūtaṃ saṃpreṣayāmāsa vṛṣṇyandhakaniveśanaṃ
tato vṛṣṇyandhakāḥ kṛṣṇaṃ puraskṛtya mahāmatiṃ
sametā mantrayāmasur yavanasya bhayāt tadā
kṛtvā ca niścayaṃ sarve palāyanam arocayan
vihāya mathurāṃ ramyāṃ mānayantaḥ pinākinaṃ
kuśasthalīṃ dvāravatīṃ niveṣayitum ipsavaḥ
iti kṛṣṇasya janniedaṃ yaḥ śucir niyatendriyaḥ
parvasu śrāvayed vidvān anṛnaḥ sa sukbi bhavet
ya idaṃ janma kṛṣṇasya paṭheta niyatavrataḥ
śrāvayed brāhmaṇas cāpi sumahat sukhāṃ āpnuyāt
