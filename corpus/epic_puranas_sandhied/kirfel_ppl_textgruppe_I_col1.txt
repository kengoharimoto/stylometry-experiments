kṛtavarmāgrajas teṣāṃ śatadhanvā tu madhyamaḥ
devāntāś ca narāntāś ca devārhaś cā subāhuś ca bhiṣag vaitaraṇāś ca yaḥ bhiṣag vaitaraṇāś ca yaḥ
sudāntāś catidāntāś ca sudāntāś cadhidāntāś ca nikāśyaḥ kāmadambhakaḥ kanakaḥ kanakodbhavaḥ
devāntasyābhavat putro devārhasya suto vidvāñ vidvān kambalabarhiṣaḥ jajñe kambalabarhiṣaḥ
asamaujāḥ sutas tasya nāsamaujāś ca tāv ubhau
ajātaputrāya sutān pradadāv asamaujase
sudamṣtram ca sucārum ca kṛṣṇam ity andhakāḥ śmṛtāḥ
andhakānām imaṃ vaṃśaṃ kirtayed yas tu nityaśaḥ
ātmano vipulaṃ vaṃśaṃ labhate nātra saṃśayaḥ
aśmakyām janayāmāsa śūram vai devamīḍhuṣam
mahiṣyām jajñire śūrād bhojāyām puruṣā daśa
vasudevo mahābāhuḥ pūrvam ānakadundubhiḥ
jajñe yasya prasūtasya dundubhyaḥ prāṇadan divi
ānakānāṃ ca sambrādaḥ sumahān abhavad divi
papāta puṣpavarṣaṃ ca śūrasya bhavane mahat
manuṣyaloke kṛtsne 'pi rūpe nāsti samo bhuvi
yasyāṣīt puruṣāgṛyasya kān'tiś candramaso yathā
devabhāgas tato jajñe tato devaśravāḥ punaḥ
anādhṛṣṭiḥ kanavako anādhṛṣṭivṛkaś caiva vatsavān atha gṛñjamaḥ nandanaś caiva śṛñjayaḥ
śyāmaḥ śamīko gaṇḍūṣaḥ pañca cāsyā varāṅganāḥ
pṛthukīrtiḥ pṛthā caiva pṛthā ca śrutadevā ca śrutadevā śrutaśravā śrutakīrtiḥ śrutaśravā
rājādhīdevī ca tathā pañcaitā vīramātarāḥ
pṛthāṃ duhitaraṃ cakre kuntis tāṃ pāṇḍur avahat
śūraḥ pūjyāya vṛddhāya kuntibhojāya tāṃ dadau
tasmāt kuntīti vikhyātā kuntibhojātmajā pṛthā
kuruvīraḥ pāṇḍumukhyas tasmād bhāryām avindata
pṛthā jajñe tataḥ putrāṃs trīn agnisamatejasaḥ
loke 'pratirathān vīrāñ śakratulyaparākrāmān
yasyāṃ sa dharmavid rājā dharmād yudhiṣṭhiraṃ putraṃ dharmaj jajñe yudhiṣṭhiraḥ mārutāc ca vṛkodaraṃ
bhīmasenas tathā vātād indrāc caiva dhanamjayaḥ
loke 'pratiratho vīraḥ śakratulyaparākramaḥ
pṛthukīrtyāṃ tu samjajñe tanayo vṛddhaśarmaṇaḥ
karūṣādhipatir vīro dantavakro mahābalaḥ
kaikeyyāṃ śrutakīrtyāṃ tu jajñe samṭardano balī
cekitānabṛhatkṣatrau tathaivānyau mahābalau
vindānuvindāv āvantyau bhrātarau sumahābalau
śrutaśravāyāṃ caidyas tu śiśupālo babhūva ha
hīraṇyakaśipur yo 'sau daityarājo 'bhavat purā
damaghoṣasya rājarṣeḥ putro vikhyātapauruṣaḥ
yaḥ purā sa daśagrīvaḥ sambabhūvārimardanaḥ
vaiśravaṇānujas tasya kumbhakarṇo 'nujas tathā
vasudevasutān vīrān kīrtayiṣyāmy ataḥ paraṃ
vṛṣṇes trividham evaṃ tu bahuśākhāṃ mahaujasaṃ
dhārayan vipulaṃ vaṃśaṃ nānarthair iha yujyate
yāḥ patnyo vasudevasya caturdaśa varāṅganāḥ patnyas tu vasudevasya trayodaśa varāṅganāḥ pauravī rohiṇī nāma madirā cāparā tathā
vaiśākhī ca tathā bhadrā sunāmnī caiva pañcamī
sahadevā śāntidevā śrīdevā devarakṣitā
vṛkadevopadevā ca devakī caiva saptamī
sutanur vaḍavā caiva dve ete paricārike
pauravī rohiṇī nāma bāhlīkasyātmajābhavat
jyeṣṭhā patnī mahābhāgā dayitanakadundubheḥ
lebhe jyeṣṭham sutaṃ rāmaṃ sāraṇam śaṭham eva ca
durdamaṃ damanaṃ śubhrāṃ piṇḍārakam uśīnaram
citrām nāma kumārīṃ ca rohiṇītanayā nava
vasudevāc ca devakyāṃ jajñe śaurir mahāyaśāḥ
rāmāc ca niśaṭho jajñe revatyāṃ dayitaḥ sutaḥ
pārśvī ca pārśvamardī ca śiśuḥ satyadhṛtis tathā
mandabāhyo 'tha rāmāṇagirikau girir eva ca
śulkagulmo ''tigulmaś ca daridrāntaka eva ca
kumāryaś cāpi pañcānyā nāmatas tā nibodhata
arciṣmatī sunandā ca surasā suvacās tathā
tathā śatabalā caiva sāraṇasya sutās tv imāḥ
bhadrāśvo bhadraguptiś ca bhadraviṣṭas tathaiva ca
bhadrabāhur bhadraratho bhadrakalpas tathaiva ca
supārśvakaḥ kīrtimāṃś ca rohitāśvaḥ śaṭhatmajāḥ
durdamaścābhibhūtaś ca rohinyāḥ kulajāḥ smṛtāḥ
nandopanandau mitraś ca kukṣimitras tathā balaḥ
citropacitrau kṛtakas tuṣṭiḥ puṣṭir athāpsaraḥ
madirāyāḥ sutā hy ete vasudevād vijajñire
upabimbo 'tha bimbaś ca sattvadantamahaujasau
catvāra ete vikhyātā bhadrāputrā mahābalāḥ
vaiśākhya adadhāc chauriḥ putraṃ kauśikam uttamaṃ
devakyāṃ jajñire śaureḥ suṣeṇāḥ kīrtimān api
udāyī bhadrasenāś ca ṛjudāyaś ca pañcamaḥ
ṣaṣṭho bhadravidehaś ca kaṃsaḥ sarvāñ jaghāna tān
atha tasyām avasthāyām āyuṣmān saṃbabhūva ha
lokanāthaḥ punar viṣṇuḥ pūrvaṃ kṛṣṇaḥ prajāpatiḥ
anujātābhavat kṛṣṇā subhadrā bhadrabhāṣiṇī
kṛṣṇā subhadreti punar vyākhyātā vṛṣṇinandinī
subhadrāyāṃ rathī pārthād abhimanyur ajāyata
akrūrāt kāśikanyāyāṃ satyaketur ajāyata vasudevasya bhāryāsu mahābhāgāsu saptasu
ye putrā jajñire śūrā nāmatas tān nibodhata
pūrvadyāḥ sahadevāyāṃ śūrad vai jajñire sutāḥ
śāntidevā janastambaṃ śaurer jajñe kulodvahaṃ
upasaṅgaṃ vasuṃ cāpi tanayau devarakṣitā
evaṃ daśa sutās tasya kaṃsas tān apy aghātayāt
bhojaś ca vijayaś caiva śāntidevāsutāv ubhau
vṛkadevaḥ sudevāyāṃ gadaś cāstāṃ sutāv ubhau
agāvaḥ mahātmānaṃ vṛkadevī vyajāyata
kanyā vigartarājasya bhāryā vai śīśirāyaṇe
saptamī devakī putraṃ suṇāmā suṣuve sutam
gaveṣaṇaṃ mahābhāgaṃ saṃgrāme citrayodhinaṃ
śrāddhadevyāṃ purodyāne vane tu vicaran dvijāḥ
vaiśyāyām adadhāc chauriḥ putraṃ kauśikam avyayaṃ
sugandhī vanarājī ca śaurer āstāṃ parigrahau
puṇḍraś ca kapilaś caiva sugandhyāś catmajau tu tau
tayo rājābhavat puṇḍraḥ kapilas tu vanaṃ yayau
anyasyām abhavad vīro vasudevātmajo balī
jarā nāma niṣādo 'sau prathamāḥ sa dhanurdharaḥ
uddhavo devabhāgasya mahābhāgaḥ suto 'bhavat
paṇḍitānāṃ paraṃ prāhur devaśravasam uttamam
aśmakyāṃ prāptavān putram anādṛṣṭir yaśasvinaṃ
nivṛttaśatruṃ śatrughnaṃ śrāddhadevaṃ mahābalaṃ
ajāyata śrāddhadevo naiṣādhir yaḥ pariśrutaḥ
ekalavyo mahāvīryo niṣādhaiḥ parivardhitaḥ
gaṇḍūṣāya tv aputrāya viṣvakseno dadau sutān
cārudeṣṇaṃ śudeṣṇaṃ ca
pañcālaṃ kṛtalakṣaṇaṃ
asaṃgrāmeṇa yo vīro nāvartata kadācana
raukmiṇeyo mahābāhuḥ kanīyān dvijasattamāḥ
vāyasānāṃ sahasrāṇi yaṃ yāutaṃ pṛṣṭhato 'nvayuḥ
cārūn adyopabhokṣyāmaś cārudeṣṇahatān iti
tantrījas tantrīpālas ca sutau kanavakasya tau
vṛkāya vai tv aputrāya vasudevaḥ pratāpavān
saumiṃ dadau sutaṃ vīraṃ śauriḥ kauśikam eva ca
vīruś caśvahanuś caiva vīrau tāv atha gṛñjimau
śyāmaputraḥ śamīkas tu śamīko rājyam āvahat
jugupsamāno bhojatvād rājasūyam avāpa saḥ
ajātaśatruḥ śatrūṇāṃ jajñe tasya vināśanaḥ
jugupsamāno bhojatvaṃ rājarṣitvam avāptavān jijñāsāṃ pauruṣe cakre na caskande ca pauruṣaṃ
kṛṣṇāyasasamaprakhyo varṣe dvādaśame tathā
mithyābhiśasto gārgyas tu manyunābhisamīritaḥ
gopakanyām upādāya maithunāyopacakrame
gopālī tv apsarās tasya gopastrīveṣadhārīṇī
dhārayāmāsa gārgyasya garbhaṃ durdharam acyutaṃ
māṇuṣyāṃ gārgyabhāryāyāṃ niyogāc chūlapāṇinaḥ
sa kālayavano nāma jajñe rājā mahābalaḥ
vṛṣapūrvārdhakāyās tam avahan vājino raṇe
aputrasya sa rājñas tu vavṛdhe 'ntaḥpure śiśuḥ
yavanasya muniśreṣṭhāḥ sa kālayavano 'bhavat
sa yuddhakāmo nṛpatiḥ paryapṛcchad dvijottamān
vṛṣṇyandhakakulaṃ tasya nārado 'kathayad vibhuḥ
akṣauhiṇyā tu sainyasya mathurām abhyayāt tadā
dūtaṃ saṃpreṣayāmāsa vṛṣṇyandhakaniveśanaṃ
tato vṛṣṇyandhakāḥ kṛṣṇaṃ puraskṛtya mahāmatiṃ
sametā mantrayāmāsur yayanasya bhayāt tadā
kṛtvā ca niścayaṃ sarve palāyanam arocayan
vihāya mathurāṃ ramyāṃ mānayantaḥ pinākinaṃ
kuśasthalīṃ dvāravatīṃ niveśayitum īpsavaḥ
iti kṛṣṇasya janniedaṃ yaḥ śucir niyatendriyaḥ
parvasu śrāvayed vidvān anṛṇaḥ sa sukhī bhavet
ya idaṃ janma kṛṣṇasya paṭheta niyatavrataḥ
śrāvayed brāhmaṇaś cāpi sumahat sukhāṃ āpnuyāt
