nārāyaṇaṃ namaskṛtya naraṃ caiva narottamam
devīṃ sarasvatīṃ caiva tato jayam udīrayet
satyavatīhṛdayanandano vyāsaḥ
yasyāsyakamalagalitaṃ vāṅmadhupuṇyaṃ jagat pibati
dvaipāyanaṃ nāradaṃ ca mārkaṇḍeyaṃ tapodhanam
bhāradvājaṃ yājñavalkyaṃ harim uddiśya yatnavān
abhojayat svādu bhojyaṃ kīrtayitvā ca śārṅgiṇam
āhūya bharataśreṣṭha saṃniveśyāsane tadā
vartamāne vivāde tu vāstuvikrayaṇaṃ prati
dhanecchā yugapat prāptā kṣetrataḥ svāmibhūbhṛtām
prāptaṃ kaliyugaṃ jñātvā sahadevo hasann iva
rājñas tu kathayām āsa dharmo naṣṭas tu bhārata
rājā ca durmanā rājan paryāptaṃ jīvitaṃ mama
iti cintya gate rājan govinde dharmadhāraṇe
yathā ghanasya gagane śabdo bhavati bhārata
salile kṣipyamāṇasya gāṇḍīvasya tathā hy abhūt
jagmur bharataśārdūla tīreṇa lavaṇāmbhasaḥ
tataḥ punaḥ samāvṛttā jagmur dakṣiṇapaścimām
haripādāṅkavinyāsatīrthīkṛtamahābhuvam
manobhiḥ pūjayantaś ca janārdanam aninditāḥ
tatas te brāhmaṇīṃ snātvā dṛṣṭvā devaṃ jale sthitam
madhyamāṃśaṃ śivaṃ gatvā dṛṣṭvā rudraṃ mahābalam
prayāgapañcake snātvā gatvā himagiriṃ śubham
īśānaṃ tu namaskṛtya snātvā haṃsodake śubhe
devadevaṃ tu kedāraṃ dṛṣṭvā spṛṣṭvā prayatnataḥ
piṇḍaṃ dattvā vidhānena pitṝn devāṃś ca tarpya vai
udakaṃ vidhivat pītvā tato nandāṃ jagāma vai
mahāpanthānam āvṛtya himavantaṃ jagāma ha
sahadevo 'pi dharmātmā sarvabuddhimatāṃ varaḥ
kālatrayavidaś cāpi na me guhyaṃ prakāśitam
saṃpratijñāgataś cāhaṃ nākariṣyaṃ janakṣayam
bhītaṃ bhaktaṃ nānyad astīti cārtaṃ
prāptaṃ kṣīṇaṃ rakṣaṇe prāṇalipsum
prāṇatyāgād apy ahaṃ nota moktuṃ
yateyaṃ vai nityam etad vrataṃ me
tejāṃsi yāni dṛṣṭāni bhūmiṣṭhena tvayā vibho
veśmāni karmadevānāṃ paśyāmūni sahasraśaḥ
