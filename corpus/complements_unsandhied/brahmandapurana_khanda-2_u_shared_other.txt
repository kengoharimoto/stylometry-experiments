atha mātuḥ ca caraṇau abhivādya kṛtāñjaliḥ uvāca praṇataḥ vākyam tayoḥ saṃharṣa kāraṇam tat labdhvā tam namaskṛtya puṣkaram samupāgataḥ tatas praṇamya deveśau pārvatī parameśvarau jamadagniḥ uvāca idam rāmam śatru nibarhaṇam iti uktaḥ praṇipatya enam mātaram ca bhṛgūdvahaḥ sagara upākhyāne bhārgavacarite catuścatvāriṃśattamaḥ adhyāyaḥ jagmuḥ tṛṣā ārttāḥ madhyāhne saritam narmadām anu tatra snātvā ca pītvā ca vāri nadyāḥ gata śramāḥ vasati asmin sutaḥ yasya rāmaḥ śastrabhṛtām varaḥ parivārya mahā rāja ruruduḥ śoka karśitāḥ bhārgavacarite pañcacatvāriṃśattamaḥ adhyāyaḥ vilalāpa mahā bāhuḥ duḥkha śoka samanvitaḥ sa tān dṛṣṭvā abhivādya etān duḥkhitān duḥkha karśitaḥ hata śiṣṭāḥ nṛpatayaḥ dudruvuḥ sarvatodiśam pitṝn saṃtarpayāmāsa yathāśāstram atandritaḥ samantapañcakam nāma tīrtham loke pariśrutam yatra snātaḥ sarva tīrthaiḥ snātaḥ bhavati mānavaḥ kṛtakṛtyaḥ tatas rāmaḥ samyak pūrṇa manorathaḥ uvāsa tatra niyataḥ kañcid kālam mahāmatiḥ brāhmaṇān tarpayāmāsa pitṝn uddiśya satkṛtān akṛta vraṇa saṃyuktaḥ mudā paramayā yutaḥ tatas teṣām anumate munīnām bhāvitātmanām viśvāmitra bharadvāja mārkaṇḍeya ādibhiḥ tathā vājimedham tatas rājan ājahāra mahā kratum viśvāmitraḥ abhavat hotā rāmasya vidita ātmanaḥ bharadvāja agniveśya ādyāḥ veda vedāṅga pāragāḥ tatas prabhṛti rāja indra pūjayāmāsa śāstrataḥ yayuḥ yathāgatam sarve munayaḥ śaṃsita vratāḥ iti eṣa jāmadagnyasya prādurbhāvaḥ udāhṛtaḥ kṛcchram mahāntam āsādya śāka mūla phala aśanaḥ iti tat vacanam śrutvā mātā tava manasvinī vratasya asya prabhāveṇa sarva lokeṣu viśrutaḥ etat ca sarvam ākhyātam vratānām uttamam tava karmaṇā anena viprarṣe kṛtārthaḥ asmi na saṃśayaḥ iti uktaḥ tena rājñā tu tathā iti uktvā mahā muniḥ sa dīkṣitaḥ vasiṣṭhena sagaraḥ rāja sattamaḥ sa kadācid mahīpālaḥ kṛta kautukamaṅgalaḥ vijitya nṛpatīn sarvān kṛtvā ca sva padānugān babhūva haihayaiḥ vīraiḥ saṃgrāmaḥ roma harṣaṇaḥ nijaghāna mahā bāhuḥ saṃkruddhaḥ kosala īśvaraḥ tasmāt asmān mahābhāga paritrātum tvam arhasi iti teṣām vacaḥ śrutvā vasiṣṭhaḥ bhagavān ṛṣiḥ dṛṣṭvā tu atapyat bhagavān sarva bhūta anukaṃpakaḥ arddha muṇḍān śakān cakre palhavān śmaśru dhāriṇaḥ athā anujñāya sagaraḥ vasiṣṭham ṛṣi sattamam tatas sā nagarī sarvā kṛta kautukamaṅgalā samucchrita dhvaja śatā patākābhiḥ aṃlakṛtā paura jānapadaiḥ tdṛṣṭaiḥ sarvatas samalaṅkṛtā tatas prakṛtayaḥ sarve tathā antar pura vāsinaḥ saṃstūyamānaḥ su bhṛśam sūta māgadha vandibhiḥ evam gacchati kāle ca vasiṣṭhaḥ bhagavān ṛṣiḥ diṣṭyā ca vijitāḥ sarve samagra bala vāhanāḥ vasiṣṭe tu gate rājā sagaraḥ prīta mānasaḥ bubhuje viṣayān ramyān yathākāmam yathāsukham sapta dvīpavatīm samyak sākṣāt dharmaḥ iva aparaḥ babhūva nṛpa śārdūle tasmin rājyāni śāsati ittham pālayataḥ pṛthvīm sagarasya mahīpateḥ sa yat vakṣyati tat sarvam kariṣye na atra saṃśayaḥ haṃsa sārasa cakrāhva kāraṇḍava śuka ādibhiḥ āsasāda āśrama upāntam mahā ṛṣeḥ bhāvitātmanaḥ kuśalam nanu te rājye bāhyeṣu ābhyantareṣu ca rājñām tu pravaraḥ dharmaḥ yat prajā paripālanam uvāca bhagavān aurvaḥ sa nideśam idam vacaḥ ṣaṣṭiḥ putra sahasrāṇām aparasyām ca jāyate sa praviśya purīm ramyām tdṛṣṭa puṣṭa jana āvṛtām kasyācid viṣaye rājñaḥ prabhūta dhana dhānyavān vārayāmāsa bahudhā duḥkhena mahatā anvitaḥ prakṣobham paramam jagmuḥ deva asura mahā uragāḥ tatra gatvā yathānyāyam devāḥ śarva purogamāḥ tasmāt yuṣmad hita arthāya yat vakṣyāmi sura uttamāḥ tvam kila akhila lokānām sthiti sahāra kāraṇaḥ kapilena evam uktāḥ te devāḥ sarve sa vāsavāḥ etasmin antare rājā sagaraḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ putrān sarvān samāhūya saṃdideśa mahayaśāḥ tatas ayodhyām samāsādya ṛṣibhiḥ parivāritām dṛṣṭvā praṇamya pitaram tasmai sarvam nyavedayan vasudhā vicitā asmābhiḥ sa śaila vana kānanā arghya pādya ādibhiḥ samyak pūjayāmāsa śāstrataḥ nāradaḥ rāja śārdūlam idam vacanam abravīt naṣṭam mṛtam atītam ca na anuśocanti paṇḍitāḥ praśraya avanataḥ bhūtvā śanais idam uvāca ha prasīda vipra śārdūla tvām aham śaraṇam gataḥ tad aṃbhasā pāviteṣu teṣām gātra asthi bhasmasu kramāt samāpayāmāsuḥ yajamāna purassarāḥ brāhmaṇa ādyaiḥ tatas varṇaiḥ ṛtvigbhiḥ ca samanvitaḥ vārakīyā kadaṃbaiḥ ca sūta māgadha vandibhiḥ sa gatvā sarayū tīram yathāśāśtram yathāvidhi vīṇā veṇu mṛdaṅga ādi nānā vāditra niḥsvanaiḥ saṃstūyamānaḥ paritas sūta māgadha bandibhiḥ praviveśa purīm ramyām hṛṣṭa puṣṭa jana āyutam sa praviśya gṛham ramyam sarva maṇḍala maṇḍitam vasiṣṭha anumate rājā yauvarājye abhyaṣecayat yuvā eva rāja śārdūlaḥ sākṣāt dharmaḥ iva aparaḥ saṃkṣepa vistarābhyām tu kathitam pāpa nāśanam tatas prabhṛti lokeṣu sāgara ākhyām avāptavān tatas tad nilayāḥ sarve sa deva asura mānavāḥ itas tatas ca saṃjātāḥ duḥkhena mahatā anvitāḥ tad tretrasya prabhāveṇa prītyā bhūta gaṇaiḥ saha kṣetrāṇām uttamam kṣetram sarva tīrtha niketanam japa homa ratāḥ śāntāḥ nipatāḥ brahmacāriṇaḥ vanam jagāma medhāvī tapase dhṛta mānasaḥ pālayāmāsa dharmeṇa vijitya sakalān arīn sarva dharma artha kuśalaḥ śrīmān amita vikramaḥ tatas gaṅgām mahā rāja samārādhya prasādya ca tatas śuṣka sumitra ādyāḥ munayaḥ śaṃsita vratāḥ tatas tān āgatān dṛṣṭvā munīn bhṛgukulodvahaḥ uvāca bhṛgu śārdūlaḥ smita pūrvam idam vacaḥ svāgatam vaḥ mahābhāgāḥ yūyam sarve samāgatāḥ aham muni gaṇaiḥ sārddham āgataḥ tvad didṛkṣayā gṛhītvā āropayāmāsa krodha saṃrakta locanaḥ paśyatām sarva bhūtānām jyā ghoṣam akarot tadā prakṣobham paramam jagmuḥ deva asura mahā uragāḥ saṃdhita astram bhṛgu śreṣṭham krodha saṃrakta locanam viṣādam agamat tīvram yamam dṛṣṭvā iva pātakī tatas sva rūpam āsthāya sarva ābharaṇa bhūṣitaḥ iti tasya vacaḥ śrutvā varuṇaḥ yādasām patiḥ bhrāmayitvā ati vegena cikṣepa lavaṇārṇave tīrtham śurpārakam nāma sarva pāpa vimocanam tatas teṣām bhṛgu śreṣṭhaḥ munīnām bhāvitātmanām tatas te munayaḥ sarve harṣeṇa tatas prabhṛti lokeṣu sāgara ākhyām avāptavān purukutsam aṃbarīṣam mucukundam ca viśrutam putraḥ bhīmarathasya api jātaḥ dhīmān prajeśvaraḥ devān atarpayat yajñaiḥ pitṝn śrāddhaiḥ tathā eva ca dharmeṇa ca prajāḥ sarvāḥ yathāvat anurañjayat bhajamānaḥ bhajiḥ ddivyaḥ vṛṣṇiḥ devāvṛdhaḥ andhakaḥ na ca satrājitaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ maṇi ratnam syamantakam sudāntaḥ ca adhidāntaḥ ca kanakaḥ kanakodbhavaḥ devabāhoḥ sutaḥ vidvān jajñe kaṃbalabarhiṣaḥ āgāvahaḥ mahātmā ca vṛkadevyāḥ majāyata ayam sa garbhāḥ devakyāḥ mama kleśān hariṣyati kāśyām tu pañca tanayān lebhe sāṃbāt tarasvinaḥ satya prakṛtayaḥ devāḥ pañca vīrāḥ prakīrttitāḥ deva asura āhava hatāḥ asurāḥ ye mahā balāḥ samutpannam kula śatam yādavānām mahātmanām krīḍate bhagavān loke bālaḥ krīḍanakeḥ iva śiśunāgāḥ daśa eva ete rājānaḥ kṣatrabandhavaḥ saptatriṃśat śatam pūrṇam tebhyaḥ śuṅgaḥ gamiṣyati kaṇvāyanāḥ tu catvāraḥ catvāriṃśat ca pañca ca teṣu chinneṣu kālena tatas kilakilaḥ nṛpaḥ etat varṣa sahasram tu jñeyam pañcāśat uttaram andhra aṃśe sa caturviṃśe bhaviṣyanti śatam samāḥ divyam varṣa sahasram tu tat saṃdhyāṃśe hi kīrtite samāptaḥ ca
