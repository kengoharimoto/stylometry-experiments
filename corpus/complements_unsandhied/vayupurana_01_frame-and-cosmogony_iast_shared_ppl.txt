prabhuḥ sarvāḥ tāḥ ca mahābhāgāḥ sarvāḥ kamala locanāḥ yoga patnyaḥ ca tāḥ sarvāḥ sarvāḥ tāḥ yoga mātaraḥ śraddhā lakṣmī dhṝtiḥ tuṣṭiḥ puṣṭiḥ medhā kriyā tathā buddhiḥ llajjā vapuḥ śāntiḥ siddhiḥ kīrttiḥ trayodaśī patnī arthe pratijagrāha dharmaḥ dākṣāyaṇīḥ prabhuḥ dvārāṇi etāni ca eva asya vihitāni svayambhuvā tābhyaḥ śiṣṭāḥ yavīyasyaḥ ekādaśa su locanāḥ khyātiḥ satī atha saṃbhūtiḥ smṛtiḥ prītiḥ kṣamā tathā ca ūrjā svāhā svadhā tathā rudraḥ bhṛguḥ marīciḥ ca aṅgirāḥ pulahaḥ kratuḥ prītim ca eva pulastyāya kṣamām vai pulahāya ca ūrjam dadau vasiṣṭāya svāhām vai hi agnaye dadau ete sarve mahābhāgāḥ prajñāḥ su anuṣṭitāḥ sthitāḥ manvantareṣu sarveṣu yāvat ābhūtasaṃplavam śraddhā kāmam vijajñe vai darpaḥ lakṣmī sutaḥ smṛtaḥ dhṛtyāḥ tu niyamaḥ putraḥ tuṣṭayāḥ santoṣaḥ ucyate puṣṭyāḥ lābhaḥ sutaḥ ca api medhā putraḥ śrutaḥ tathā kriyāyāḥ tu nayaḥ proktaḥ daṇḍaḥ samayaḥ eva ca lajjāyāḥ vinayaḥ putraḥ vyavasāyaḥ vapuḥ sutaḥ kṣemaḥ śānti sutaḥ ca api sukham siddheḥ vyajāyata yaśaḥ kīrtteḥ sutaḥ ca api iti ete dharma sūnavaḥ kāmasya harṣaḥ putraḥ vai devyāḥ ratyāḥ vyajāyata jajñe hiṃsā tu adharmāt hai nikṛtiḥ ca anṛtau ubhau bhayāt jajñe atha sā māyā mṛtyum bhūta apahāriṇam vedanāyāḥ tatas ca api duḥkham jajñe atha rauravāt mṛtyayoḥ vyādhi jvarā śokāḥ krodhaḥ asūyā ca jajñire duḥkha antarā smṛtāḥ hi ete sarve ca adharma lakṣaṇāḥ teṣām bhāryā asti putraḥ vā te sarve nidhanāḥ smṛtāḥ iti eṣa tāmasaḥ sargaḥ jajñe dharma niyāmakaḥ saḥ abhidhyāya satīm bhāryān nirmame hi ātma sambhavām na adhikān na ca hīnān tān mānasān ātmanaḥ samān sahasram hi sahasrāṇām asṛjat kṛmi vāsasā tulyāḥ ca eva ātmanaḥ sarve rūpa tejaḥ bala śrutaiḥ piṅgalān sat niṣaṅgān ca sa kaparddān vilohitān vivāsān hari keśān ca dṛṣṭighnān ca kapālinaḥ bahu rūpān virūpān ca viśva rūpān ca rūpiṇaḥ sahasra śata bāhūn ca divyān bhauma antarikṣa gān sthūla śīrṣān aṣṭa daṃṣṭrān udvijihvān tri locanān anna ādān cavaḥ ājyapān somapān tathā medapām svātikāyān ca śitikaṇṭa ugramanyavaḥ sa upāsaṅga talatrān ca dhanvinaḥ hi upavarmiṇaḥ āsīnān dhāvataḥ ca eva jṛmbhinaḥ ca eva dhiṣṭitān buddhān buddhatamān ca eva brahmiṣṭān śubha darśanān sahasrākṣān saṃrvāḥ ca atha kṣapācarān adṛśyān sarva bhūtānām mahā yogān mahā ojasaḥ rūdataḥ dravataḥ ca eva evaṃyuktān sahasraśas apātayāmān asṛjat rudra rūpān sura uttamān brahmā dṛṣṭvā abravīt etān mā asrākṣīḥ īdṛśīḥ prajāḥ sraṣṭavyāḥ na ātmanaḥ tulyāḥ prajāḥ na eva adhikāḥ tvayā anyāḥ sṛja tvam bhadra ante sthitaḥ han tvam sṛja prajāḥ ete ye vai mayā mṛṣṭāḥ virūpāḥ nīla lehitāḥ jñānam vairāgyam aiśvaryam tapaḥ sacatyam kṣamā dhṛtiḥ sraṣṭṛ tvam ātma sambodhaḥ tu adhiṣṭātṛ tvam eva ca atha yāni daśa etāni śaṅkare sarvān devān ṛṣīn ca eva sametān asuraiḥ saha jñānena ca munīn sarvān yogāt bhūtāni sarvaśas
