sadasyāḥ mahā ojasaḥ atha teṣām purāṇasya śuśrūṣā samapadyata dṛṣṭvā tam āti viśvastam vidvāṃsam lomaharṣaṇam tasmin satre gṛhapatiḥ sarva śāstra viśāradaḥ itihāsa purāṇa artham vyāsaḥ samyak upāsitaḥ dudoha vai matim tasya tvam purāṇa āśrayām kathām śuśrūṣā asti mahābuddhe tat śrāvayitum arhasi sarve hi ime mahātmānaḥ nānā gotrāḥ samāgatāḥ svān svān vaṃśān purāṇaiḥ tu śrṛṇuyuḥ brahma vādinaḥ purāṇa artham purāṇa jñaiḥ satya vrata parāyaṇaiḥ svadharmaḥ eṣa sūtasya sadbhiḥ dṛṣṭaḥ purātanaiḥ vaṃśānām dhāraṇam kāryam śrutānām ca mahātmanām itihāsa purāṇeṣu diṣṭāḥ ye brahma vādibhiḥ na hi vedeṣu adhikāraḥ kaśvid sūtasya dṛśyate kasmāt samyak na vibrūyām purāṇam ṛṣi pūjitam araṇī iva hutāśasya nimittam yasya janmanaḥ tasyām jātaḥ mahā yogī vyāsaḥ veda vidām varaḥ puruṣāya purāṇāya bhṛgu vākya pravarttine mānuṣa chadma rūpāya viṣṇave prabhaviṣṇave jāta mātraṃ ca yam vedaḥ upatasthe sa saṅgrahaḥ matim manthānam āvidhya yena asau śruti sāgarāt prakāśam janitaḥ loke mahābhārata candramāḥ tasmāt aham upaśrutya purāṇam brahma vādinaḥ sarvajñāt sarva vedeṣu pūjitāt dīpta tejasaḥ dvīpānām sa samudrāṇām parvatānām ca kīrttanam liṅga udbhava kathā puṇyā sarva pāpa praṇāśinī meghaḥ hi ekārṇavam vāyuḥ tathā rātriḥ mahātmanaḥ kīrttanam śravaṇam ca asya dhāraṇam ca viśeṣataḥ na ced purāṇam saṃvidyāt na eva sa syāt vicakṣaṇaḥ yat tat smṛtam kāraṇam aprameyam brahma pradhānam prakṛti prasūtiḥ prakṛti avasthā eṣu ca kāraṇeṣu sthitiḥ yā ca punar pravṛttiḥ ṛṣayaḥ tu tatas śrutvā naimiṣa araṇya vāsinaḥ bhavān vai vaṃśa kuśalaḥ vyāsāt pratyakṣa darśavān pṛṣṭām ca etām kathām divyām ślakṣṇām pāpa praṇāśinīm kramaṇaḥ kramaṇīya tvāt varṇakasya abhipālanāt aram iti eṣa nipātaḥ kavibhiḥ smṛtaḥ dṛṣṭvā punar prajāḥ ca api eka anasā daitya hanī māyā mahiṣa marddinī tamaḥ mātrā vṛtaḥ brahmā tadā prabhṛti duḥkhitaḥ sahasrāṇām ātmanā upama niścitāḥ tat sarvam śrotum icchāmi yogam māheśvaram prabho ādityaiḥ vasubhiḥ sādhyaiḥ aśvibhyām ca eva sarvaśas prāṇāyāmaḥ tathā dhyānam pratyāhāraḥ atha dhāraṇā teṣām krama viśeṣeṇa lakṣaṇam kāraṇam tathā pravakṣyāmi tathā tattvam yathā rudreṇa bhāṣitam prāṇāyāma gatiśvā api prāṇasya āyāmaḥ ucyate prāṇānām ca nirodhaḥ tu sa prāṇāyāma saṃjñitaḥ mandaḥ dvādaśa mātraḥ tu udvātāḥ dvādaśa smṛtāḥ madhyamaḥ ca dvis udvātaḥ caturviṃśati mātrikaḥ sveda kampa viṣādānām jananaḥ hi uttamaḥ smṛtaḥ siṃhaḥ vā kuñjaraḥ vā api tathā anyaḥ vā mṛgaḥ vane kāla antara vaśāt yogāt gamyate parimarddanāt abhayāya manuṣyāṇām mṛgebhyaḥ saṃpravarttate prāṇāyāmena yuktasya viprasya niyata ātmanaḥ sarve doṣāḥ praṇaśyanti sattvasthaḥ ca eva jāyate tapāṃsi
