yojanāt iha praṇaṣṭā hriyamāṇā vai muṣṭibyām sikatā yathā agrasat bhūri yuga balāt grāmya āraṇyāḥ caturdaśa phalam gṛhṇanti puṣpaiḥ ca puṣvam patraiḥ ca yāḥ punar brahmā svayambhūḥ bhagavān jñātvā tāsām manīṣitam yuktam pratyakṣa dṛṣṭena darśanena vicārya ca grastāḥ pṛthivyāḥ oṣadhyaḥ jñātvā pratyaduhat punar maryādāḥ sthāpayāmāsa yathā ārabdhāḥ parasparam ye vai parigṛhītāraḥ tāsām āsan vidha ātmakāḥ teṣām karmāṇi dharmāḥ ca brahmā tu vyadadhāt prabhuḥ deva asura pitṛ tvaiḥ ca paśu pakṣi sarīsṛpaiḥ evaṃbhūteṣu lokeṣu brahmaṇā loka kartṛṇā satīm bhavāya prāyacchat khyātim ca bhṛgave tathā marīcaye ca sambhūtim smṛtim āṅgirase dadau prajāḥ sṛja iti vyādiṣṭaḥ brahmaṇā nīlalohitaḥ ete devāḥ bhaviṣyanti rūdrāḥ nāma mahā balāḥ pṛthivyām antārīkṣe ca rudra nāmnā pratiśrutāḥ śata rudra samāmnātāḥ bhaviṣyanti iha yajñiyāḥ taiḥ sārddham ījyamānāḥ te sthāsyanti iha yuga kṣayāt pratyuvāca tadā bhīmam hṛṣyamāṇaḥ prajāpatiḥ brahmaṇā samanujñā te sadā sarvam abhūt kila tatas prabhṛti deveśaḥ na prāsūyata vai prajāḥ ūrddhvaretāḥ sthitaḥ sthāṇuḥ yāvat ābhūtasaṃplavam yasmāt ca uktam sthitaḥ asmi iti tatas sthāṇuḥ iti smṛtaḥ yogam tapaḥ ca satyam ca dharmam ca api mahā mune iti etat
