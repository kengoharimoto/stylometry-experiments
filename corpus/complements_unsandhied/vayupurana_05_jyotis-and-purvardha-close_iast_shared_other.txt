tisraḥ koṭyaḥ tu vistāraḥ saṃkhyātaḥ sa caturdiśam vistārāt triguṇam ca eva pṛthivī antasya maṇḍalam tisraḥ koṭyaḥ tu vistīrṇāḥ viṣuvant yā api sā smṛtā abhijit pūrvatas svātiḥ nāgavīthī udayāḥ trayaḥ rathakṛcchraḥ ca taporyaḥ ca eva tau ubhau ṛṣiḥ atriḥ vasiṣṭhaḥ ca takṣakaḥ rambhaḥ eva ca amṛtena tṛptiḥ tu arddha māsam surāṇām māsa arddha tṛptiḥ svadhayā pitṝṇām vīthi āśrayāṇi carati nakṣatrāṇi tathā śaśī yuktaḥ hayaiḥ piśaṅgaiḥ tu aṣṭābhiḥ vāta raṃhasaiḥ śānaiścaram tathā sthānam devaḥ ca eva śanaiścaraḥ vṛttam brahma purogāṇām kasmin kāle mahā dyute etat ākhyāhi naḥ samyak yathā vṛttam tapodhana yathā śrutam mayā pūrvam vāyunā jagadāyunā kathyamānam dvijaśreṣṭāḥ satre varṣa sahasrake nīla tā yena kaṇṭhasya devadevasya śūlinaḥ tat aham kīrttayiṣyāmi śrṛṇudhvam śaṃsita vratāḥ uttare śailarājasya sarāṃsi saritaḥ hradāḥ puṇya udyāneṣu tīrtheṣu devatāyataneṣu ca giri śrṛṅgeṣu tuṅgeṣu gahvara upavaneṣu ca ṛk yajuḥ sāma vedaiḥ ca nṛtya gīta arccana ādibhiḥ oṅkāreṇa namaskāraiḥ arccayanti sadā śivam devatāḥ niyata ātmānaḥ sarve tiṣṭhanti tām kathām namaḥ dvādaśa netrāya ṣaṇmukhāya namaḥ astu te yena prodbhūta mātreṇa kṛta kṛṣṇaḥ janārdanaḥ daṇḍa kṛṣṇa ajina dharaḥ kapālī ghora rūpadhṛk tena sarvam idam sṛṣṭam hetu mātrāḥ vayantu iha dvi akṣaram kuhu mātram tu evam kālaḥ tu sa smṛtaḥ parvaṇaḥ parva kālaḥ tu tulyaḥ vai tu vaṣaṭkriyā sthāneṣu pācyamānāḥ ca yāta āyāteṣu teṣu vai paśca ādi sthāvara anteṣu bhūtānām teṣu karmasu dharma saṃsthāpana arthāya teṣām śāstre tapaḥ mayā ekam ādhvaryavam pūrvam āsīt dvaidham punar tatas adhārmikāḥ tu anācārāḥ tīkṣṇa kopa alpa tejasaḥ bhrūṇa hatyāḥ prajāḥ tatra prajāḥ evam pravarttate tadā sūkṣme mahā udarkaḥ durlabhaḥ bhoginām tathā maṇḍalāni bhavanti atra deśeṣu nagareṣu ca anaṣṭa cetanāḥ puṃsaḥ mukta keśāḥ tu cūlikāḥ duḥkha pracārataḥ alpa āyuḥ deśa utsādaḥ sa roga tā mānuṣāṇām paśūnām ca pakṣiṇām sthāvaraiḥ saha teṣām āyuḥ parikrāntam yuga dharmeṣu sarvaśas asthitiḥ tu kalau dṛṣṭā bhūtānām āyuṣaḥ tu vai parama āyuḥ śatantu etat manuṣyāṇām kalau smṛtam sāṃsiddikāni atha etāni su pratīkāni tasya vai adviṣeṇa hi arūpaḥ ca vītahavya sumedhasaḥ ekādaśa tathā rudrān vāyuputram viśeṣataḥ sarve pāpa vinirmuktāḥ gatāḥ te sūrya maṇḍalam tadā prabhṛti tat tīrtham jātam pātaka nāśanam ṛk yajuḥ sāma atharva rūpiṇe brahmaṇe namaḥ
