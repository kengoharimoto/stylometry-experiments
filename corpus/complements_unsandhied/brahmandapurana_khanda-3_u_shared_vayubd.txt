vivarjanam vairāgya kāraṇam hi ete prakṛtīnām layasya ca aṣṭau prakṛtayaḥ jñeyāḥ pūrva uktāḥ vai yathākramam avyakta ādyāḥ tu vijñeyāḥ bhūta antāḥ prakṛteḥ bhavāḥ varṇa āśrama ācāra yuktaḥ śiṣṭaḥ
