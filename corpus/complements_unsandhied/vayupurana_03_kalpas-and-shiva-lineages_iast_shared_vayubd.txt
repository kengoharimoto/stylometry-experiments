kṛtaḥ śālmalau tu vapuṣmantam rājānam abhiṣiktavān jyotiṣmantam kuśadvīpe rājānam kṛtavān prabhuḥ dyutimantam ca rājānam kraujcadvīpe samādiśat śākadvīpa īśvaram ca api havyan cakre priyavrataḥ caturtham lavaṇam smṛtam pañcamam dhṛtimat varṣam ṣaṣṭham varṣam prabhākaram teṣām dvīpāḥ kuśadvīpe tad sanāmānaḥ eva tu āśrama ācāra yuktābhiḥ prajābhiḥ samalaṃkṛtāḥ śālmalasya īśvarāḥ sapta putrāḥ
