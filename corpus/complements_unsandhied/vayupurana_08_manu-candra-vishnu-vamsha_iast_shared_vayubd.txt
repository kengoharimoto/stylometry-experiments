adhārmikāḥ ca te atyartham pāṣaṇḍāḥ ca eva sarvaśas pranaṣṭe nṛpa śabde ca sandhyā āśliṣṭe kalau yuge kiṃñcid śiṣṭāḥ prajāḥ tāḥ vai dharmme naṣṭe aparigrahāḥ asādhanāḥ hata āśvāsāḥ vyādhi śokena pīḍitāḥ anāvṛṣṭi hatāḥ ca eva paraspara vadhena ca anāthāḥ hi paritrastāḥ vārttām utsṛjya duḥkhitāḥ tyaktvā purāṇi grāmān ca bhaviṣyanti vanaukasaḥ evam nṛpeṣu naṣṭeṣu prajāḥ tyaktvā gṛhāṇi tu naṣṭe snehe durāpannāḥ bhraṣṭa snehāḥ suhṛd janāḥ aṅgān kaliṅgān vaṅgān ca kāśmīrān kāśi kośalān ṛṣi kānta giri droṇīḥ saṃśrayiṣyanti mānavāḥ kṛtsnam himavataḥ pṛṣṭham kūlam ca lavaṇāmbhasaḥ mṛgaiḥ mīnaiḥ vihaṅgaiḥ ca śvāpadaiḥ takṣubhiḥ tathā madhu śāka phalaiḥ mūlaiḥ varttayiṣyanti mānavāḥ cīram parṇam ca vividham valkalāni ajināni ca bīja annāni tathā kāṣṭha śaṅkubhiḥ aja eḍakam khara uṣṭram ca pālayiṣyanti yatnataḥ nadīḥ vatsyanti toya arthe kūlam āśritya mānavāḥ bahu manyāḥ prajā hīnāḥ śauca ācāra vivarjitāḥ hīnāt hīnān tathā dharmmān prajāḥ samanuvarttate āyuḥ tadā trayoviṃśam na kaścid ativarttate durbalāḥ viṣaya glānāḥ jarayā saṃpariplutāḥ patra mūla phala āhārāḥ cīra kṛṣṇa ajina ambarāḥ etad kālam anuprāptāḥ prajāḥ kali yuga antake niḥśeṣāḥ tu bhaviṣyanti sārddham kali yugena tu sa sandhyāṃśe tu niḥśeṣe kṛtam vai pratipatsyate yadā candraḥ ca sūryaḥ ca tathā tiṣya bṛhaspatī eṣa vaṃśa kramaḥ kṛtsnam kīrtitaḥ vaḥ yathākramam mahādeva abhiṣekāt tu janma yāvat parikṣitaḥ pramāṇam vai tathā ca uktam mahāpadma antaram ca yat antaram tat śatāni aṣṭau ṣaṭtriṃśat ca samāḥ smṛtāḥ etad kāla antaram bhāvyāḥ andhra antāḥ ye prakīrttitāḥ bhaviṣyaiḥ tatra saṅkhyātāḥ purāṇa jñaiḥ śruta ṛṣibhiḥ saptarṣayaḥ tadā prāhuḥ pratīpe rājñi vai śatam saptaviṃśaiḥ śataiḥ bhāvyāḥ andhrāṇām te tvayā punar saptarṣayaḥ tu tiṣṭhanti paryāyeṇa śatam śatam saptarṣīṇām yugam hi etat divyayā saṅkhyayā smṛtam sā sā divyā smṛtā ṣaṣṭiḥ divyā ahnāḥ ca eva saptabhiḥ saptarṣīṇām tu ye pūrvāḥ dṛśyante uttarā diśi tena saptarṣayaḥ yuktāḥ jñeyāḥ vyomni śatam samāḥ nakṣatrāṇām ṛṣīṇām ca yogasya etat nidarśanam saptarṣayaḥ maghā yuktāḥ kāle pārikṣite śatam imāḥ tadā tu prakṛtiḥ vyāpatsyanti prajāḥ bhṛśam anṛta upahatāḥ sarvāḥ dharmataḥ kāmataḥ arthataḥ śrauta smārtte praśithile dharme varṇa āśrame tadā śaṅkaram durbala ātmānaḥ pratipatsyanti mohitāḥ saṃsaktāḥ ca bhaviṣyanti śūdrāḥ sārddham dvijātibhiḥ brāhmaṇāḥ śūdra yaṣṭāraḥ śūdrāḥ vai mantra yonayaḥ lavam lavam bhrasyamānāḥ prajāḥ sarvāḥ krameṇa tu kṣayam eva gamiṣyanti kṣīṇa śeṣāḥ yuga kṣaye yasmin kṛṣṇaḥ divam yātaḥ tasmin eva tadā dine sahasrāṇām śatāni iha trīṇi mānuṣa saṅkhyayā ṣaṣṭim ca eva sahasrāṇi varṣāṇām ucyate kaliḥ niḥśeṣe ca tadā tasmin kṛtam vai pratipatsyate ailaḥ ikṣvāku vaṃśaḥ ca saha bhedaiḥ prakīrttitau ikṣvākoḥ tu smṛtaḥ kṣatraḥ sumitrā antam vivasvataḥ ailam kṣatram kṣemakāntam soma vaṃśa vidaḥ viduḥ ete vivasvataḥ putrāḥ kīrttitāḥ kīrtti varddhanāḥ yuge yuge mahātmānaḥ samatītāḥ sahasraśas bahu tvāt nāmadheyānām parisaṃkhyā kule kule eva āyāntu yuga ākhyāyām yatas kṣatram prapatsyate devāpiḥ pauravaḥ rājā ikṣvākoḥ ca eva yaḥ mataḥ na ca viṃśe yuge soma vaṃśasya ādiḥ bhaviṣyati evam sarvatra vijñeyam santāna arthe tu lakṣaṇam gotrāṇām kṣatriyāṇām ca bhaviṣyete pravarttakau dvāpara aṃśe na tiṣṭhanti kṣatriyāḥ ṛṣibhiḥ saha evam eva tu sarveṣu tiṣṭhanti iha antareṣu vai saptarṣayaḥ nṛpaiḥ sārddham santāna artham yuge yuge manvantarāṇām saptānām santānāḥ ca śrutāḥ ca te yathā pravṛttiḥ teṣām vai pravṛttānām tathā kṣayaḥ saptarṣayaḥ viduḥ teṣām dīrgha āyuḥ tvā akṣayantu te utpadyamānāḥ tretāyām kṣīyamāṇe kalau punar anuyānti yuga ākhyām tu yāvat manvantara kṣayaḥ jāmadagnyena rāmeṇa kṣatre niravaśeṣite kṛte vaṃśa kulāḥ sarvāḥ kṣatriyaiḥ vasudhādhipaiḥ dvi vaṃśa karaṇāḥ ca eva kīrttayiṣye nibodhata rājānaḥ śreṇi baddhāḥ tu tathā anye kṣatriyāḥ nṛpāḥ aila vaṃśasya ye khyātāḥ tathā eva aikṣvākavāḥ nṛpāḥ teṣām ekaśatam pūrṇam kulānām abhiṣekinām tāvat eva tu bhojānām vistāraḥ dviguṇaḥ smṛtaḥ bhajate triṃśakam kṣatram caturdhā tat yathādiśam teṣu atītāḥ samānāḥ ye bruvataḥ tān nibodhata śatam vai prativindhyānām śatam nāgāḥ śatam hayāḥ dhṛtarāṣṭrāḥ ca ekaśatam aśītiḥ janamejayāḥ śatam ca brahmadattānām śīriṇām vīriṇām śatam tatas śatam purā umānām śveta kāśa kuśa ādayaḥ tatas apare sahasram vai ye atītāḥ śata bindavaḥ ījire ca aśvamedhaiḥ te sarve niyuta dakṣiṇaiḥ teṣām nibodhata utpannāḥ loke santatayaḥ smṛtāḥ aṣṭāviṃśat yuga ākhyāḥ tu gatāḥ vaivasvate antare etāḥ rājarṣibhiḥ sārddham śiṣṭāḥ yāḥ tāḥ nibodhata catvāriṃśat ca ye ca eva bhaviṣyāḥ saha rājabhiḥ yuga ākhyānām viśiṣṭāḥ tu tatas vaivasvata kṣaye etat vaḥ kathitam sarvvam samāsa vyāsa yogataḥ kīrttitāḥ ca amitāḥ ye me lokān vai dhārayanti uta āyuḥ kīrttim dhanam putrān svargam ca ānantyam aśnute iti eṣa vaḥ pādaḥ tṛtīyaḥ kathitaḥ dvijāḥ iti śrī mahāpurāṇe vāyuprokte anuṣaṅgapādaḥ nāma saptatriṃśaḥ adhyāyaḥ iti anuṣaṅga pādaḥ
