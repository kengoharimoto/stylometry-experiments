prākṛtaiḥ tatra hi āvṛtam prati saptabhiḥ paraspara āvṛtāḥ sarve utpannāḥ ca parasparāt aṇḍasya asya samantāt tu sanniviṣṭaḥ ghana udadhiḥ samantāt yena toyena dhāryamāṇaḥ sa tiṣṭhati dhāryamāṇam samantāt tu tiṣṭhate ghana tejasā ayaḥ guḍa nibhaḥ vahniḥ samantāt maṇḍala ākṛtiḥ samantāt dhana vātena dhāryamāṇaḥ sa tiṣṭhati ghana vātaḥ tathā ākāśan dhārayāṇaḥ tu tiṣṭhati bhūtādiḥ ca tathā ākāśam bhūta ādyan ca api asau mahān na eka yojana sāhasram viprakṛṣṭam tamaḥ vṛtam tamasaḥ ante ca vikhyātam ākāśa ante ca bhāsvaram maryādāyām atas tasya śivasya āyatanam mahat tridaśānām agamyantu sthānam divyam iti śrutiḥ candra āditya avataptāḥ tu ye lokāḥ prathitāḥ budhaiḥ te lokāḥ iti abhihitāḥ jagataḥ ca na saṃśayaḥ sapta skandhāḥ tathā vāyoḥ sa brahmasadanāḥ dvijāḥ āpātālāt divam yāvat atra pañcavidhā gatiḥ pramāṇam etat jagataḥ eṣa saṃsāra sāgaraḥ anādyantā prayāti evam na eka jāti samudbhavāḥ pṛthivyāḥ ca agni vāyūnām mahataḥ tamasaḥ tathā kṣayaḥ vā parimāṇam vai antaḥ vā api na vidyate tasya ca uktam mayā pūrvam tasmin nāma anukīrttane arṇaveṣu ca sarveṣu divi ca eva na saṃśayaḥ anekadhā vibhakta aṅgaḥ mahā yogī maheśvaraḥ ādhāra ādheya bhāvena vikārāḥ te vikāriṇaḥ paraspara adhikāḥ ca eva praviṣṭāḥ ca parasparam yasmāt dviṣṭāḥ ca te anyonyam tasmāt sthairyam upāgatāḥ prāk āsan hi aviśeṣāḥ tu viśeṣa anyonya veśanāt pṛthivī ādyāḥ ca vāyu antāḥ paricchinnāḥ trayaḥ tu te guṇa apacaya sāreṇa paricchedaḥ viśeṣataḥ śeṣāṇām tu paricchedaḥ saukṣmyāt na iha vibhāvyate bhūtebhyaḥ paratas tebhyaḥ hi ālokaḥ paratas smṛtaḥ bhūtāni āloke ākāśe paricchinnāni sarvaśas pātre mahati pātrāṇi yathā eva antargatāni tu tathā hi āloke ākāśe bhedāḥ tu antargatāḥ matāḥ kṛtsnāni etāni catvāri anyonyasya adhikāni tu jantūnām iha saṃskāraḥ bhūteṣu antargataḥ matāḥ tasmāt parimitāḥ bhedāḥ smṛtāḥ kārya ātmakāḥ tu te kāraṇa ātmakāḥ tathā eva syuḥ bhedāḥ ye mahat ādayaḥ iti eṣa sanniveśaḥ vaḥ mayā proktaḥ vibhāgaśaḥ sapta dvīpa samudrāyāḥ yāthātathyena vai dvijāḥ vaiśvarūpam pradhānasya parimāṇa eka deśikam evam bhūta gaṇāḥ sapta sanniviṣṭāḥ parasparam etāvān sanniveśaḥ tu mayā śakyaḥ asaṅkhyeyāḥ prakṛtayaḥ tiryak ūrddhvam adhas ca yāḥ tārakā sanniveśaḥ ca yāvat divyam tu maṇjalam maryādā sanniveśaḥ tu bhūmeḥ tadanu maṇḍalam atas param pravakṣyāmi pṛthivyām vai dvijottamāḥ iti śrī mahāpurāṇe vāyuprokte bhuvanavinyāsaḥ nāma ekonapañcāśaḥ adhyāyaḥ
