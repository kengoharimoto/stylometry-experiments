śakti ādi guṇa eka rāśiḥ paraḥ parāṇām sakalāḥ na yatra kleśa ādayaḥ santi parāvara īśe saḥ īśvaraḥ vyaṣṭi samaṣṭi rūpaḥ vyakta svarūpaḥ saṃjñāyate yena tat asta doṣam śuddham param nirmalam eka rūpam svādhyāya saṃyamābhyām sa dṛśyate puruṣottamaḥ svādhyāya adhi ogam āsīta yogāt svādhyāyam āvaset svādhyāya yoga saṃpattyā paramātmā prakāśate tad īkṣaṇāya svādhyāyaḥ cakṣuḥ yogaḥ tathā param na mām sa cakṣuṣā draṣṭum brahma bhūtaḥ sa śakyate bhagavan tam aham yogam jñātum icchāmi tam vada jñāte yatra akhila ādhāram paśyeyam parameśvaram yathā keśidhvajaḥ prāha khāṇḍikyāya mahātmane katham tayoḥ ca saṃvādaḥ yoga saṃbandha kāraṇāt kṛtadhvajaḥ ca nāmnā āsīt sadā adhyātma ratiḥ nṛpaḥ keśidhvajaḥ api atīva āsīt ātma vidyā viśāradaḥ keśidhya jena khāṇḍikyaḥ sva rājyāt avaropitaḥ rājyāt nirākṛtaḥ saḥ atha durga araṇya caraḥ abhavat iyāha saḥ api su bahūni ajñān jñāna vyapāśrayaḥ tatas rājā hatām śrutvā dhanum vyāghreṇa ca ṛtvijaḥ te pi ūcuḥ na vayam vidma kaśeruḥ pṛcchatām iti na keśaruḥ na ca eva aham na ca ekaḥ sāṃpratam bhuvi sa ca āha tam vrajāmi eṣa praṣṭum ātmarīpum mune prāptaḥ eva mahā yajñaḥ yadi mām sa haniṣyati prāyaścittam aśeṣeṇa sa ced pṛṣṭaḥ vadiṣyati iti uktvā ratham āruhya kṛṣṇa ajina dharaḥ nṛpaḥ vanam jagāma yatra āste sa khāṇḍikyaḥ mahāmatiḥ tam āpatantam ālokya khāṇḍikyaḥ ripum ātmanaḥ provāca krodha tāmra akṣaḥ samāropita kārmukaḥ kṛṣṇa ajinam tvam kavacam ābadhya asmān haniṣyasi mṛgāṇām vada pṛṣṭheṣu mūḍha kṛṣṇājinam na kim yeṣām mayā tvāya ca ugrāḥ prahitāḥ śita sāyakāḥ sa tvām aham haniṣyāmi na me jīvan vimokṣyase khāṇḍikya saṃśayam praṣṭum bhavantam ahām āgataḥ na tvām hantum vicārya etat kopam bāṇam vimuñca vā mantrayāmāsa khāṇḍikyaḥ sarvaiḥ eva mahāmatiḥ tam ūcuḥ matriṇaḥ vadhyaḥ ripuḥ eṣa vaśam gataḥ khāṇḍikyaḥ ca āha tān sarvān evam etat na saṃśayaḥ sa ca ācaṣṭa yathānyāyam dvija keśidhvajāya tat vidita arthaḥ sa tena eva hi anujñātaḥ mahātmanā yāga bhūmim upāgamya cakre sarvāḥ kriyāḥ kramāt krameṇa vidhivat yāgam nītvā saḥ avabhṛtha āplutaḥ kṛtakṛtyaḥ tatas bhūtvā cintayāmāsa pārthivaḥ pūjitāḥ ca dvijāḥ sarve sadasyāḥ mānitāḥ mayā khāṇḍikyāya na dattā iti mayā vai guru dakṣiṇā khāṇḍikyaḥ api punar dṛṣṭvā tam āyāntam dhṛta āyudham bho na aham te aparādhāya prāptaḥ khāṇḍikya mā kruddhāḥ niṣpāditaḥ mayā yāgaḥ samyak tvad upadeśataḥ saḥ aham te dātum icchāmi vṛṇīṣva guru dakṣiṇām bhūyasya mantribhiḥ sārdham mantrayāmāsa pārthivaḥ guru niṣkraya kāmaḥ ayam kim mayā prārthyatām iti tam ūcuḥ matriṇaḥ rājyam aśaṃṣam prārthyatām ayam śatribhiḥ prārthyate rājya manāḥ yā asita sainikaiḥ prahasya tān āha nṛpaḥ sa khāṇḍikyaḥ mahāmatiḥ su alpa kālam mahīpālyam mādṛśaiḥ prārthyate katham parama arthaḥ katham kaḥ atra yūyam na atra vicakṣaṇāḥ uvāca kim avaśyam tvam dadāsi guru dakṣiṇām bāḍham iti eva tena uktaḥ khāṇḍikyaḥ tam athā bravīt bhavān adya ātma vijñāna parama artha vicakṣaṇaḥ na prārthitam tvayā kasmāt asmad rājyam akaṇṭakam rājya lābhāt vinā na anyat kṣatriyāṇā mati priyam rājyam etat aśeṣam te yatra gṛdhnanti apaṇḍitāḥ tatra aśaktasya me doṣaḥ na eva asti apahṛte tvayā na yācñā kṣatrabandhūnām dharmayā etat satām matam rājye gṛdhnanti avidvāṃsaḥ mamatva āhṛta cetasaḥ ahaṃmāna mahā pāna mada mattāḥ na mādṛśāḥ aham hi avidyayā kṛtyum tartu kāmaḥ karomi vai rājyam yāgān ca vividhān yogaiḥ puṇya kṣayam tathā tat idam te manaḥ diṣṭyā viveka aiśvarya tām gatam tat śrūyatām avidyāyāḥ svarūpam kula nandana pañcabhūta ātmake dehe dehī moha tamaḥ vṛtaḥ ākāśa vāyu agni jala pṛthivībhyaḥ pṛthak sthite mṛṇmayam hi yathā geham lipyate vai mṛd aṃbhasā pañja bhūta ātmakaiḥ pañcabhūta ātmakam vapuḥ āpyāyate yadi tatas puṃsaḥ bhogaḥ atra kim kṛtaḥ aneka janma sāhasrīm saṃsāra padavīm vrajan moha śramam prayātaḥ asau vāsana remu kuṇṭhitaḥ prakṣālyate yadā sā usya reṇu jñāna uṣṇa vāriṇā tadā saṃsāra pānthasya yāti moha śramaḥ śamam moha śrame śamam yāte svastha antaḥkaraṇaḥ pumān ananya atiśaya ābādham param nirvāṇam ṛcchati nirvāṇa mayaḥ eva ayam ātmā jñāna mayaḥ amalaḥ duḥkha ajñāna mayāḥ dharmāḥ prakṛteḥ te tu na ātmanaḥ jalasya na agni saṃsarga sthālī saṃgāt tathā api hi tathā ātmā prakṛteḥ saṃgāt ahaṃmāna ādi dūṣitaḥ kleśānām ca kṣaya karam yogāt anyat na vidyate vijñāta yoga śāstra arthaḥ tvam asyām nimi saṃtatau yoga svarūpam khāṇḍikya śrūyatām gadataḥ mama manaḥ eva munuṣyāṇām kāraṇam bandha mokṣayoḥ bandhāya viṣaya āsaṃgi muktyai nirviṣayam manaḥ viṣayebhyaḥ samāhṛtya vijñāna ātmā amanaḥ muniḥ vikāryam ātmanaḥ śaktyā loham ākarṣakaḥ yathā ātma prayatna sāpekṣā viśiṣṭā yā manaḥ gatiḥ yadi antarāya doṣeṇa dūṣyate ca asya mānasam svādhyāya śauca saṃtoṣa tapāṃsi niyata ātmavān ete yamāḥ saniyamāḥ pañca pañca ca kīrtitāḥ viśiṣṭa phala dāḥ kāmyāḥ niṣkāmānām vimukti dāḥ ekam bhadrāsana ādīnām samāsthāya guṇaiḥ yutaḥ prāṇāyāmaḥ sa vijñeyaḥ sa bījaḥ abījaḥ eva ca paraspareṇa abhibhavam prāṇa apānau yathā anilau kurutaḥ sat vidhānena tṛtīyaḥ saṃyamāt tayoḥ śabda ādiṣu anuraktāni nigṛhya akṣāṇi yoga vid vaśya tā paramā tena jāyata iti cala ātmanām indriyāṇām avaśyaiḥ taiḥ na yogī yoga sādhakaḥ prāṇa yāmena pavane pratyāhāreṇa ca indriye vaśīkṛte tatas kuryāt sthitam cetaḥ śubha āśraye bhūpa mūrtam amūrtam ca param ca aparam eva ca brahma ākhyā karma saṃjñā ca tathā ca eva ubhaya ātmikā karma bhāva ātmikā hi ekā a brahma bhāva ātmikā parā ubhaya ātmikā tathā evā anyā trividhā bhāva bhāvanā karma bhāvanayā ca anye deva ādyāḥ sthāvara avarāḥ kṣetrajña saṃjñitā a prāṇa vat su su alpā sā sthāvareṣu tatas adhikā pātat triṃbhyaḥ mṛgāḥ tebhyaḥ tad śaktyā paśavaḥ adhikāḥ paśubhyaḥ manujāḥ ca ati śaktyā puṃsaḥ prabhāvitāḥ hiraṇyagarbhaḥ api
